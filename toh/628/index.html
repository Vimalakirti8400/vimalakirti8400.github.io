About this text

The Noble Great Sūtra on Entering the City of Vaiśālī, known in Sanskrit as the Ārya Vaiśālī Praveśa Mahāsūtra and in Tibetan as Phags pa yangs pa'i grong khyer du 'jug pa'i mdo chen po, is a Mahāyāna sūtra that recounts events surrounding the Buddha's journey to the renowned city of Vaiśālī in the land of Vṛji. The text opens with the Buddha instructing his attendant Ānanda to travel with him to Vaiśālī, where he takes up residence in the Mango Grove of Ambapālī, a setting familiar from other canonical accounts associated with this city. As a sūtra framed in the classical "thus did I hear" narrative style, it belongs to the discourse literature of the Buddhist canon. It is catalogued as Toh 628 in the Kangyur, where it is counted among the general sūtra collections.
