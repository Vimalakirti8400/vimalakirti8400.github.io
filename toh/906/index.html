About this text

The Noble Dhāraṇī of Avalokiteśvara Hayagrīva, known in Sanskrit as the Ārya Avalokiteśvara Hayagrīva Dhāraṇī and in Tibetan by a title that opens with "Phakpa Chenrezik," the Noble Avalokiteśvara, is a short incantatory text devoted to Hayagrīva, the horse-necked wrathful form associated with the bodhisattva of compassion. After paying homage to all buddhas and bodhisattvas, it presents a long Sanskrit formula. The formula honors Avalokiteśvara as the great compassionate one who carries beings across their fears, pacifies existence, cuts through every bond, frees beings from suffering, and dispels darkness. It describes the mantra as spoken from Avalokiteśvara's own mouth and revered by sages, gods, nāgas, yakṣas, Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Maheśvara, and other powerful beings, and it culminates in the supreme heart essence of Hayagrīva of the vajra hooves. It is Toh 906 in the Kangyur, where it belongs to the class of dhāraṇī texts, the collected spells and incantations within the tantra portion of the canon.
