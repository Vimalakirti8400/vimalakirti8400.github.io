About this text

Toh 640 in the Kangyur is a dhāraṇī text known in Sanskrit as the Ārya-sarva-nīvaraṇa-viṣkambhinī-nāmāṣṭottaraśataka-dhāraṇī-mantra-sahita and in Tibetan as the Pakpa Dribpa Tamché Nampar Selwé Tsen Gyatsa Gyépa Zung Ngak Dang Chepa, rendered in English as "The Noble One Hundred and Eight Names of the Remover of All Obscurations, Together with Dhāraṇī and Mantra." The text belongs to the dhāraṇī genre of canonical literature and centers on the bodhisattva Sarva-nīvaraṇa-viṣkambhin, the Remover of All Obscurations, presenting his one hundred and eight names along with accompanying dhāraṇī and mantra formulas held to generate great merit and virtue for sentient beings who hear them. It is set as a discourse in which the Tathāgata Vairocana commends and validates the dhāraṇī-mantra, framing its recitation as a source of immense spiritual benefit. As Toh 640 in the Kangyur, it is classified among the dhāraṇī texts that form part of the broader collection of Buddha-word preserved in the Tibetan Buddhist canon.

Translation

In the language of India: Ārya-sarva-nīvaraṇa-viṣkambhinī-nāmāṣṭottaraśataka-dhāraṇī-mantra-sahita. In the language of Tibet: The Noble One Hundred and Eight Names of the Remover of All Obscurations, Together with Dhāraṇī and Mantra.

Homage to the bodhisattva, the great being, the Remover of All Obscurations.

Oṃ amṛta bindu java mahā sukha svāhā.

Then the Blessed One, the Tathāgata Vairocana, extended his right hand and, having satisfied the bodhisattva, the great being, the Remover of All Obscurations, declared: "You who possess a treasury of precious qualities — it is excellent, excellent that you have brought forth this dhāraṇī-mantra and thereby produced great bliss for sentient beings. O son of the Victors, whoever hears this dhāraṇī-mantra shall come to possess roots of virtue in the presence of Tathāgatas as numerous as the grains of sand in ten Ganges rivers. And whoever has it resound in their ears shall likewise be freed from all obscurations. If one who resides near a stūpa containing bodily relics fasts on the eighth or fifteenth day of the waxing lunar fortnight, performs the restoration ceremony, makes offerings of incense, fumigation, butter-lamps, flowers, flower garlands, ointments, and the like, circumambulates, and recites this mantra, one shall attain whatever one desires that is excellent, and shall be liberated from all sins, all sufferings, and all fears. If one makes offerings of incense and the like to the bodhisattva Remover of All Obscurations and recites the mantra, he will appear in one's dreams, bring all one's wishes to complete fulfillment, and remove all afflictions and all sufferings."

Then Vajrapuṣpa Mahāmukuṭa, the lord of the three grounds, together with his retinue, rose from his seat in the midst of that great assembly-maṇḍala, bowed to the bodhisattva, the great being, the Remover of All Obscurations, and praised him with one hundred and eight names:

Supreme lord of all sentient beings,
great in the conduct of a bodhisattva,
transcending all the stains of affliction —
to you, the dharmadhātu, I bow.

Utterly and surely freed from all obscurations,
cleansing away all the stains of affliction,
fully liberated from every fault —
to you, the faultless one, I bow.

Showing the way of the single narrow path,
unified in the approach of the one vehicle,
having attained the way of the Great Vehicle —
to you, the pure vehicle, I bow.

Possessed of great illumination, one who has attained great light,
sovereign of the world, maṇḍala of qualities,
white with radiance like the moon —
to you, the one beyond the world, I bow.

Pure and vast in the Great Vehicle,
known as the vehicle of the buddhas,
renowned throughout the vajra-maṇḍala —
to you, the great vajra, I bow.

Great hero, victorious over māra,
mighty in valor, who has vanquished the afflictions,
who overcomes all obstacles and masters death —
to you, the precious net, I bow.

Great moon, great sun,
great mountain, great lord,
the Great God (Śiva), the great protector —
to you, the greatly endowed, I bow.

Cutting through all obscurations,
clearing away all obscurations,
omniscient, great in essential qualities —
to you, the All-Remover, I bow.

One who has entered the domain of the buddhas,
who has gone into the domain of the Dharma,
sovereign of wisdom, great in gnosis —
to you, the one free of craving, I bow.

Knowing the essence of the qualities that adorn the body,
adorned with the vajra-maṇḍala,
the path of the buddhas, greatly purposeful —
to you, the pure gnosis, I bow.

Perfect in conduct and in abiding, with pure faculties,
a maker of bliss, with a subtle mind,
vast, stainless, endowed with wisdom —
to you, the pure light, I bow.

Profound and firm, who overpowers completely,
peaceful, who purifies the world —
Teacher, worthy of refuge in the three realms of existence —

Courageous and mighty — to you I bow.
One of great fortune who has vanquished the afflictions,
who shatters the maṇḍala of cyclic existence,
who is peace, who makes peace, source of bliss —
river of peace — to you I bow.

Cause of liberation, great in the Dharma tradition,
dwelling in fortunate destiny, stainless,
possessing a pure mind, knowing peace,
remover of faults — to you I bow.

Renowned far and wide at the summit,
who tames with many great mantras,
the supreme maṇḍala of the great essence —
knower of the maṇḍala — to you I bow.

Dispeller of the three sufferings, supreme among the three,
whose domain is like that of the Three Jewels,
knower of the three aspects, lord of attainment,
the three liberations — to you I bow.

Who has gained the wealth of the qualities of the bodhisattva
and has attained equanimity,
who through incalculable aeons
has gathered the great accumulations — to you I bow.

Dwelling on the grounds, who has attained the great ground,
whose fruit of the ground is complete and excellent,
arising from the true holder of the ground —
ground of gnosis — to you I bow.

Who has gained mastery in meditative absorption,
who has abandoned the three faults and dispels evil,
knowing samādhi, vast in scope,
equal in equality — to you I bow.

Great protector, greatly luminous,
who dispels the stain of great suffering,
who is pacified, who without exception makes peace,
whose qualities are boundless — to you I bow.

Equal to all buddhas, endowed with speech,
all phenomena peaceful and excellent,
of the Three Jewels, of the lineage, beyond comparison —
jewel of the infinite — to you I bow.

Supreme great vajra, stainless and foremost,
bearing the vajra weapon, free of all evil,
encompassing the whole of space as its domain,
possessing an ocean of wisdom's domain —
sharing the same domain as the Tathāgata,
a dispeller, one who purifies stains,
great wisdom, son of the Victors —
severer of craving — to you I bow.

Vajra maṇḍala, supreme among vajras,
who tames with the vajra, Vajradhara (Skt. Vajradhara),
power among those endowed with vajra power —
vajra luminance — to you I bow.

Great jewel, jewel-mind,
jewel-vajra, greatly radiant,
jewel-light, beautiful, jewel-born,
who guards the jewel teaching —
who has attained the domain of emptiness,
who teaches emptiness through gnosis,
who possesses emptiness as the domain of enjoyment —
supreme of emptiness — to you I bow.

Wisdom without equal, endowed with wisdom,
pure virtue, free of the grounds,
luminous one arising from a single Dharma,
great in the manner of transcending the world —
one who illumines the world, skilled in speech,
wholly transcending the dharmas of the world,
who has attained the quality of knowing the world,
whose maṇḍalas are adorned —
dispelling all faults without exception,
benefiting the vast multitude of ordinary beings,
the renowned holder of the ground —
omniscient one — to you I bow.

Who knows joy and delights in joy,
supreme vidyā (Skt. vidyā) of joy, foremost among all supreme ones,
endowed with conceptual thought that causes joy —
luminance of joy — to you I bow.

Who subdues great passion and is endowed with intention,
who is steadfast in the practice of all adornment,
vajra vidyā, supreme of the passionate —
great passion — to you I bow.

Supreme among buddhas and bodhisattvas,
great maṇḍala of the supreme vajra,
who abides in the manner of the Great Vehicle —
unsurpassed supreme — to you I bow.

Hero, supreme bodhisattva,
who dispels all obscurations —

Having praised with one hundred and eight names
by those of the vajra maṇḍala assembly,
the renowned teaching of the vajra
was spoken by the Blessed One, Vajrin (Skt. Vajrin).

Alas! Whoever is of the maṇḍala of great fortune and luminance,
whoever is of the Victor's maṇḍala —
if one recites individually these one hundred and eight names,
I shall turn back all faults
and shall always follow in that one's footsteps.
Rising in the morning, with purity,
for the dispelling of all obscurations —
whoever, being intelligent, performs this praise —

that one's misdeeds shall be no more.

Whoever recites these supreme one hundred and eight names
upon rising in the morning
— all who bear aversion shall come under that one's sway,
and even the obstructing spirits shall be utterly released.
One shall attain purification from misdeeds,
merit shall likewise increase,
and one shall enjoy an ocean of primordial wisdom.
The supreme and great ground shall be attained.

Whoever, having settled in firm resolve,
recites these supreme one hundred and eight names —
that one shall swiftly, without long delay,
attain the buddha ground of great abundance.

Whoever possesses a great continuum and a vast mind,
and recites these supreme names —
into the teaching of the vajra maṇḍala
that one shall swiftly enter, without long delay.

Here ends the One Hundred and Eight Names of the Great Bodhisattva Who Dispels All Obscurations, together with the dhāraṇī mantras. This is complete.
