About this text

This short text, whose title the source lines do not clearly give but which opens with verses on the wrathful vajra, belongs to the Vajrakīlaya (vajra dagger) cycle and is presented as a verse practice of wrathful dagger meditation. It begins by describing a blazing blue weapon from which a drop appears in the sky, to be visualized at the gateway of the life-force and meditated upon at the heart, while teaching that the sacred commitment is liberation through compassion rather than killing or oppression. It includes the mantra "Oṃ vajra kīli kīlaya sarva vighnān baṃ hūṃ hūṃ phaṭ" and invocations to the youthful vajra deity and awareness-holders. The colophon attributes the text to the treasure revealer Dorje Yangwang and says it was translated by the translator Jñānakumāra, though its Sanskrit title is not evident from the lines given. It is Toh 841 in the Kangyur, where it is classed among the tantras.

Translation

Translated by the translator Jñānakumāra. This is the complete text from the hand of Dorje Yangwang, the treasure revealer.

The wrathful vajra cuts through aversion.
Within a blazing blue great weapon,
a drop appears from the midst of the sky.
It appears at the gateway of the life-force,
and one should meditate on it at the gateway of the heart.
The sacred commitment of liberating through compassion
is not killing and not oppressing.
Having filled the aggregates with vajra,
meditate on consciousness as vajra.

Oṃ vajra kīli kīlaya sarva vighnān baṃ hūṃ hūṃ phaṭ.

Youthful vajra, holders of awareness,
accomplish the vajra of existence.
Deity of the vajra dagger of existence,
accomplish the wrathful deities of wisdom.
From the wisdom body of all the buddhas,
from the spontaneously present vajra basic space of phenomena,
the blazing wrathful ones, the unbearable,
may I become the very substance of that body.
With the conduct of skillful means, for the sake of beings,
you tame whomever you will through loving-kindness and compassion.
Having completed the awakened activity of the buddhas,
bestow empowerment and blessing here.

Oṃ vajra krodha hūṃ kāra hūṃ, garjja garjja hūṃ phaṭ.

The Great Wrathful One, Hūṃkāra, together with his consort Vajra Sound-Emitter. Their emanated attendants have a boar's head and a bear's head.

Oṃ vajra krodha vijaya hūṃ, hana hana hūṃ phaṭ.

The Great Wrathful One Vijaya, the Victorious, together with his consort Vajra Namsemma. Their emanated attendants have a tiger's head and a vulture's head.

Oṃ vajra krodha nīladaṇḍa hūṃ, daha daha hūṃ phaṭ.

The Great Wrathful One Blue Staff, together with his consort Vajra Cymbal. Their emanated attendants have a yak's head and a raven's head.

Oṃ vajra krodha yamāntaka hūṃ, paca paca hūṃ phaṭ.

The Great Wrathful One Yamāntaka, Slayer of Death, together with his consort Lady of the Charnel Ground. Their emanated attendants have a deer's head and an owl's head.

Oṃ vajra krodha ārya acala hūṃ, bandha bandha hūṃ phaṭ.

The Great Wrathful One Acala, the Immovable Protector, together with his consort Vajra Pounder. Their emanated attendants have a leopard's head and a crow's head.

Oṃ vajra krodha hayagrīva hūṃ, hulu hulu hūṃ phaṭ.

The Great Wrathful One Hayagrīva, Horse-Neck, together with his consort Vajra Fierce Lady. Their emanated attendants have a cat's head and a pūśud's head.

Oṃ vajra krodha aparājita hūṃ, tiṣṭha tiṣṭha hūṃ phaṭ.

The Great Wrathful One, glorious Aparājita, Unconquerable by Others, together with his consort Vajra Arrow-Bearer. Their emanated attendants have a wolf's head and a hawk's head.

Oṃ vajra krodha amṛtakuṇḍali hūṃ, chinda chinda phaṭ.

The Great Wrathful One Amṛtakuṇḍali, Swirling Nectar, together with his consort Vajra Wind-Emitter. Their emanated attendants have a lion's head and a pavang's head.

Oṃ vajra krodha trailokya vijaya hūṃ, vinda vinda hūṃ phaṭ.

The Great Wrathful One, Victor over the Three Realms, together with his consort Vajra Slayer. Their emanated attendants have a bear's head and a dhole's head.

Oṃ vajra krodha mahābala hūṃ, sphoṭa sphoṭa hūṃ phaṭ.

The Wrathful One Mahābala, the Greatly Powerful, together with his consort Vajra Mover. This is completed with their emanated attendants, with the heads of bears, mice, and so forth.

To accomplish the dagger of existence,
and to receive empowerment and accomplishment,
I pray that the wrathful deities of wisdom will come.
And after the Great Wrathful Ones have come,
show signs and omens,
and bestow the accomplishment of the dagger.

The time of the great sacred commitment has arrived.
The time of the great emanation has arrived.
The time of the great hand-image has arrived.
The time of the jackal-faced ones has arrived.
The time of the great self has arrived.
The time of your awakened activity has arrived.
The time of the great earth-lords has arrived.
The time of drawing forth miraculous power has arrived.
Generating the mind of supreme awakening,

You who guard the lineage of the Victors of the three times,
to us, the holders of awareness (vidyādharas),
the harmful and malicious enemies and obstructors,
the māras and those who cut off our way:
by the blessing of the Great Wrathful Ones,
summon them here in an instant,
and slay them in accordance with the rite of wrathful action.
Having crushed their bodies and speech to dust,
make them taste suffering.

Oṃ laṃ hūṃ laṃ. Stambhaya nān. Moha gaya bhagavān. Śikri vikri. Śrī Vajra Hūṃkāra Hūṃ Hūṃ Phaṭ Phaṭ. Vajra Kīli Kīlaya. Jaḥ Hūṃ Baṃ Hoḥ. Sarva vighnān. Oṃ Vajra Kīli Kīlaya Hūṃ Hūṃ Phaṭ Phaṭ. Vajra Hūṃkāra Hūṃ Phaṭ. Hūṃkāra Hūṃ Aḥ.

You obstructors who hinder accomplishment,
you who are malicious and bear hatred:
seize them together, strike them down, bind them utterly.
Cut them off, and let them be parted from their companions.
Pierce them to the heart and let them cry out.
Cast them down and torment them thoroughly.
Having come under my power,
let them heed the commands I give.

Hūṃ para vidhvan Hūṃ Phaṭ. Chinda chinda Hūṃ Phaṭ. Bhinda bhinda Hūṃ Phaṭ. Gṛhṇa gṛhṇa Hūṃ Phaṭ. Hana hana Hūṃ Phaṭ. Bandha bandha Hūṃ Phaṭ. Tathāya tathāya Hūṃ Phaṭ. Hasaya. Raṇa hata na Hūṃ Phaṭ. Śrīkrin ānaya Hūṃ Phaṭ. Jvālāpaya jvālāpaya Hūṃ Phaṭ. Āveśaya āveśaya Hūṃ Phaṭ. Vidhyaya bindhaya Hūṃ Phaṭ.

Assembly of servants and messengers who heed commands,
the time of your sacred commitment has arrived.
As you pledged in former times,
swiftly accomplish the wrathful activity.

Hana hana vajra. Daha daha vajra. Paca paca vajra. Matha matha vajra. Jvala jvala vajra. Suru suru vajra. Muru muru vajra. Kataṅkateya. Jaye vijayeya. Ajite aparājitaya. Māra sainyaṃ pramardanāya Hūṃ Phaṭ.

Whoever sinful one transgresses
the secret command of the Vajra,
by the Great Wrathful One of mighty power
let their skull be split into a hundred pieces.
Listen, you gathering of obstructors and misleading spirits:
do not transgress my command.

Hūṃ mama vaśi kuru. Mathama. Nyakara. Inan dasti. Mama karma śrī kraṅ kareya.

Whether you be a god or even a māra,
malicious and filled with hatred,
you who create obstacles for me,
make them powerless, without strength or miraculous power.

Sarva ānaya Hūṃ Phaṭ. Tadthāya tadthāya Hūṃ Phaṭ.

Pound them with the blazing vajra pestle!
Those sinful ones who transgress the command,
having burned their hearts with the fire of the vajra,
let their bodies and speech be ground to fine dust.
In the pit of the blazing wrathful goddess,
having struck them with the vajra hammer,
slay them, even if they are gods.

Khathama, khathama, khathama. Hūṃ hūṃ hūṃ. Phaṭ phaṭ phaṭ. Vajra Yakṣa Krodha khāhi, khāhi, khahi. Hi hi hi. Ha ha ha. Hūṃ hūṃ hūṃ. Phaṭ phaṭ phaṭ.

This is the truth of the nature of reality. It is blessed by the secret mantra. It is the power of the buddhas. This is for that very investigation, when the time for abhicāra, wrathful rites, has arrived.

Recite the Kīli Kīlaya twenty-one times. One hundred and eight recitations. Within the boundary of white and black,

Translator's notes

The text breaks off mid-sentence at the end; I have left it unfinished. The colophon at the start belongs to a preceding text and was kept as it appears. Several consort and attendant names are uncertain transliterations of Tibetan renderings: "Namsemma" (rnam snyems ma) and "pūśud" (pu shud), and the animal "pavang" (pha wang) and "dhole" (dred) are rendered by sound or by best guess. The ordering of the third and fourth lines of the dedication verse about "weapon" imagery follows the Tibetan literally. "Hand-image" (phyag brnyan) is rendered literally; its precise ritual sense here is uncertain. The mantra syllables are given in reconstructed Sanskrit, and several are corrupt or unclear in the Tibetan transcription (for example "mathama," "nyakara," "inan dasti," "khathama"), so they are rendered as transliterated and not translated. The final rubric, beginning "This is for that very investigation..." (ཨ་བྷི་ཙཱརྱའི་དུས་ལ་བབ་པའི་བརྣག་པ་དེ་ཁོ་ན་ལའོ), is obscure and rendered tentatively. The passage breaks off mid-sentence at "the boundary of white and black," which presumably continues in the next section. "Hundred and eight" reconstructs the abbreviated bcwa brgya d.
