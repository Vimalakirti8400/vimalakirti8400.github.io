About this text

Toh 517 in the Kangyur is a dhāraṇī text known in Sanskrit as the Ārya Vimala nāma dhāraṇī and in Tibetan as "The Stainless One" (Phags pa dri ma med pa zhes bya ba'i gzungs), belonging to the broader collection of noble dhāraṇīs. The text records a teaching delivered by the Buddha at Vulture Peak Mountain near Rājagṛha, addressed to the bodhisattva Viśuddhamati, and concerns the complete purity of the Tathāgata as expressed through moral discipline, meditative absorption, patience, and related virtues. As a dhāraṇī sūtra, it combines doctrinal exposition on the qualities of buddhahood with the sacred formulaic character typical of this genre of canonical literature. It is preserved in the Kangyur alongside a preceding dhāraṇī text known as the "Stack of Flowers," with which it shares a colophon, situating it within the dhāraṇī section of the collection.
