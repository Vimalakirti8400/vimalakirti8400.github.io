About this text

The Rite of Recollecting Glorious Vajrasattva, known in Sanskrit as Śrī Vajrasattva Anusmṛti Nāma Vidhi and in Tibetan as Pal Dorjé Sempa Jesu Drenpé Choga, is a short tantric ritual manual devoted to the meditative recollection and worship of Vajrasattva, the embodiment of the purity of all the buddhas. Written largely in verse, it guides the practitioner through visualizing the maṇḍala as a magical display of nondual wisdom, generating the deity with his consort, retinue goddesses and door guardians from seed syllables, and then making offerings, praises, mantra recitations and hand gestures that unite skillful means and wisdom, before closing with a torma offering and dedication. It is Toh 1682 in the Tengyur, where it belongs to the collection of tantra commentaries and practice texts, and its colophon attributes it to a great Indian master.

Homage to glorious Vajrasattva.

In one's own place, delightful with its host of objects,
in the maṇḍala, a conventional magical display,
all objects are joined in pure nonduality:
the supreme Victor, arisen from the mind of awakening.
Therein the Dharma, the play of that appearance,
unobserved to the very limit of existence, halts the three times.
Endowed with the tantra of activity to be examined,
the place of praise of the maṇḍala of the family lord,
the root tantra, definitive, supremely secret,
the great means of accomplishing it,
the supreme vehicle of the dual and the nondual,
which brings forth recollection:
this excellent rite is marvelous.

Vajra words of the lineage of secret meaning,
bound with the marks of the continuum of the three times,
the place of praise of the maṇḍala of the family lord,
wherein all the activities of saṃsāra abide and are engaged:
the excellent maṇḍala of the solitary hero
is explained together with the branches of approach and accomplishment.
The domain of the intent of all the buddhas,
complete in all the branches of action,
which reveals the definitive secret,
is the unsurpassed maṇḍala of the result.

What is called "all times,"
the Dharma from which view and the rest arise,
purifies all states of affliction,
and through the equality of intrinsic nature is free of elaboration.
The perfect buddha, great bliss,
made clear through constant habituation,
is the companion that completes the branches and the two paths.
By thorough training one enters it
and traverses all the stations of the vidyādharas.
All the parts of cause and condition complete,
by the power of common and particular conditions,
the later continuum arising from the former,
it is the very nature of the assembled results.

All the vidyādharas dwelling on the grounds, each on its ground,
by way of union with self-awareness,
with all the bases of secret mantra and mudrā,
and by the force of the particular remedies for what is adverse,
are ever endowed with limitless accomplishments;
and at the end of perfecting all the dharmas of meditative absorption
they see the abode of all the buddhas
and perfect the supreme definitive ground.
Turning all the wheels of the families,
through the purity of the places of the families,
the supreme family, a host of excellent qualities,
is joined in union by the Victors with all the families.

The supreme vehicle, truly definitive,
the sublime domain arisen from the sublime,
is itself a garland in the way it abides,
adorned with true bliss.
The great maṇḍala, a pleasure grove,
the supreme city of the King of Dharma:
by entering the wheel of Dharma in stages,
the excellent fruit of the Dharma
truly arises through thorough application,
just as, from a flower garden, colors and scents
bring satisfaction to the hosts of desires;
one's own place is a garland of the limbs of awakening.

Approach and close approach,
accomplishment and great accomplishment:
when all the parts of these branches are complete,
the particular rites of pacifying and the rest are set out.
Through the branches of the vehicle of the buddhas
the yoga that is applied is accomplished.
The very domain of those skilled in the boundaries of the grounds
is fully perfected by power together with its conditions.

The guru endowed with the power of the branches of the maṇḍala,
abiding in excellence, keeper of the yogic discipline,
lord of the fruit, the jewel of good qualities,
pure in the supreme mind of awakening:
having praised him with true devotion,
supplicate him with faith in your three doors.
Holding all the branches of his instruction,
with a supreme feast for the close retinue,
having pleased him in the great maṇḍala,
the empowerment of the wheel, fully complete,
together with its branches and pledges,
is taught through definitive entry.

The various meanings of the secret tantras,
the unsurpassed branch that guides,
the mudrā that is definitively taught:
complete in its branches, it is the supreme vehicle.

In solitary places,
or in one's own pleasure garden and the like,
the fortunate, in every manifold way,
with supreme bliss in every manifold way,
should accomplish the intentions, in every manifold way,
just as they please.

Sweep the surroundings of the place,
clean it with fragrant water,
and anoint the excellent, supreme ground of the maṇḍala
with auspicious purifying substances.
In the ten directions, with canopies and victory banners,
pennants, parasols, streamers and yak-tail whisks,
masses of jewels and flower garlands,
magically displayed food, drink and sense pleasures,
with mudrās, garments and ornaments,
make everything beautiful without exception.
The supreme discipline of method and wisdom,
the expanse of the play of blissful wisdom:
by imagining it as a magical display,
the wheel of activity becomes stable.
With all the hosts of offerings
and with tormas, adorn every place;
cleanse and sprinkle, bless it as auspicious,
and set the mantras and mudrās in order.

The hosts of afflictions in all the places where beings go,
through the force of evil, the power of nonvirtue and the rest,
crush every place with misery and torment
and ruin the mindstreams of self and others, all of them.
To liberate them with a mind of great compassion,
all of them without exception, together with their domains,
the wisdom body of single mindfulness
is fully liberated through pure meditative absorption.
The intrinsic nature of all outer and inner phenomena
is the nondual mind of awakening;
transcending the objects that appear therein,
it is the spontaneously present nature of reality.

For one who has wholly relinquished this way,
then from that, the continuum is likewise.
Oh! The domain of great compassion,
the very perfection of the maṇḍala of the family!
Through meditative absorption on the threefold deity,
join the three kinds of mantra and mudrā;
having perfected the power and might of blessing,
purify self and others and make them clear.
The lord of body, speech and mind of the supreme family,
through fully applying true light rays,
extends the clear maṇḍala.
With the attire of the mudrās of the three modes,
arising well in a perfect array,
the abode of the four immeasurables becomes complete.
Invited from every side and made stable:
this is the mudrā of close application.

delighting in the work of the complete maṇḍala,
you who conquer the objects of ignorance,
in the great abode of awareness, in union,
joining in the blissful manner of the mudrā:

By the force of joining mudrā and secret mantra,
