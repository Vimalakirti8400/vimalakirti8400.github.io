About this text

The Noble One Hundred and Eight Names of Sarvanīvaraṇaviṣkambhin, Together with the Dhāraṇī-Mantra, known in Tibetan as Phakpa Dribpa Tamche Nampar Selwé Tsen Gya Tsa Gyepa Zung Ngak Dang Chepa, is a short scriptural work devoted to the bodhisattva whose Sanskrit name, Sarvanīvaraṇaviṣkambhin, means "Remover of All Obscurations." It opens with homage to this great being and a brief mantra, after which the Tathāgata Vairocana stretches out his right hand, praises the bodhisattva for bringing forth his dhāraṇī-mantra, and explains the benefits it brings to beings. Vairocana says that anyone who hears it gains roots of virtue as vast as if planted before countless buddhas, and becomes free of obscurations simply by having it reach their ears. The work is Toh 880 in the Kangyur, where it belongs among the dhāraṇī texts, the class of scriptures centered on sacred formulas and the recitation of names for protection, purification, and spiritual merit.

The Noble One Hundred and Eight Names of Sarvanīvaraṇaviṣkambhin, Together with the Dhāraṇī-Mantra

Homage to the bodhisattva, the great being Sarvanīvaraṇaviṣkambhin.

oṃ amṛta bindu ja ba mahā su kha svāhā

Then the Blessed One, the Tathāgata Vairocana, stretched out his right hand, gladdened the bodhisattva, the great being Sarvanīvaraṇaviṣkambhin, and said: "You who possess a host of precious qualities, by bringing forth this dhāraṇī-mantra of yours you produce great happiness for sentient beings. Excellent, excellent!

"O son of the Victors, whoever hears this dhāraṇī-mantra will come to possess roots of virtue produced in the presence of as many tathāgatas as there are grains of sand in ten Ganges rivers. Whoever has it reach their ears will also become free from obscurations. If, before a stupa containing relics, on the eighth or the fifteenth day of the waxing fortnight, one fasts and keeps the poṣadha observance, makes offerings of perfumes, incense, lamps, flowers, garlands, ointments and so forth, circumambulates and recites it, one will obtain whatever excellent thing one wishes, and will be freed from all evil deeds, from suffering, and from all fears. If one makes offerings of perfumes and the like to the bodhisattva Sarvanīvaraṇaviṣkambhin and recites it, he will appear in one's dreams, fulfill all one's aspirations, and dispel all afflictions and suffering."

Then Great Vajra Flower Crown, together with the Lord of the Three Worlds, rose from his seat in the midst of that great circle of the assembly, bowed to the great bodhisattva Sarvanīvaraṇaviṣkambhin, and praised him with one hundred and eight names:

Great chief of all sentient beings,
Great in the way of the bodhisattvas,
Gone beyond all the stains of affliction,
Dharmadhātu, homage to you!

Definitively freed from all obscurations,
Clearing away all the stains of affliction,
Fully liberated from every fault,
Flawless one, homage to you!

Showing the way of the single path,
One in the way of the single vehicle,
Having attained the way of the Great Vehicle,
Pure vehicle, homage to you!

Great light who has attained the light,
Maṇḍala of qualities, lord of the world,
Shining with white light like the moon,
Transcender of the world, homage to you!

Great purity of the Great Vehicle,
Who has realized the vehicle of the buddhas,
Renowned in the vajra maṇḍala,
Great vajra, homage to you!

Great hero, victorious over Māra,
Great flourishing one who conquers the afflictions,
Conqueror of obstructors, conqueror of Yama,
Net of jewels, homage to you!

Great moon, great sun,
Great Meru, great lord,
Mahādeva, great protector,
Great in riches, homage to you!

You who cut through all obscurations,
You who clear away all obscurations,
Omniscient one, great embodiment of qualities,
Dispeller, homage to you!

Having entered the sphere of the buddhas,
You have gone to the sphere of the Dharma;
Lord of wisdom, great in wisdom,
Free of craving, homage to you!

Beautiful in form, knower of the essence of qualities,
Adorned with the vajra maṇḍala,
Path of the buddhas, greatly meaningful,
Pure wisdom, homage to you!

Abiding in the teaching, powerful lord,
Bringer of happiness, your mind at peace,
Vast, stainless, endowed with wisdom,
Pure light, homage to you!

Profound, steadfast and overpowering,
Peaceful, purifier of the world,
Teacher worthy of refuge in the three realms of existence,
Mighty hero, homage to you!

Greatly fortunate, destroyer of afflictions,
Crusher of the maṇḍala of saṃsāra,
Peaceful, pacifying, source of bliss,
Calmer of the floods, homage to you!

Cause of liberation, great Dharma tradition,
Abiding in the happy destinies, stainless,
Possessed of a pure mind, knower of peace,
Dispeller of faults, homage to you!

Renowned at the vast summit,
Taming with many great mantras,
Great essence, supreme maṇḍala,
Knower of the maṇḍala, homage to you!

Dispeller of the three sufferings, supreme of the three,
Your sphere like that of the Three Jewels,
Lord who has found the threefold knowledge,
Three liberations, homage to you!

Having attained the wealth
Of a bodhisattva's qualities, and attained sameness,
For countless eons
You gathered the great accumulations; homage to you!

Abiding on the grounds, you attained the great ground,
The perfect fruit of the grounds,
Born of truly holding the grounds,
Ground of wisdom, homage to you!

Having gained mastery of samādhi,
Abandoning the three misdeeds, clearing away evil,
Knower of samādhi, vast one,
Equal in sameness, homage to you!

Great protector, great light,
Clearing away the stains of great suffering,
Pacified, pacifying without remainder,
Farthest shore of qualities, homage to you!

Possessed of speech equal to all the buddhas,
Perfect in the peace of all phenomena,
Of the family of the Three Jewels, without compare,
Infinite jewel, homage to you!

Great vajra, stainless and supreme,
Holding the vajra weapon, free of evil,
Possessing the domain of all of space,
Having an ocean as the sphere of your wisdom,
One in domain with the tathāgatas,
Dispeller who purifies the stains,
Great in wisdom, son of the Victors,
Cutter of craving, homage to you!

Vajra maṇḍala, supreme vajra,
Tamer by the vajra, Vajradhara,
Might of those with vajra might,
Vajra light, homage to you!

Great jewel, jewel mind,
Jewel vajra of great radiance,
Beautiful jewel light, source of jewels,
Protector of the jewel teaching,
Who has attained the domain of emptiness,
Who teaches wisdom through emptiness,
Possessing emptiness as the domain you enjoy,
Supreme emptiness, homage to you!

Of unequaled wisdom, endowed with wisdom,
Pure virtue, beyond the grounds,
Illuminator arisen from the one Dharma,
Great way that transcends the world,
Illuminator of the world, skilled in speech,
Wholly beyond the worldly concerns,
Having gained the quality of knowing the world,
Adorner of the maṇḍalas,
Dispeller of faults without remainder,
Benefactor of the multitudes of beings,
Renowned upholder of the earth,
Omniscient one, homage to you!

Knower of joy, enjoyer of joy,
Supreme among the supreme of the family of joy,
Whose thought is to bring about joy,
Shining in joy, homage to you!

Great in charm, gentle and full of intent,
Steadfast in the conduct of charm,
Vajra knowledge, supreme in charm,
Great in charm, homage to you!

Supreme among buddhas and bodhisattvas,
Great maṇḍala of the supreme vajra,
Abiding in the way of the Great Vehicle,
Unsurpassed supreme one, homage to you!

When the retinue of the vajra maṇḍala
Had praised with a hundred and eight names
The hero, supreme among bodhisattvas,
Sarvanīvaraṇaviṣkambhin,
The Blessed Vajrin,
Renowned for the vajra teaching, spoke:

"Oh, you of the maṇḍala of the Victor,
The maṇḍala of great splendor and light!
If anyone recites these hundred and eight names,
Each one in turn,
I will avert all their faults
And always follow close behind them.

Whatever wise one, rising at dawn,
And being clean, praises
Sarvanīvaraṇaviṣkambhin,
That person's wrongdoing will cease to be.

Whoever, rising at dawn,
Recites the supreme hundred and eight names,
All who are hostile will come under that person's power,
And obstructors too will leave them altogether.

They will attain the purification of wrongdoing,
And likewise their merit will increase;
They will enjoy the ocean of wisdom
And attain the great supreme ground.

Whoever, keeping to a fixed place,
Recites the supreme hundred and eight names,
Will before long attain
The buddha ground of great splendor.

Whoever possesses the great tantra, of great intelligence,
And recites these supreme names,
Will before long enter
The teaching of the vajra maṇḍala."

Here ends The Hundred and Eight Names of the Great Bodhisattva Sarvanīvaraṇaviṣkambhin, Together with the Dhāraṇī-Mantra.

Translator's notes

The speaker is introduced as "Great Vajra Flower Crown, together with the Lord of the Three Worlds." This is uncertain. The Tibetan may name one figure or two. The first element (with thod, "crown, headdress" or "skull") could not be securely identified with a Sanskrit name, so it is translated literally.

In the twenty-second stanza, the line rendered "holding the vajra weapon" reads irregularly in the Tibetan (mtshon pa snams, likely for mtshon bsnams, "holding a weapon"). The evident reading is translated.

In the seventh stanza, lha chen is rendered by its Sanskrit form Mahādeva, an epithet of Śiva. The phrase rendered "the maṇḍala of great splendor and light" (Tibetan 'byor chen snang ba) may allude to Vairocana, the "Illuminator," whose maṇḍala frames the text. The reading is uncertain.

"Keeping to a fixed place" (Tibetan nges par gnas byas) could also mean "remaining steadfast" in the practice.

"Possesses the great tantra" (Tibetan rgyud chen ldan) could also be read as "of great lineage" or "of a great mental continuum."
