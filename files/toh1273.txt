The Rite of the Vidyā of the Glorious Ten Wrathful Ones (Śrīdaśakrodhavidyāvidhi)

The Rite of the Vidyā of the Glorious Ten Wrathful Ones, known in Sanskrit as Śrī Daśakrodhavidyāvidhi and in Tibetan as Pal Trowo Chü Rikpé Choga, is a brief tantric ritual manual catalogued as Toh 1273 in the Tengyur, where it belongs among the commentaries and ritual works on the tantras. It instructs the practitioner to draw an eight-spoked wheel-shaped diagram on birch bark or a leaf and to inscribe in each of its eight directions the mantra of one of the ten wrathful deities, with a further mantra in the hub and a long protective mantra, invoking the White Parasol and related protectors, written around the rim. Each mantra asks for protection, pacification and increase for the one who wears the diagram, so the work functions as a protective amulet rite. Its colophon attributes it to the paṇḍita Jetāri and records that it was translated at the monastery of Tharpaling by the Tibetan monk Nyima Gyaltsen Palsangpo under the guidance of an Indian grammarian.

The Rite of the Vidyā of the Glorious Ten Wrathful Ones is complete. It was composed by the paṇḍita Jetāri.

It was well translated at the great monastery of glorious Tharpaling by the Śākya monk Nyima Gyaltsen Palsangpo, under the great grammarian Jaitakarṇali.
