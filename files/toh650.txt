The Dhāraṇī Called 'That Which Increases Wisdom' (Prajñāvardhanī nāma dhāraṇī)

All those who dwell in water and so forth, and whoever drinks that water, will have all their misdeeds and obscurations purified and will come to travel the path of happy destinies. The heart of the Wisdom Star dhāraṇī is complete.

In the language of India: Prajñāvardhanī nāma dhāraṇī. In the Tibetan language: The dhāraṇī called "That Which Increases Wisdom."

Homage to the Three Jewels. Homage to the noble Avalokiteśvara, bodhisattva, great being, one of great compassion.

Thus: Oṃ biba biba, prajñāvarddhani, jara jara, medhāvarddhani, dhiri dhiri, buddhivarddhani svāhā.

The ritual practice of this mantra is as follows. Each morning, recite it twenty-one times over a full cup of fresh barley gruel, and drink it continually for the span of one month. By doing so, one will come to retain what one has heard.

The dhāraṇī called "That Which Increases Wisdom" is complete.
