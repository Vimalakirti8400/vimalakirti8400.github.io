The Noble Sūtra of the Great Vehicle Called 'The Twelve Buddhas' (Ārya-dvādaśabuddha-nāma-mahāyānasūtra)

The Noble Sūtra of the Great Vehicle Called "The Twelve Buddhas," known in Sanskrit as the Ārya-dvādaśabuddha-nāma-mahāyānasūtra and in Tibetan as Pakpa Sangye Chunyipa Zhejawa Tekpa Chenpo'i Do, is a Mahāyāna sūtra in which the Buddha, dwelling on Vulture Peak Mountain near Rājagṛha, addresses the bodhisattva Maitreya and reveals the existence of twelve buddhas presiding over distant buddha-fields in the eastern direction. The text belongs to the genre of buddhānusmṛti, or recollection of the buddhas, presenting the names and qualities of these awakened beings as objects of devotion and aspiration. It is Toh 511 in the Kangyur, catalogued among the general sūtra collections of the Mahāyāna section of the Tibetan Buddhist canon.

Homage to all buddhas and bodhisattvas.

Thus did I hear. At one time the Blessed One was dwelling on Vulture Peak Mountain near Rājagṛha together with a great Saṅgha of twelve hundred and fifty monks, and also together with a great Saṅgha of twenty-two thousand bodhisattvas headed by the bodhisattva Ajita (the Undefeated) and others.

Then the Blessed One spoke to the bodhisattva, the great being, Maitreya: "Maitreya, beyond buddha-fields to the east of this buddha-field, past buddha-fields numbering as many atoms as there are in ten times one hundred thousand myriads of koṭis of buddha-fields too numerous to express, there exists a world-system called Muktimān, the Pearl-Adorned. There dwells, even now, a Tathāgata, arhat, perfectly complete buddha whose name is Splendor-of-the-Sky, Stainless, Dust-Dispelling, Supremely Beautiful, Crown of Qualities, Light-Flame-Lotus, Whose Body Is Like the Rays of Vaidūrya and Precious Gems, Worshipped with Supreme and Sacred Incense, Whose Body Is Splendidly Adorned and Well-Ornamented, Whose Crown Emits Measureless Rays of the Sun from Its Very Pinnacle, Adorned with Moonlight Aspiration-Prayers, Supremely Manifested in Great Array, Risen Beyond the Dharmadhātu, Free of Attachment, Jewel King — and he lives, abides, and teaches the Dharma.

Whoever holds the name of that Victor will be turned back from saṃsāra for great aeons as numerous as the atoms of Jambudvīpa. That buddha has prophesied the bodhisattva, the great being, called Incomparable. That very one will, immediately after the passing of the sacred Dharma of the Tathāgata, arhat, perfectly complete buddha — Splendor-of-the-Sky, Stainless, Dust-Dispelling, Supremely Beautiful, Crown of Qualities, Light-Flame-Lotus, Whose Body Is Like the Rays of Vaidūrya and Precious Gems, Worshipped with Supreme and Sacred Incense, Whose Body Is Splendidly Adorned and Well-Ornamented, Whose Crown Emits Measureless Rays of the Sun from Its Very Pinnacle, Adorned with Moonlight Aspiration-Prayers, Supremely Manifested in Great Array, Risen Beyond the Dharmadhātu, Free of Attachment, Jewel King — manifest complete perfect awakening in the unsurpassed, perfectly complete enlightenment, and will arise in the world as the Tathāgata, arhat, perfectly complete buddha, possessed of knowledge and conduct, Sugata, knower of the world, unsurpassed guide of persons to be tamed, teacher of gods and humans, buddha, Blessed One, whose name is Water-God, Youthful, Lamp of the Sun, Moon-Flower Lotus, Golden-Hued, Whose Body Is Like the Sky, Vairocana, Supremely Adorned with Rays, Whose Unobstructed Wheel-Maṇḍala Pours Forth Rays in All Ten Directions and Illuminates All World-Systems, Victory Banner Among Blossoms, King.

In that same eastern direction, one shall also hold the name of the Tathāgata, arhat, perfectly complete buddha called Stainless Light of Total Array. Recite it. Bow down.

Of the southern direction, one shall also hold the name of the Tathāgata, arhat, perfectly complete buddha called Intended Ornament of Courageous Eloquence. Reflect on it.

Of the western direction, one shall also hold the name of the Tathāgata Stainless Moon-Crown King of Renown. Bow down.

Of the northern direction, one shall also hold the name of the Tathāgata Who Causes the Array of Flowers to Shine. Bow down.

Of the southeast direction, one shall also hold the name of the Tathāgata called Light Maker. Bow down.

Of the southwest direction, one shall also hold the name of the Tathāgata called Jewel Supreme Pinnacle of Renown. Bow down.

Of the northwest direction, one shall also hold the name of the Tathāgata called Fearless All-Seeing. Bow down.

Of the northeast direction, one shall also hold the name of the Tathāgata called Free from Fear, Without Shame, Causing No Trembling. Bow down.

Of the direction below, one shall also hold the name of the Tathāgata called Throat of the Rearing Lion. Bow down.

Of the direction above, one shall also hold the name of the Tathāgata called King of Golden Radiance and Splendor Free from Fear. Bow down.

O Maitreya, sons and daughters of faithful family, before these twelve Tathāgatas, arhats, perfectly complete buddhas, reciting their names, for ten days confess all wrongdoings and transgressions of every kind of non-virtue, rejoice in all roots of virtue, urge all the buddhas, entreat all the buddhas, and wholly dedicate all roots of virtue into the basic space of phenomena as a complete dedication. Through this, all non-virtues will be exhausted, and all karmic obscurations will be entirely purified.

After that, one will also attain excellence in the display of qualities of buddha fields, excellence in fearlessness, excellence in major marks, excellence in the Saṅgha of bodhisattvas, excellence in dhāraṇī, and excellence in meditative absorption. Just as one wishes, one will swiftly obtain full excellence in the utter purity of buddha fields and excellence in spiritual friends. One will not decline from, nor utterly fall away from, unsurpassed, perfectly complete awakening.

And even while wandering in saṃsāra during the time of that wandering, one will be universally beautiful, excel in enjoyment of resources, in noble lineage, in auspicious birth, in clan, in excellent form, and one will fully obtain a retinue of companions of like station.

Concerning this, the following is to be said:

In whatever place and wherever one is born,
in every single lifetime,
one becomes a source of joy and delight to all who see,
a maker of light, of great power,
and one of great abundance.

These bases of the dhāraṇī mantra are likewise to be recited.

Tadyathā: akhe makhe samantamukhe sautiyukte nirukte prabhe samayoge citta citta akhe makhe padane vibhrate samantaguṇi satya rame yukte prayukte hili mili masale ānale came aciti coce arahe bahucche māyugrahe hemavati jyotivati dharmacite cūtapaṅke avikṣapera dhṛkṣame skandhavibhakte.

I bow down to all buddhas and bodhisattvas. May these bases of the mantra be accomplished through the blessing of the buddhas.

All that is heard will be retained without loss.
Whoever recites the names of these protectors of the world
will swiftly abandon all lower destinies

Supreme meditative absorption (samādhi) and dhāraṇī
will become manifest to that person.
One will fully awaken to awakening.

The merit accumulated by one who holds

While practicing the conduct of awakening,

Gladdening the arising of buddhas,

Having beheld the lamp of the world,

Whoever holds these names —
for incalculable tens of millions of eons,

Whoever holds these names

Whoever holds these names —
for that one, throughout many eons,

One will swiftly attain full awakening.

Whoever holds these names

Whoever holds these names —

If a woman holds the names of the great sages,
without becoming attached to a woman's body,

Obtaining a human body,

They make offerings to the buddhas and their teachings, and fearlessly present their petitions. Those Victors, knowing their aspirations, will then give them prophecies. Having received prophecy from those protector-buddhas, one becomes utterly joyful and has no doubt whatsoever in the Victors' teaching.

Whoever holds these names will hear the profound Dharma from the protectors of the world, and having heard it, will retain all of it. Then the perfections (pāramitās), fearlessnesses, powers, and all the major and minor marks will be completely fulfilled without exception.

In whatever realm of those buddhas one wishes to be reborn, there one will fully realize unsurpassed and perfect awakening and become a buddha.

Whether man or woman, whoever holds these names — these qualities and others besides will be proclaimed continually by the buddhas for an entire age.

Having become heedful and having heard these perfect and glorious qualities, they will hold the names of the fully awakened buddhas renowned among all.

These buddhas — Splendor-Essence, Cloud-King, Supreme Among Humans, the Protectors of the World — whoever hears their names for an entire age, and whoever hears and venerates these names upon hearing them, those ones will become supremely wise, the highest among two-footed beings.

Upon hearing such names of those great sages, who that is heedful, learned, and possessed of wisdom would not hold them?

When the Blessed One had thus spoken, the bodhisattva great being Maitreya, those bodhisattvas, those monks, and the entire assembly gathered there — together with gods, humans, asuras (demigods), and gandharvas — rejoiced, and they acclaimed with approval what the Blessed One had taught.

The noble Mahāyāna sūtra called The Twelve Buddhas is complete.
