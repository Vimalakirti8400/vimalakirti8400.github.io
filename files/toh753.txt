In the Indian language: Vajrasūkṣmāpratihata-nāma-dhāraṇī. In the Tibetan language: the dhāraṇī called "The Subtle Vajra, Unobstructed."

Homage to all the buddhas and bodhisattvas. Homage to all the wrathful ones of the Sugata.

Thus did I hear at one time. The Blessed One entered into meditative absorption in the samādhi of the Vajra Family. Through the power of the buddhas, Vajrapāṇi, Lord of Secrets, displayed a radiant, blazing face bearing the syllable Hūṃ, and took his seat before the Blessed One. At that moment, from the emanation of the Blessed One's mind there arose a brahmin youth, who spoke these words to the Lord of Secrets:

"Lord of Secrets, Lord of Vidyās, Lord of Dhāraṇīs — please recite the secret mantras, the vidyā mantras, and the dhāraṇī mantras that powerfully crush and overwhelm, with fierce and overpowering subjugation, the lesser gods among the gods of the three divine abodes, and the lesser nāgas."

Vajrapāṇi spoke: "Brahmin youth, listen well and hold this in mind. I shall now recite this very heart-essence, which is constantly proclaimed by all the wrathful Sugatas.

Namo ratnatrayāya. Namaś caṇḍa-vajrapāṇaye, mahāyakṣasenāpataye. Tadyathā: Oṃ hara hara vajra, matha matha vajra, dhuna dhuna vajra, daha daha vajra, paca paca vajra, dhara dhara vajra, dhāraya dhāraya vajra, dāruṇa dāruṇa vajra, chinda chinda vajra, bhinda bhinda vajra, hūṃ phaṭ. Namaś caṇḍa-vajrakrodhāya. Hulu hulu, tiṣṭha tiṣṭha, bandha bandha, hana hana, amṛte hūṃ phaṭ.

"Brahmin youth, whoever takes a portion of the nectar of the Sugata swirling within, and recites these vajra words of all the wrathful ones, projecting from every single pore countless subtle vajras outward in the ten directions — all the evil habitual tendencies of every sentient being are purified; life and merit flourish greatly; one is liberated from enemies, poisons, and untimely death. As for any evil adversarial intent directed against such a person — one visualizes it as dispersed by a cloud of ricinus smoke, or as a fresh myrobalan fruit crushed flat beneath gold, and then recites these vajra words. By radiating the subtle vajras, the five aggregates and the five sense faculties of those adversaries are swiftly destroyed. If a god recites it, within one divine month or divine year, an asura's head will split open. If a monk recites it, the weapons of Māra are swiftly subdued. If a human recites it, the one at whom it is aimed will certainly be destroyed within one human month or five human years."

The Blessed One said "Excellent!" to Vajrapāṇi and the brahmin youth, and then vanished from sight.
