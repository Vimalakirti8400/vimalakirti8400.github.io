Homage to the buddhas, the Blessed Ones.

The Victor Vipaśyin dwells at the foot of the pāṭali tree. The Victor Śikhin dwells at the foot of the white lotus tree. Viśvabhū, protector of all, dwells at the foot of the sāla tree. The brahmin Krakucchanda, destroyer of saṃsāra, dwells at the foot of the śirīṣa tree. The buddha Kanakamuni, the golden sage, dwells at the foot of the udumbara tree. Kāśyapa, protector of light, dwells at the foot of the banyan tree. And Śākyamuni, the supreme sage, went to the most excellent place and there attained awakening, Gautama.

To all those buddhas of great miraculous power, whichever gods are seated there with devotion and faith, may those gods be gladdened and joyful, and may they always be virtuous and peaceful.

Homage to the buddhas.

May you who are two-footed be well. May you who are four-footed be well. May those who have set out upon the road be well, and may those who are returning be well. May the night be well, and the day be well. May the midday also be well. May you all be well in every way. May none of these become evil.

Every day is virtuous. Every star is auspicious. All the buddhas are of great miraculous power. All the arhats are free of defilement. By these words of truth, may all be well.

This is the aspiration prayer taught in the Great Peahen, Queen of Vidyā Mantras, together with its preceding material.
