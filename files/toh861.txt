This short text is a dhāraṇī whose closing title identifies it as The Heart of the Noble Vairocana, a brief mantra-based work devoted to the Buddha Vairocana, here called King of Light, and to the bodhisattva Ākāśagarbha. It opens with homage to the Three Jewels and to these two figures, then gives the dhāraṇī itself, which invokes Ākāśagarbha through the radiance of Vairocana. The text promises that one who recites it will succeed and be protected from weapons, fire, water, poison, sorcery, and threats from kings and thieves, and that where it is written down and kept, epidemics and contagious diseases will not arise. Homage to the Three Jewels. Homage to the Tathāgata, the arhat, the perfectly complete Buddha, the noble Vairocana, King of Light. Homage to the bodhisattva mahāsattva Ākāśagarbha.

Tadyathā: kala kala, kili kili, biri biri, huru huru, vairocana raśmi sañcodite, āgaccha ārya ākāśagarbha mahākāruṇika, pūraya hāśāna, dhāraya buddhe viśca yana, cara cara, ciri ciri svāhā.

By reciting this, one succeeds. Weapons, fire, water, poison, compounded poison, and sorcery will not be able to harm one. Kings, thieves, and all others will find no opportunity against one. Wherever it is written down and kept, epidemics, harms, and all contagious diseases will not arise. One will also attain the meditative absorption called Stainless Light.

The dhāraṇī called The Heart of the Noble Vairocana is complete.
