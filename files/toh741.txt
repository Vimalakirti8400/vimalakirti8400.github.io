The Twelve Names of the Glorious Goddess (Śrīdevīdvādaśanāma)

Toh 741 in the Kangyur is a short liturgical and devotional text known in Tibetan as "Dpal gyi lha mo'i mtshan bcu gnyis pa," which translates into English as "The Twelve Names of the Glorious Goddess," a title corresponding in Sanskrit to something like "Śrīdevī Dvādaśanāma." It belongs to the general class of dhāraṇī and praise literature within the Kangyur, texts concerned with the recitation of sacred names and the merits thereby obtained. The text is set in the pure land of Sukhāvatī, where the Buddha addresses Avalokiteśvara in the presence of the Great Glorious Goddess, a wrathful protective deity of considerable importance in Tibetan Buddhism, and proceeds to reveal the twelve names by which she is to be known and venerated. Recitation of these names is presented as a powerful practice conferring protection and spiritual benefit upon monks, nuns, laypeople, and all sentient beings alike.

Homage to the Three Jewels. Homage to the noble Avalokiteśvara. Homage to Vajrapāṇi. Homage to the Great Glorious Goddess.

Thus did I hear. At one time the Blessed One was dwelling in the world of Sukhāvatī. Then the bodhisattva, the great being Avalokiteśvara went to where the Blessed One was, and having arrived, prostrated with his head at the feet of the Blessed One and sat down to one side.

Then also the bodhisattva, the great being, the Great Glorious Goddess, went to where the Blessed One was, drew near, prostrated with her head at the feet of the Blessed One, and sat down to one side.

Then the Blessed One, having looked upon the Great Glorious One, spoke thus to Avalokiteśvara: "Avalokiteśvara, any monk, nun, layman, or laywoman whatsoever, or any sentient being who knows the twelve names of this Great Glorious One, who bears them, reads them, puts them into practice, and recites them — that one, abandoning poverty, shall become supremely wealthy." Thereupon the entire assembly of spirits likewise said, "May it be so!"

The twelve names of the Great Glorious Goddess are as follows: The Auspicious One, the Resplendent One, the Lotus-Garlanded One, the Mistress of Wealth, the White-Robed One, the Greatly Renowned One, the Lotus-Eyed Sovereign, the Greatly Radiant One, the Active One, the Bestower of Food, the One Endowed with Jewel-Light, and the Supremely Glorious One.

Homage to all the buddhas. Homage to Avalokiteśvara. Homage to the Great Glorious One.

It is as follows: Jini, Ghriṇi — O you who accomplish all needs. Simi simi, nimi nimi — may you dispel all my ignorance. Svāhā!

When the Blessed One had thus spoken, the bodhisattva, the great being Avalokiteśvara rejoiced in what the Blessed One had declared.

The Twelve Names of the Glorious Goddess.
