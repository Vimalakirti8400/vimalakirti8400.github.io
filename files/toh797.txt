The Terrifier of All Maras (Sarvamārabhayaṅkara)

The Noble Dhāraṇī "The Terrifier of All Maras" (Tibetan: Phags pa bdud thams cad skrag par byed pa) is a short dhāraṇī text, a brief invocation built around a mantra, whose Sanskrit title appears to be Āryasarvamāratrāsana-nāma-dhāraṇī, though that reconstruction should be treated as tentative. It opens with homage to the Blessed One, Śākyamuni, and then gives the mantra itself, which draws on the imagery of jewels, blazing light, and the womb of the dharma-realm. The text then describes the effects of reciting it: virtuous gods are delighted and offer protection, while hostile forces, the maras, tremble and lose consciousness, and arrogant, unruly beings are struck with fear. It is Toh 797 in the Kangyur, where it belongs to the Tantra section as one of the many dhāraṇīs and protective mantras preserved there.
