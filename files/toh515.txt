Homage to all buddhas and bodhisattvas.

Thus did I hear. At one time the Blessed One was dwelling in a pleasure grove by the shore of the ocean, seated upon a lion throne amid the trees of the buddhas' domain of activity, surrounded on all sides by a retinue of gods, facing forward, and teaching the Dharma.

Then the Blessed One declared to the sons of the gods of the Pure Abodes, headed by the Great Lord of Power Auspicious Ascetic, as follows: "Sons of the gods, therefore listen well, take it to mind, and I shall speak. In a later time, a Tathāgata will teach the Dharma-category of the dhāraṇī called 'The Heart of the Buddha' in order to gather in the four assemblies, to receive them under care, to fully receive them all under care, to bring them to distinction, to accomplish all aims and all Dharma in completeness, and to enable those who have genuinely entered the Great Vehicle to attain excellence in every aspect. He will uphold it.

Now, you are to take it to mind, and after I have passed into nirvāṇa, in the time to come you shall teach it to the four assemblies who have genuinely entered the Great Vehicle, speak it, and without allowing it to be lost, proclaim it with certainty. For all those who have fallen into poverty and all those who are burdened with obscurations, preserve the speech of the Tathāgata, declare it with certainty, and proclaim it."

They replied: "Blessed One, so shall it be. Just as the Tathāgata has declared, so do we request it. In accordance with the Tathāgata's command, we shall fulfill this with diligence and completeness."

The Blessed One then declared: "Sons of the gods, what is the Dharma-category of the dhāraṇī called 'The Heart of the Buddha'? It is this:

Sons of the gods, this is the Dharma-category of the dhāraṇī called 'The Heart of the Buddha.' Whatever son of good family or daughter of good family among the four assemblies who have genuinely entered the Great Vehicle carries it upon the body or recites it well and retains it should not think poorly of it. The Tathāgata, exactly as he is, will dwell at that person's crown of the head, at the border above it. Some will certainly come to see a buddha. Some will come to hear the Dharma. Some will come to teach the Dharma. Some will come to hold the dhāraṇī without forgetting it.

Those who abide on the path of training will find their stream of the path uninterrupted and their stream of teaching the Dharma uninterrupted. They will retain all their holdings without forgetting them. They will accomplish in a single birth what remains to be done. Being endowed with these qualities, they will attain the dhāraṇī that holds the entirety of the ocean of all that has been heard. They will attain the patient acceptance of the non-arising of phenomena (anutpattikadharmakṣānti), and from that point onwards they will come to successively attain all things, up to and including the unobstructed liberation of a buddha in its complete fullness.

You must take to heart in this very life the intent of my teaching. And to those sons of good family or daughters of good family, you must teach it unmistakably and proclaim it. Do not discard it carelessly. Do not defer it. Do not forget it.

The assembly responded: Blessed One, we will accomplish it exactly so. This Dharma-category of the dhāraṇī called 'The Heart of the Buddha' — in the final age we will teach it to the four assemblies without deferral and without interruption, and we will expound it. To those sons of good family or daughters of good family as well we will teach it unmistakably and proclaim it. By doing so, we will be in accord with the scriptural word of the Tathāgata. We will thus have thoroughly preserved the noble Dharma of the Tathāgata. And for those sons of good family or daughters of good family it will be a great meaning and a great benefit.

The Blessed One then declared: Sons of the gods, therefore do exactly as has been said. This is the instruction I entrust to you.

When the Blessed One had spoken these words, the sons of the gods of the pure abodes — Maheśvara (Śiva) of excellent austerity and all the rest — together with the entire retinue, were filled with joy, and they offered praise to what the Blessed One had taught.

The noble dhāraṇī called 'The Heart of the Buddha' is complete.
