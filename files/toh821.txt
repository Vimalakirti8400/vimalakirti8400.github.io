The Auspicious Verses of the Seven Buddhas

This short verse text is an auspicious benediction centered on the buddhas of the past and present, known in English as something like "Verses of Auspiciousness" and traditionally titled in Tibetan with the word for auspiciousness, bkra shis; I cannot determine the Sanskrit title with confidence, though it would correspond to a maṅgala-type composition. After a prostration to the Three Jewels, it moves through the buddhas in sequence, beginning with Vipaśyin, Śikhin, and Viśvabhū, giving for each the lineage, the human lifespan of the age, the city, and the tree beneath which he awakened. Each stanza closes with a refrain wishing that auspicious perfection may be present here today, so the text serves as a blessing recited to invoke good fortune. It is Toh 821 in the Kangyur, where it belongs among the tantra-section texts of auspicious verses and benedictions.

I prostrate to the Three Jewels.

The Blessed One Vipaśyin, of the glorious warrior lineage,
When human life was eighty thousand years,
In the city of Bandhumatī,
Awakened fully beneath the pāṭali tree.
May there be auspicious perfection here today, O Sugata.

The Sugata Śikhin, who bears the crown protuberance, of the warrior lineage, endowed with miraculous power,
When human life was seventy thousand years,
In the city of Aruṇavatī,
Awakened fully beneath the supreme puṇḍarīka tree.
May there be auspicious perfection here today, O Sugata.

Viśvabhū, protector of all the Victors, of the warrior lineage, possessed of intelligence,
When human life was sixty thousand years,
In the city of Anupamā,
Awakened fully beneath the supreme sāla tree.
May there be auspicious perfection here today, O Sugata.

Krakucchanda, sage who destroys saṃsāra, powerful one, of the brahmin lineage,
When human life was forty thousand years,
In the city of Kṣemāvatī,
Awakened fully beneath the śirīṣa tree.
May there be auspicious perfection here today, O Sugata.

Kanakamuni, guide, golden sage, supreme in discipline, of the brahmin lineage,
When human life was thirty thousand years,
In the city of Śobhāvatī,
Awakened fully beneath the udumbara tree.
May there be auspicious perfection here today, O Sugata.

Kāśyapa, protector, "Guardian of Light," of the warrior lineage, of unsurpassed qualities,
When human life was twenty thousand years,
In Vārāṇasī,
Awakened fully beneath the nyagrodha tree.
May there be auspicious perfection here today, O Sugata.

Śākyamuni, protector of beings, of the warrior lineage, endowed with diligence,
When human life was a hundred years,
In the city of the Śākyas,
Awakened fully beneath the aśvattha tree.
May there be auspicious perfection here today, O Sugata.

That auspicious perfection of the seven buddhas,
The wondrous blessing of the Sugatas of all directions and times,
The purity of the dharmadhātu, the power of dependent origination, unchanging:
May it be so.
