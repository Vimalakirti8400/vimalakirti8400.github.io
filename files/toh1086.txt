The Noble Dhāraṇī of Mahākāla That Brings Liberation from All Fevers (Āryamahākāladhāraṇī Sarvajvaravimocanī)

The Noble Dhāraṇī of Mahākāla That Brings Liberation from All Fevers, known in Tibetan as Pakpa Nakpo Chenpö Zung Rimné Tamché Lé Tarwar Jepa, is a brief protective incantation addressed to Mahākāla, the Great Black One, a fierce guardian deity. After paying homage to all buddhas and bodhisattvas, the text introduces the dhāraṇī as the spell of Mahākāla, described as a dark yellow figure with sharp fangs, and presents it as a means of gaining complete freedom from fever and febrile illness. Its core is a Sanskrit mantra that commands the fever to be struck down and burned away, then asks that it release each part of the body in turn, from the head, eyes, and ears down to the hands, feet, and fingers, before ordering it to depart elsewhere. The work is Toh 1086 in the Kangyur, where it belongs among the dhāraṇī texts, the collection of short ritual formulas recited for healing and protection.

The Noble Dhāraṇī of Mahākāla That Brings Liberation from All Fevers

Homage to all buddhas and bodhisattvas.

This should be recited: the dhāraṇī of Mahākāla, the dark yellow man with sharp fangs, the Great Fang, which brings complete liberation from all fevers.

In whatever direction I look, there may I be free of fever!
