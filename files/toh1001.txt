The Dhāraṇī of Noble Tārā (Āryatārādhāraṇī)

The Dhāraṇī of Noble Tārā, which may be rendered in Sanskrit as Āryatārādhāraṇī and in Tibetan as 'Phags ma sgrol ma'i gzungs, is a very brief incantation text devoted to the female bodhisattva Tārā. After paying homage to the Three Jewels and to the noble Avalokiteśvara, the great compassionate bodhisattva, it presents a single mantra built on Tārā's characteristic syllables, tāre tuttare ture. The mantra calls on her to crush, immobilize, bewilder, and bind all malevolent and hostile forces on behalf of the reciter. The work is Toh 1001 in the Kangyur, where it belongs to the class of dhāraṇī texts, the short protective spells gathered in the canon's compendium of dhāraṇīs.

Homage to the Three Jewels.

namo ratnatrayāya nama āryāvalokiteśvarāya bodhisatvāya mahāsahāya mahākāruṇikāya tadyathā oṃ tāre tuttare ture sarva duṣṭa praduṣṭaṃ mama kṛte jambhaya stambhaya mohaya bandhaya hūṃ hūṃ hūṃ phaṭ phaṭ phaṭ sarva duṣṭa stambhani tāre svāhā
