The Dhāraṇī for Retaining the Noble Avataṃsaka

The Dhāraṇī for Retaining the Noble Avataṃsaka is a very brief incantation that offers a shortcut to the merit of one of the largest scriptures in the Mahāyāna tradition, the Avataṃsaka Sūtra, known in Tibetan as Phal po che. After a homage to the Three Jewels, the text gives a short mantra, opening with namaḥ sarva buddhānām and continuing with seed syllables such as oṃ and bhūṃ. It then declares that whoever retains this formula will in effect retain the noble Avataṃsaka itself, and that writing it out or reading it aloud brings the same result. The work is Toh 940 in the Kangyur, where it belongs among the dhāraṇī texts, the class of short ritual formulas valued for preserving and invoking the power of the Buddha's teachings.

Homage to the Three Jewels.

oṃ nthāṃ namaḥsarba bud ṅhā nāṃ oṃ māṃ bhūṃ mūṃ tadya thā oṃ bhūṃ

By retaining this, one will come to retain the noble Avataṃsaka. Writing it and reading it likewise have the same effect.

The Dhāraṇī for Retaining the Noble Avataṃsaka is complete.

This concludes the translation. The following are translator's notes.

"Avataṃsaka" renders ཕལ་པོ་ཆེ, the standard Tibetan name for the Buddhāvataṃsaka Sūtra, the Flower Ornament Scripture. The point of the dhāraṇī is that holding this short formula in memory carries the merit of holding that vast scripture. Writing out the dhāraṇī or reading it aloud is said to have the same effect.
