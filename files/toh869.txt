The Recollection of the Heart of All the Tathāgatas (Sarvatathāgatahṛdayānusmṛti)

The Recollection of the Heart of All the Tathāgatas (in Sanskrit, Sarvatathāgatahṛdayānusmṛti, though the Tibetan title line given here does not state it) is a very short dhāraṇī text, consisting of a single mantra followed by a statement of its benefit. The mantra opens with homage to the hearts of all the tathāgatas and concludes with the syllables "oṃ kuruṃgini svāhā." The brief colophon-like promise that follows declares that reciting it even once exhausts the obscurations of karma accumulated over eight hundred million eons. It belongs to the genre of protective and purificatory spells, a type of text that circulates widely in Buddhist practice for the removal of karmic hindrances. It is Toh 869 in the Kangyur, where it is classed among the tantra section's dhāraṇī texts.

Namaḥ sarva tathāgata hṛdaya anugate oṃ kuruṃgini svāhā.

By reciting this even once, even the obscurations of karma accumulated over eight hundred million eons will be exhausted.

Here ends the Recollection of the Heart of All the Tathāgatas.
