Homage to the Great Mother, the Perfection of Wisdom.

Thus did I hear. At one time the Blessed One was dwelling on Vulture Peak mountain at Rājagṛha, together with a great Saṅgha of thirteen hundred monks and many tens of millions of bodhisattvas.

At that time the Blessed One spoke these words to the venerable Ānanda: "Ānanda, take up this text called the Perfection of Wisdom in a Single Syllable for the benefit and happiness of sentient beings. It is this: A."

When the Blessed One had spoken these words, the venerable Ānanda, all those monks, and all those great bodhisattvas understood the perfection and, filled with joy, offered heartfelt praise to what the Blessed One had taught.

The text known as the Perfection of Wisdom in a Single Syllable, the Mother of All Tathāgatas, is complete.

The single syllable "A" is the entire content of this Perfection of Wisdom teaching. In Indo-Tibetan tradition the syllable A represents emptiness, the unborn nature of all phenomena, and the primordial ground of the Perfection of Wisdom, so its presentation here as the complete teaching is doctrinally intentional rather than textually defective.
