The Chapter on the Means of Accomplishment Set Forth from the Great Tantra of Susiddhikara (Susiddhikaramahātantrasādhanopāyikapaṭala)

Toh 807 in the Kangyur is the Susiddhikara, known in Sanskrit as the Susiddhikaramahātantrasādhanopāyikapaṭala and in Tibetan as the Legs par grub par byed pa'i rgyud chen po las sgrub pa'i thabs rim par phye ba, or "The Chapters on the Methods of Accomplishment Arranged in Sequence from the Great Tantra of Successful Accomplishment." It belongs to the class of kriyā and caryā tantras, texts devoted to ritual procedures, mantra recitation, and meditative practices aimed at achieving specific spiritual and worldly accomplishments (siddhi) through the cultivation of deities such as Mañjuśrī and Vajrapāṇi. Opening with homage to glorious Vajrapāṇi and an invocation involving the Blessed One Mañjughoṣa's gnosis, the text proceeds to lay out, in an organized series of chapters, the detailed practical instructions and ritual stages required for successful tantric accomplishment. As one of the more extensive and systematically arranged tantras of its kind, it served as an important source for later ritual and practical manuals within the broader tantric tradition.
