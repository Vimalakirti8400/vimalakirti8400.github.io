Homage to the Three Jewels.

Namo ratnatrayāya. Namo bhagavate vajrasārapramardani tathāgatāya arhate samyaksaṃbuddhāya. Tadyathā: oṃ vajre vajre, mahāvajre, mahātejovajre, mahāvidyāvajre, mahābodhicittavajre, mahābodhimaṇḍopasamkramaṇavajre, sarvakarmāvaraṇaviśodhanavajre svāhā.

The ritual of this dhāraṇī mantra is as follows. The mere moment of recollecting it causes one to have made offerings to, and to have prostrated at the feet of, all the blessed buddhas dwelling in the ten directions in a number equal to the finest particles of sand grains of the Ganges River. One comes to have made offerings of flowers, incense, perfume, garlands of flowers, unguents, powder, parasols, victory banners, all manner of pennants, thrones, canopies, garments, fine food and drink, and the like, together with ornaments and all manner of adornments. One becomes free of all evil and comes to possess all roots of virtue. Upon seeing all buddhas and bodhisattvas, one is perfectly relieved and gladdened. All gods, nāgas, yakṣas, gandharvas, asuras, garuḍas, kiṃnaras, great mahoragas, Vajrapāṇi and the other lords of secrets, the four great kings — all these follow in one's wake and truly accompany one,
