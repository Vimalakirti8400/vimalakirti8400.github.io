The Noble Dhāraṇī Called "Light of Wisdom" That Thoroughly Purifies All Destinies (Āryajñānālokā Nāma Dhāraṇī Sarvagatipariśodhanī)

This text is the *Dhāraṇī of Jñānolka*, known in Sanskrit as the *Āryajñānolkānāma­dhāraṇī­sarvagati­pariśodhanī* and in Tibetan as *'phags pa ye shes ta la la zhes bya ba'i gzungs 'gro ba thams cad yongs su sbyong ba*, which may be rendered in English as "The Noble Dhāraṇī of the Torch of Wisdom, Which Purifies All Rebirth Destinies." It belongs to the genre of dhāraṇī literature, consisting of sacred formulas invoking the power of buddhas and bodhisattvas, here specifically aimed at purifying beings across all realms of rebirth and dispelling obstacles that bind them to suffering. The text opens with an homage to the Buddha as the all-knowing one, setting a devotional tone that frames the dhāraṇī's subsequent recitation as an act of veneration and spiritual protection. As Toh 848, this work is found in the Kangyur, the section of the Tibetan Buddhist canon containing the translated words of the Buddha, within the broader category of tantric and dhāraṇī texts that serve ritual, protective, and soteriological functions for practitioners.
