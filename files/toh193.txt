The Noble Prophecy of the Great Goddess Śrī (Ārya-Śrī-Yamahādevī-vyākaraṇa)

Toh 193 in the Kangyur is a Mahāyāna sūtra known in Sanskrit as the Ārya-Śrī-Yamahādevī-vyākaraṇa and in Tibetan as the Pakpa Lhamo Chenmo Pal Lungtenpа, rendered in English as The Noble Prophecy of the Great Goddess Śrī. It belongs to the class of vyākaraṇa, or prophecy texts, in which a buddha foretells the future spiritual attainments or destiny of a particular being — in this case the great goddess Śrī, a prominent divine figure in both Hindu and Buddhist traditions.
