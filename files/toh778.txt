The Dhāraṇī for the Complete Purification of Offerings

The Dhāraṇī for the Complete Purification of Offerings (in Tibetan, Yongsu jongwé zungs) is a very short dhāraṇī text that gives a single mantra to be recited over offerings in order to purify them. Then, the dhāraṇī for the complete purification of offerings:

Namo bhagavate sumeru-kalparājāya tathāgatāya arhate samyaksambuddhāya. Tadyathā: Oṃ kalpa kalpe, mahā-kalpe, kalpa-pariśodhani svāhā.

It is taught that by reciting this dhāraṇī even once, one purifies offerings as vast as Mount Meru. Therefore, one should recite it five or six times.

Here ends the dhāraṇī for the complete purification of offerings.
