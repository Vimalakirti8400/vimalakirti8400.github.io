The Dhāraṇī for Thoroughly Purifying Offerings (Dakṣiṇāviśodhanadhāraṇī)

This short text, known as the Dhāraṇī for Thoroughly Purifying Offerings (Skt. *Dāna-viśodhana-dhāraṇī*; Tib. ཡོན་ཡོངས་སུ་སྦྱོང་བའི་གཟུངས), belongs to the dhāraṇī genre of Buddhist scripture, consisting of a brief invocation and mantra attributed to the Tathāgata Sumerukalparāja. It teaches that the recitation of this formula—centered on the mantra "oṃ kalpe kalpe mahākalpe kalpapariśodhani svāhā"—purifies offerings made to the Three Jewels, rendering them as vast and meritorious as Mount Meru itself, and recommends its repetition some five or six times to ensure this purification. As Toh 778 in the Kangyur, it is part of a broader collection of short dhāraṇī texts used in ritual practice to consecrate and enhance the efficacy of offerings.

Next, the dhāraṇī for thoroughly purifying offerings:

Namo bhagavate sumerukalparājāya tathāgatāya arhate samyaksaṃbuddhāya. Tadyathā: oṃ kalpe kalpe mahākalpe kalpapariśodhani svāhā.

It is taught that reciting this dhāraṇī once purifies offerings equal in measure to Mount Meru. Therefore one should recite it about five or six times.

Thus concludes the dhāraṇī for thoroughly purifying offerings.
