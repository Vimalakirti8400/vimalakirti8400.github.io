This short work, known in English as the Dhāraṇī for Causing Displeasure and preserved in Tibetan translation without a clearly attested Sanskrit title, is catalogued as Toh 781 in the Kangyur. It belongs to the broad class of dhāraṇī texts—brief ritual incantations attributed to the Buddhist canon that combine mantra recitation with simple material rites for practical ends. Its concise, formulaic structure is typical of the many minor dhāraṇī and mantra collections found in the Kangyur, which address specific worldly aims such as protection, influence over others, or, as in this case, the deliberate arousal of an emotional response in those one encounters.
