Homage to the Three Jewels.

Vairocana, who holds the ocean of sublime wisdom,
Whose body is like the full moon, who has reached the far limit of existence,
The auspiciousness of his complete and perfect realization of all things:
By that auspiciousness, may all beings be brought to peace.

Akṣobhya, the king who purifies existence and delusion,
The protector, made auspicious by the fullness of all qualities,
The auspiciousness of one whose mind is equal and attentive to all:
By that auspiciousness, may all beings be brought to peace.

The Buddha adorned with the ornaments of all excellent qualities,
Who unfailingly bestows the riches of true reality,
The auspiciousness of the one called Ratnasambhava, the Source of Jewels:
By that auspiciousness, may all beings be brought to peace.

The abode of meditative concentration, absorption, and sublime wisdom, gone to the other shore,
Seated upon the lotus of wisdom, Amitāyus, the Lord of Limitless Life,
The auspiciousness of bestowing a life without measure:
By that auspiciousness, may all beings be brought to peace.

Who directs the mind toward acting for the benefit of sentient beings,
Who frees all beings from the bonds that bind them,
The auspiciousness of the supreme Sage, Amoghasiddhi, the Accomplisher of Aims:
By that auspiciousness, may all beings be brought to peace.
