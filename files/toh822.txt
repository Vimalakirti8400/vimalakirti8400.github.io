Homage to the Three Jewels.

Vairocana, possessed of sacred wisdom vast as a great ocean,
whose body is like the full moon, who has reached the farthest shore of existence—
whatever auspiciousness lies in the complete fulfillment of all his intentions,
by that auspiciousness may all beings be brought to peace.

Akṣobhya, the Unshakeable King, who thoroughly purifies existence and delusion,
the Protector, auspicious through the perfect completion of all qualities—
whatever auspiciousness lies in possessing a mind of equal, virtuous intent,
by that auspiciousness may all beings be brought to peace.

Adorned with the ornament of all a buddha's qualities,
unfailingly bestowing the riches of genuine truth—
whatever auspiciousness lies in the one called Ratnasambhava, Source of Jewels,
by that auspiciousness may all beings be brought to peace.

The abode of the perfection of the sacred wisdom of meditative concentration and absorption,
seated upon a lotus of wisdom, possessing immeasurable life—
whatever auspiciousness lies in his blessing beings with countless lifespans,
by that auspiciousness may all beings be brought to peace.

Turning his mind to acts for the benefit of sentient beings,
freeing all migrators from the bonds that bind them—
whatever auspiciousness lies in the supreme sage Amoghasiddhi, Unfailing Accomplishment,
by that auspiciousness may all beings be brought to peace.
