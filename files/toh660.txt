Homage to the noble Blessed Lady, Mother of the Planetary Spirits.

Thus did I hear. At one time, the Blessed One was in the great city of the pastoral lands, praised by a great multitude of gods (devas), nāgas, yakṣas, rākṣasas, gandharvas, asuras, garuḍas, kinnaras, great mahoragas, māras, Yama the Lord of Death, the Sun, the Moon, Mars (the Red-eyed One), Jupiter (the Remaining Planet), Saturn, Venus (the Pure One), Mercury, Rāhu, the Comet (the Long-tailed One), and the twenty-eight lunar mansions, and many others. Seated upon a lion throne consecrated by the great vajra ornament of sacred commitment, together with many bodhisattvas, namely: the bodhisattva mahāsattva Vajrapāṇi, the bodhisattva mahāsattva Vajra the Fierce, the bodhisattva mahāsattva Vajra Host, the bodhisattva mahāsattva Vajra Bow, the bodhisattva mahāsattva Lord of the Vajra, the bodhisattva mahāsattva Vajra Ornament, the bodhisattva mahāsattva Vajra Light, the bodhisattva mahāsattva noble Avalokiteśvara, the bodhisattva mahāsattva the All-Seeing, the bodhisattva mahāsattva Glory of the World, the bodhisattva mahāsattva Lotus Crest, the bodhisattva mahāsattva Fully Bloomed Face, the bodhisattva mahāsattva Lotus Essence, the bodhisattva mahāsattva Lotus Eyes, the bodhisattva mahāsattva Mañjuśrī, the bodhisattva mahāsattva Maitreya, and many other such bodhisattvas — surrounded by and arrayed before hundreds of assemblies of bodhisattvas — he taught the Dharma. He taught the Dharma called "The Wish-fulfilling Jewel, the Ornament of the Great Array," good at the beginning, good in the middle, good at the end, excellent in meaning, excellent in word, unmixed, perfectly pure, and perfectly refined.

Then, through the blessing of the buddhas, Vajrapāṇi gazed upon that assembly, rose up, circumambulated the Blessed One many thousands of times through the blessing of his own miraculous power, and paid homage. He then seated himself with dignity and composure in the cross-legged posture before the Blessed One, gazed upon the assembly, joined his vajra-palms together at his own heart, and addressed the Blessed One with these words:

Blessed One, the planetary spirits are beings of fierce and ferocious form, beings of violent and savage form, beings of wrathful and angry mind, who inflict harm and injury upon sentient beings and rob them of their vitality. Some rob beings of their wealth and possessions. Some rob them of their very life. Some sentient beings who would live long lives they cause to die young. Thus do they inflict harm upon sentient beings. Therefore I ask the Blessed One to please teach a category of Dharma by which sentient beings may be protected.

The Blessed One declared: Vajrapāṇi, it is excellent, truly excellent, that out of compassion and for the benefit of sentient beings you ask the Tathāgata concerning this great secret, supremely secret teaching. I will explain it. Listen, retain it well, and take it to heart.

Concerning the planetary spirits of fierce and terrifying form, I shall teach the making of excellent offerings and good recitation — a secret that is more secret than secret.

They are honored by offerings, and when harm is done, they in turn cause harm. The colors of each of those planetary spirits, what pleases them, and the secret mantras and offerings that pacify the fierce and greatly majestic wrath of the gods, asuras, kinnaras (half-human spirit beings), nāgas, yakṣas, rākṣasas (flesh-eating demons), vetālas (reanimating spirits), humans, and others — all of these I shall set forth in due order.

Then from the very heart of the Blessed One Śākyamuni himself there blazed forth a ray of light called "Display of Compassion," which entered the crown of the heads of the planetary spirits. In that very instant the planetary spirits, beginning with the Sun, rose up and addressed the Blessed One with these words:

"Blessed One, Tathāgata, arhat, perfectly and completely awakened Buddha — you have appointed us as physicians for beings. We beseech the Blessed One to teach us a category of Dharma: namely, those who recite the Dharma that gathers us together and makes us useful — please teach what it is that will protect them, fully guard them, bring them peace and well-being, perform the rites of propitiation, avert punishments, turn away weapons, neutralize poison, kill poison, define boundaries, and bind the ground."

Then the Blessed One, the Tathāgata, arhat, perfectly and completely awakened Buddha Śākyamuni, taught the offerings and secret mantras for the planetary spirits.

According to the proper sequential arrangement of colors and directions, one makes a fragrant maṇḍala and draws petals of a lotus measuring twelve finger-widths, with a hub also twelve finger-widths across, beautifully adorned with four gates in due order. Upon the open lotus one is to visualize the god the Sun: his body is a mass of blazing glory like a great fire of ruby-red coals, his light equal to that of a thousand suns.

Now the food offerings for each planetary spirit are as follows: for the Sun, a porridge of milk; for the Moon, a curd-rice dish; for Mars, a food of red beans mixed with flesh; for Jupiter, a load of excellent butter; for Mercury, fresh milk; for Venus, a load of curd; for Saturn, a food of black beans mixed with flesh blended with mung beans; for Rāhu, a sesame porridge; for Ketu, a sesame porridge with sesame seeds; and a relish of molasses.

The offering cake (torma) for the yakṣas is the curd-rice dish of Dhṛtarāṣṭra. For Virūḍhaka: curd with a food of beans mixed with flesh. For Virūpākṣa: a food of milk-porridge.

For Vaiśravaṇa: a food of curd of poor creature mixed with beans and flesh, blended with molasses and beer.

Flowers of the appropriate color for each in sequence are to be offered. Incense of the appropriate color for each in sequence is to be offered. Banners of the appropriate color for each in sequence are to be offered. A lamp is to be offered to each one individually. Offerings are to be made in accordance with the proper sequence.

A conch-shell bowl is to be filled with butter and honey, the five precious substances placed within it, a water offering presented, and the front face bound with a white silk scarf.

Vajrapāṇi, the following are the mantras that accomplish results when recited — the essence mantras of these planetary spirits. In the center of a maṇḍala of incense twelve finger-breadths across, made of incense matching the appropriate color for each in sequence, the offerings are to be made. Offering water is to be presented in a clay vessel, or copper or similar, or a vessel of gold or silver, and for each one individually the mantra is to be recited one hundred and eight times.

Vajrapāṇi, after that, the words of the dhāraṇī mantra called the Mother of the Planetary Spirits are then to be recited seven times. After that, all the planetary spirits — the Sun and the rest — will protect one. They will free one from the poverty of demonic possession. Those whose lifespan has been exhausted will come to live long.

Vajrapāṇi, furthermore, whatever monks, nuns, male lay practitioners, or female lay practitioners, or other sentient beings belonging to any birth among sentient beings, should hear this in the range of their ears — they too will not come to make a time of death at an untimely hour.

Vajrapāṇi, furthermore, whoever makes offerings in the center of the maṇḍala of the planetary spirits and recites and teaches the Dharma from this text day by day — all the planetary spirits will, in every respect, through their own selves, fulfill all that person's wishes completely. They will also bring an end to the poverty of that person's family line.

Vajrapāṇi, these words of the dhāraṇī mantra called the Mother of the Planetary Spirits — beginning from the seventh day of the waxing fortnight of the month of the Pleiades in the autumn, the last month of autumn —

— having taken the restoration ceremony (poṣadha), one should make offerings and recite it every single day up to the fourteenth day of the fortnight; and then, from that time onward, should one read it continuously day and night in the month of Pisces, no danger of death will arise for that person for up to ninety-nine years. The danger of falling meteors and the danger of being afflicted by planetary spirits and wandering stars will not arise. One will come to remember one's past lives. All the planetary spirits will make offerings to that person. The planetary spirits will bestow upon that person the supreme of whatever one wishes. Thereupon those planetary spirits said "Excellent!" to the Blessed One, and then vanished from sight.

The dhāraṇī called the Noble Mother of the Planetary Spirits is complete.
