The Essence of the Noble Great Peacock (Āryamahāmayūrī-hṛdaya)

Homage to the Three Jewels. Homage to the unobstructed Teacher of all buddhas.

Oṃ piśuni svāhā.

Whoever holds this will come to hold the essence of the noble Great Peacock. The essence of the noble Great Peacock is complete.

Homage to the Three Jewels.

Namaḥ samanta-buddhānāṃ apratihata-śāsanānāṃ. Oṃ maṇi-dhari vajriṇi mahā-pratisare hūṃ hūṃ phaṭ phaṭ svāhā.

Whoever holds this will come to hold the noble great queen of wisdom, Mahāpratisarā. The dhāraṇī that enables one to come to hold the noble great Mahāpratisarā is complete.
