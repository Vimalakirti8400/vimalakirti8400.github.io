Homage to the seven fully and perfectly awakened buddhas together with their śrāvaka Saṅghas.

Victorious over all enemies and every fear,
whose vision is vast and whose majesty is great,
whose miraculous power is the very crown of his being,
with the wisdom that protects all,
with the power that dispels wrong faith,
with the training of Kanakamunithe Golden Sage,
with the qualities of Kāśyapa,
and with the diligence of Śākyamuni,
may I always dwell in well-being.

Thus: jayanti, vijayanti, jaye vijayan —
