Svāhā. Looking toward the sun, recite this mantra seven times over water and scatter it in the ten directions; one will then be protected. This completes the dhāraṇī for protecting oneself.

The noble one called "Pleasing to the Mind."

Homage to the Buddha, the Blessed One.

Namo bhagavate. Uṣṇīṣāya dhāre dharaṇatiye svāhā.

It succeeds merely by being recited, even without a formal practice of accomplishment. If one recites it one hundred and eight times over gorocana and marks the forehead with a dot, one becomes pleasing to the minds of all. If one recites the mantra over tītsala and applies it to the eyes, whomever one looks upon will be agreeable and meet one's gaze. If one recites it over water and drinks it, others will give one food and will feel affection and wish for one's company.

This completes the dhāraṇī of "Pleasing to the Mind."
