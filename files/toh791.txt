Svāhā. Facing the sun, having cast this mantra upon water seven times, if one scatters it in the ten directions, one will be protected. The dhāraṇī for protecting oneself is complete.

The noble [dhāraṇī] called Pleasing to the Mind.

Homage to the Buddha, the Blessed One.

Namo bhagavate uṣṇīṣāya, dhāre dharaṇatiye svāhā.

Even without formal accomplishment, it succeeds merely through recitation. If it is recited one hundred and eight times over bezoar (gi wang) and a dot is placed with it upon the forehead, one becomes pleasing to the minds of all. If the mantra is recited over collyrium and applied to the eyes, then whomever one looks upon, the meeting of gazes will be harmonious. If it is recited over water and that water is drunk, others will feel a heartfelt wish to give one food.

The dhāraṇī Pleasing to the Mind is complete.
