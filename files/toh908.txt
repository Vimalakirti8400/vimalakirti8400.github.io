The Dhāraṇī Called "Compassion Without Delay"

Compassion Without Delay, known in Tibetan as Snying rjes mi bshol ba, is a short dhāraṇī devoted to the bodhisattva Avalokiteśvara, the great being renowned for his boundless compassion. It opens with homage to the Three Jewels, to seventy million perfect buddhas, and to Avalokiteśvara as the one who frees beings from harm, pacifies illness, turns back hostile spirits, and rescues those caught in the eight unfavorable states. It then presents a mantra of rhythmic, repeating syllables, together with heartfelt petitions asking the Great Compassionate One to keep the reciter in mind and to act swiftly on their behalf. This work is Toh 908 in the Kangyur, where it belongs among the dhāraṇī texts, the collection of sacred incantations recited for protection, healing, and blessing.

In the Tibetan language: The Dhāraṇī Called "Compassion Without Delay."

Homage to the Three Jewels!
Homage to the seventy million perfect buddhas!
Homage to the bodhisattva, the great being, noble Avalokiteśvara, possessor of great compassion, who frees from all harm, who completely pacifies all illness, who decisively turns back all bhūtas, and who truly rescues sentient beings fallen into the eight unfavorable states!

Having paid homage to him, I shall recite this dhāraṇī called "Great Compassion Without Delay," spoken by noble Avalokiteśvara:

You who possess great compassion, please keep me in mind!

By the truth of the Buddha, the truth of the Dharma, and the truth of the Saṅgha, please arise, please arise! With great loving-kindness, please turn back all bhūtas! With the cloud of great compassion, please completely pacify all my illnesses!

Protect me from all fears, all harms, and all illnesses! svāhā

The dhāraṇī called "Compassion Without Delay" is complete.
