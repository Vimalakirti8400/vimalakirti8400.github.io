Homage to the Three Jewels. Homage to Maitreya, the Victor.

Namo ratnatrayāya. Namo bhagavate Śākyamunaye tathāgatāya arhate samyaksaṃbuddhāya. Tadyathā: Oṃ ajite ajite aparājite ajitañjaya hara hara maitry avalokite kara kara mahāsamayasiddhe bhara bhara mahābodhimaṇḍavīje smara smara asmākaṃ samaya bodhi bodhi mahābodhi svāhā. This is the root mantra.

Oṃ mohi mohi mahāmohi svāhā. This is the heart.

Oṃ muni muni smara svāhā. This is the close heart.

By merely hearing this dhāraṇī, or reading it, or reciting it, or reflecting on it with proper attention, or merely meditating upon it — at that time when I myself become fully and perfectly awakened in unsurpassed, perfect, and complete awakening, I will unfailingly seek out that sentient being and bestow upon them a prophecy of unsurpassed, perfect, and complete awakening. Even if it is proclaimed into the ears of deer and birds born in the animal realm, they too shall receive a prophecy of unsurpassed, perfect, and complete awakening.

By merely hearing this, one will never again be reborn in the lower realms. One will not be stained by the defilements of the lower realms. One will not be born in a mother's womb. For a thousand divine aeons one will become a wheel-turning king. One will come to possess the path of the ten virtuous actions. Whatever one wishes and seeks, one's wealth and prosperity will be accomplished.

O Blessed One, I will not abandon those sentient beings. Definitely, when I have reached the heart of awakening, no matter what circumstances arise, I will bestow upon them a prophecy of unsurpassed, perfect, and complete awakening.
