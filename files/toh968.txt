The Noble Victorious Averter (Āryavijayavatīnāmapratyaṅgirā)

The Noble Victorious Averter, known in Sanskrit as Āryavijayavatī nāma Pratyaṅgirā, is a short protective scripture whose Tibetan title carries the same meaning of a victorious spell that turns back harm. It opens with homage to all the buddhas and bodhisattvas who strive to protect beings, to the accomplished ones and the holders of spell-mantras, to the Three Jewels, and to the great compassionate bodhisattva Avalokiteśvara. It then calls for recitation of the Victorious Averter, which is said to dispel killers, sorcery, and vetāla spirits, to pacify what is inauspicious, and to stupefy and paralyze the dangers posed by kings, thieves, fire, water, ḍākinīs, pretas, piśācas, and other hostile forces. The work belongs to the class of dhāraṇī and averting texts, recitations meant to ward off misfortune and malevolent influences, and it is catalogued as Toh 968 in the Kangyur, where it appears among the collected dhāraṇīs.

In the language of Tibet: The Noble Victorious Averter.

Homage to all the buddhas and bodhisattvas who earnestly strive to protect all beings. Homage to all the accomplished ones (siddhas). Homage to all the holders of vidyā-mantras. Homage to the Buddha. Homage to the Dharma. Homage to the Saṅgha. Homage to the noble Avalokiteśvara, the bodhisattva, the great being, endowed with great compassion.

Having paid homage to them, one should recite the one called the Victorious Averter. It dispels killers and sorcery, and vetālas, and fully pacifies what is inauspicious. It stupefies, paralyzes and bewilders the fear of kings, the fear of thieves, the fear of fire, the fear of water, the fear of ḍākinīs, the fear of pretas, piśācas and kumbhāṇḍas, the fear of shadow-oppressors, the fear of apasmāras, the fear of pūtanas, the fear of kaṭapūtanas, the fear of going astray and of setting out on a journey, and the fear of the various beings that roam by day and by night. It makes the complexion fair and fully increases joy. It repels all enemies, fully pacifies all obstructors and vināyakas, and thoroughly purifies all wrongdoing. It stupefies the mouths of others, paralyzes their mouths, and makes one's own face beautiful.

Homage to the noble Avalokiteśvara, the bodhisattva, the great being, endowed with great compassion. May these bases of secret mantra be accomplished!

This Victorious Averter,
Proclaimed as the source of victory:
Whoever wears it at the neck
Will surely be victorious.
Wrongdoing, bad dreams, the inauspicious,
