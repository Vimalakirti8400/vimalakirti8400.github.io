They are as follows: the adornment of not forgetting the awakening mind; the adornment of searching out all learning without ever being satisfied; the adornment of determining the profound Dharma through dependent origination; the adornment of knowing the superior and inferior faculties of all sentient beings; the adornment of examining and analyzing with proper discernment the teachings one has heard and understood; the adornment of not apprehending any phenomenon as a fixed referent, so as to fully ripen all sentient beings;
