Dhāraṇī of the Clouds of Offerings

This brief text, Toh 1072 in the Kangyur, is a short dhāraṇī whose title is simply its opening mantra, beginning "namaḥ sarva buddha bodhisattvānāṃ." It is often described as a mantra for producing clouds of offerings, though no separate formal title in Sanskrit or Tibetan can be confidently determined. The text presents a single Sanskrit formula addressed to all buddhas and bodhisattvas and explains that reciting it eight times causes vast clouds of offerings to arise, filling the expanse of space. It thus serves as a practical aid to devotion, allowing a practitioner to multiply ritual offerings to the awakened ones through recitation alone. It belongs to the collection of dhāraṇīs and related incantations within the tantra section of the Kangyur, where many such short ritual formulas are preserved.

By reciting namaḥ sarba buddha bodhi satvānāṃ sarbathā utgate spharaṇa imaṃ gaganakhaṃ samanta svāhā eight times, clouds of offerings arise.
