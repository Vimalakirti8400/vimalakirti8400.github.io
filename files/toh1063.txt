The Noble Great Cloud (Āryamahāmegha)

The Noble Great Cloud, known in Sanskrit as Āryamahāmegha and in Tibetan as Pakpa Trinchenpo, is a Buddhist scripture set in the palace of the nāga kings Nanda and Upananda, where the Buddha teaches before a vast assembly of monks, bodhisattvas, and a long roll of serpent kings who are named one by one. Because the nāgas are traditionally associated with water and weather, the work belongs to a family of texts concerned with bringing timely rain, relieving drought, and ensuring the welfare of the land and its people, and it offers protective formulas and ritual instructions for that purpose. It is Toh 1063 in the Kangyur, where it is placed among the dhāraṇī texts, the collection of incantations and protective formulas associated with the tantra section of the canon.

In the language of India: ārya mahā megha. In the language of Tibet: The Noble Great Cloud.

Homage to all the buddhas and bodhisattvas.

Thus did I hear at one time. The Blessed One was dwelling in the abode of the nāga kings Nanda and Upananda, in the palace of the Great Cloud Maṇḍala, the Essence of the Glory of Precious Jewels. He was together with a great Saṅgha of monks, a great Saṅgha of bodhisattvas, and a great assembly of nāga kings. These great nāga kings and others, eighty-four hundred thousand koṭis of niyutas of nāgas in all, had gathered and were seated there with him.

At that time those nāga kings, together with their retinues, rose from their seats, draped their upper robes over one shoulder, and placed their right knees on the ground. Bowing toward the Blessed One with joined palms, with reverence for the teacher and with great devotion, they offered to the Blessed One countless, immeasurable, supremely beautiful offerings of every kind: flowers, incense, perfumes, garlands, ointments, powders, garments, parasols, victory banners, pennants, silken tassels, the sound of music, cymbals, clapping, songs, jewel flowers, jewel tassels, strings of pearls, nāga flowers, and nets of pearls. Having offered these, they circumambulated him and sat down to one side.

Having sat down to one side, they made these aspirations. They thought: "We shall create bodies as numerous as the atoms in all the oceans of world systems. In all the oceans of maṇḍalas of the assemblies of buddhas and bodhisattvas, in all the vast oceans of world systems, among the atoms of all earth, water, fire, and wind, and on every single atom of all that appears as form, we shall create oceans of clouds of bodies. They will pass beyond every ocean of counting, beyond the countless, the immeasurable, the inconceivable, the incomparable, the unfathomable, and the inexpressible. On each of these bodies we shall create immeasurable, countless oceans of clouds of hands, reaching out in streams toward every direction. And from every part of every atom, with distinct oceans of clouds of every kind of offering, filling the ground of all directions far and wide, we shall make offerings to all the buddhas and bodhisattvas."

That is to say: "By our power we shall cover the whole expanse of the sky with distinct oceans of clouds of bodies of all-pervading conduct, immeasurable, countless, inconceivable, unfathomable, limitless, and inexpressible. In the same way as with the oceans of clouds of bodhisattva bodies, we shall cover the whole expanse of the sky with oceans of clouds of maṇḍalas of retinues of bodies that shine with the colors and dense rays of all jewels and are like all suns and moons; with oceans of clouds of garlands of all jewels and of flowers; with oceans of clouds of multistoried pavilions whose core is the great light of all jewels; with oceans of clouds of treasuries of powders and of all trees; with oceans of clouds displaying every form of perfume and incense; with oceans of clouds of the sound of music proclaiming every sound; and with oceans of clouds of trees of every fragrance. With these and other distinct oceans of clouds of every kind of offering, immeasurable, countless, inconceivable, unfathomable, limitless, and inexpressible, we shall honor all the oceans of buddhas and bodhisattvas. We shall revere them. We shall esteem them. We shall worship them.

"By our power we shall cover the whole expanse of the sky with oceans of clouds of kings of jewels whose essence illuminates the arrays in every land, and we shall honor all the oceans of buddhas and bodhisattvas. We shall revere them. We shall esteem them. We shall worship them.

"We shall cover the whole expanse of the sky with oceans of clouds of kings of jewels adorned with a rain of precious jewels that illuminate everything; with oceans of clouds of kings of jewels ablaze with the radiant light of all jewels, whose melodies are wholly turned toward the buddhas; with oceans of clouds of kings of jewels that fill everything with sound, melodious with all the teachings of the buddhas; with oceans of clouds of jewel doors on every side whose rays of light make the emanations of the buddhas appear; with oceans of clouds of kings of jewels that display the light of every array set out in the distinct maṇḍalas of the assemblies; with oceans of clouds of kings of jewels that are radiant lamps reaching into every domain of the buddhas; with oceans of clouds of kings of jewels that display the light of the palaces of the tathāgatas in inconceivable buddha fields; and with oceans of clouds of kings of jewels that make the forms of the buddhas of the three times shine forth in the particles of every kind of jewel. By our power we shall cover the sky with these, and we shall honor all the oceans of buddhas and bodhisattvas. We shall revere them. We shall esteem them. We shall worship them.

"We shall cover the whole expanse of the sky with oceans of clouds of distinct multistoried pavilions made of every kind of jewel, perfume, and flower; with oceans of clouds of multistoried pavilions adorned with every jewel king of boundless color; with oceans of clouds of multistoried pavilions radiant with jewel lamps and with the light of perfumes; with oceans of clouds of multistoried pavilions ornamented with every kind of pearl; with oceans of clouds of the stamens of every flower; and with oceans of clouds of multistoried pavilions beautifully adorned with boundless jewel garlands. And then we shall cover the whole expanse of the sky with oceans of clouds of multistoried pavilions in which every array gathers together, whose core is light shining in all ten directions; with oceans of clouds of multistoried pavilions adorned with the dust of all jewels, displaying many arrays; with oceans of clouds of multistoried pavilions adorned with every array, filling everything far and wide; and with oceans of clouds of gateways on every side with flower archways hung with nets of bells. By our power we shall cover the sky with these, and we shall honor all the oceans of buddhas and bodhisattvas. We shall revere them. We shall esteem them. We shall worship them."

"By our power we shall cover the whole expanse of the sky with ocean-clouds of lion thrones: ocean-clouds of lion thrones whose core is gold, clusters of various jewels, garlands and jeweled necklaces; ocean-clouds of lion thrones whose core is varied like the radiance of flowers; ocean-clouds of lion thrones whose core is a variety of lotuses of sapphire and of gold from the Jambū River; ocean-clouds of lion thrones whose core is a lotus of precious jewel lamps; ocean-clouds of lion thrones whose core is a variety of lotuses bearing banners of brilliantly shining precious jewels; ocean-clouds of lion thrones whose core is a variety of lotuses adorned with jewels; ocean-clouds of lion thrones whose core is a lotus with rays of light blazing beautifully with sapphire; ocean-clouds of lion thrones whose core is a lotus with the splendor of inexhaustible blazing rays of light; ocean-clouds of lion thrones whose core is a lotus from which all things arise as blazing jewels; ocean-clouds of lion thrones whose core is a lotus from which arise all the supreme and excellent blazing jewels; and distinct ocean-clouds of lion thrones whose core is a lotus bearing rays of light that are the sound of the Buddha's speech. With these we shall honor all the oceans of buddhas and bodhisattvas. We shall revere them. We shall esteem them. We shall worship them.

"By our power we shall cover the whole expanse of the sky with ocean-clouds of trees and of lion thrones: ocean-clouds of trees of incense and of every kind of jewel; ocean-clouds of incense trees from whose gateways and leaves on every side arise clouds of palms joined in reverence; ocean-clouds of trees that display everywhere arrays of the boundless forms of all trees; ocean-clouds like trees from which hang clouds of every kind of flower; ocean-clouds of trees possessing boundless treasuries from which every array arises; ocean-clouds of trees that illuminate the maṇḍala of all radiant jewels; ocean-clouds of trees that display everywhere the half-bodies of bodhisattvas in the powder of every kind of sandalwood; ocean-clouds of trees possessing treasuries of every precious garment, made to shine like the sun, from trees whose light is the inconceivable array of every seat of awakening; With these we shall honor all the buddhas and bodhisattvas, vast oceans of them like ocean-clouds. We shall revere them. We shall esteem them. We shall worship them.

...one should bless oceans of clouds of parasols made of every kind of precious jewel so that they cover the whole expanse of the sky. With oceans of clouds of flower parasols arrayed with the radiant light of the ten precious substances, oceans of clouds of parasols that are treasuries of pearls of infinite colors, oceans of clouds of parasols of jewel-kings that sound forth the gateway of the compassion of all buddhas and bodhisattvas, oceans of clouds of parasols garlanded with the streaming light of various jewels, oceans of clouds of parasols hung with nets of bells richly adorned with all-illuminating jewel dust, oceans of clouds of parasols adorned with trunks and branches made of every jewel, oceans of clouds of parasols of jewel-kings streaming light that illuminates like the sun, and of every perfume and incense, oceans of clouds of parasols that fill everything with treasuries of sandalwood powder, and distinct oceans of clouds of parasols that fill everything with all-pervading arrays illuminating the great domain of the buddhas, one should honor, revere, respect, and worship all the oceans of buddhas and bodhisattvas.

Having made such aspirations, those nāga kings again circumambulated the Blessed One three times and bowed at his feet, and, with the Blessed One's permission, each sat down on his own seat.

At that time the great lord of nāgas of the great trichiliocosm, called Parasol-Like King of the Splendid Maṇḍala of Ocean-Cloud Arrays with an Endless Retinue, who possessed the power of an irreversible aspiration, came to this Jambudvīpa in order to make offerings to the Tathāgata, to pay homage, to honor and serve him, and to listen to the Dharma. Then the lord of nāgas Parasol-Like King of the Splendid Maṇḍala of Ocean-Cloud Arrays with an Endless Retinue rose from his seat, draped his upper robe over one shoulder, and placed his right knee on the ground. He bowed with palms joined toward the Blessed One and addressed him with these words: "If the Blessed One will give me leave to ask a question and will answer what I ask, I would ask the Blessed Tathāgata, the arhat, the perfectly complete Buddha, about a certain matter."

When he had said this, the Blessed One spoke to the lord of nāgas Parasol-Like King of the Splendid Maṇḍala of Ocean-Cloud Arrays with an Endless Retinue: "Lord of nāgas, ask whatever you wish, and I shall satisfy your mind by answering each thing you ask."

When he had spoken, the lord of nāgas Parasol-Like King of the Splendid Maṇḍala of Ocean-Cloud Arrays with an Endless Retinue said this to the Blessed One: "Blessed One, how may all the nāga sufferings of all nāgas be cut off? How may they come to have every happiness and great joy? How may a stream of rain fall on this Jambudvīpa at the proper times? How may all grasses, shrubs, medicinal plants and forests grow? How may all grains sprout, and all nourishing essences properly arise, so that the people of Jambudvīpa come to have happiness?"

When he had asked this, the Blessed One spoke to the lord of nāgas Parasol-Like King of the Splendid Maṇḍala of Ocean-Cloud Arrays with an Endless Retinue: "Lord of nāgas, you have set out for the benefit and happiness of all sentient beings, and you think to question the Tathāgata fully on this matter. Excellent, excellent! Therefore, lord of nāgas, listen well and carefully and keep it in mind, and I shall explain it to you.

"Lord of nāgas, if one possesses a single quality, all the nāga sufferings of all nāgas will be cut off and they will come to have happiness. What is that single quality? It is loving-kindness. Lord of nāgas, gods and humans who abide in loving-kindness are not burned by fire, not wounded by weapons, not carried off by water, and not harmed by poison. They are not overpowered by hostile armies. They fall asleep in happiness and wake in happiness. They are protected by their own merit. Through the splendor of great merit they become radiant. They are not overcome by the world with its gods. They become beautiful and delightful to behold. They become unobstructed in every destiny. All their sufferings are cut off and they become greatly joyful. They come to have every happiness, and having realized the superhuman Dharma, after the body breaks up they are reborn in the world of Brahmā. Lord of nāgas, these are the benefits for gods and humans who abide in loving-kindness. Therefore, lord of nāgas, one should abide in loving acts of body, loving acts of speech, and loving acts of mind.

"Furthermore, lord of nāgas, one should recite the dhāraṇī called Giver of All Happiness. It cuts off all the nāga sufferings of all nāgas and bestows every happiness. It makes a stream of rain fall on this Jambudvīpa at the proper times, and makes all grasses, shrubs, medicinal plants, forests and crops grow. Lord of nāgas, what is the dhāraṇī called Giver of All Happiness? It is this:

tadyathā dhāraṇī dhāraṇī uttāraṇi sampratiṣṭhite vijaya varṇa satyapratijñā saraha jñānapati uddevādani pināmani abhiṣecani abhivyāhāra śubhavati ajimatu ehi kumbalani vahahāra klaiśana dhunapāpaṃ śodhaya margaṇa niratika dharmataśuddha lokavitimiraracas duḥkhaśamana sarvabuddha avalokana adhiṣṭhite brajñājñānabhe svāhā

"Lord of nāgas, these words of the dhāraṇī, which accomplish every aim, which enter the dharmakāya, which are blessed by all the buddhas, and which bestow every happiness, should be retained and recited.

"Furthermore, lord of nāgas, one should retain and keep in mind the names of the tathāgatas who display emanations of the essence of splendor of the array of blessings arising from the family of clouds, who illuminate the golden light rays of the glorious cloud maṇḍala of the stream of water from the crest of wisdom, and whose clan and family arose from a single portion of the tip of a hair. Those names cut off all the nāga sufferings of all nāgas, all nāga families, all nāga clans, all nāga lineages, all nāga beings, all nāga places of birth, all nāga kings, all nāga daughters, and all nāga servants, and they bring about every requisite of happiness.

"Lord of nāgas, what are those names of the tathāgatas? They are these:

Lord of nāgas, by reciting these names of the tathāgatas, all the sufferings of all the nāgas are cut off, they come to possess every happiness, and here in Jambudvīpa streams of rain fall in their proper seasons, and grass, shrubs and trees, medicinal plants, forests and all crops are made to grow.

Then the lord of nāgas of the great trichiliocosm, the nāga king Parasol-like Maṇḍala of Splendor of the Ocean-Cloud Array with Endless Retinue, made this request to the Blessed One: "Blessed One, in later times, in the future age, please explain such bases of dhāraṇī secret mantra. When they are recited at times when rain does not fall here in Jambudvīpa, they cause great streams of rain to fall.
