The Dhāraṇī of the Noble Perfection of Wisdom in Eight Thousand Lines (Āryāṣṭasāhasrikāprajñāpāramitādhāraṇī)

The Dhāraṇī of the Noble Perfection of Wisdom in Eight Thousand Lines, known in Tibetan as Phakpa Sherab kyi Pharol tu Chinpa Gyaktongpé Zung and in Sanskrit approximately as Āryāṣṭasāhasrikāprajñāpāramitādhāraṇī, is a very brief work that distills one of the most celebrated Perfection of Wisdom sutras into a single incantation. After paying homage to the Blessed Mother, the Perfection of Wisdom, it presents a short mantra and explains that by retaining it one effectively retains the entire Perfection of Wisdom in Eight Thousand Lines. It further promises that the dhāraṇī averts every kind of evil omen and completely purifies karmic obscurations and the causes of rebirth in the lower realms. It is Toh 934 in the Kangyur, where it belongs to the class of dhāraṇī texts, the collection of short incantations valued for their protective and purifying power.

The Dhāraṇī of the Noble Perfection of Wisdom in Eight Thousand Lines

Homage to the Blessed Mother, the Perfection of Wisdom.

tadyathā oṃ hrīḥ śruti smṛti vijaye svāhā prajñāpāramitāyai sarva durgate śvadhaya rājāya svāhā

By retaining this, one will retain the Perfection of Wisdom in Eight Thousand Lines. It also averts every kind of evil omen. It will completely purify the obscurations of karma and all the lower realms.

This completes the Dhāraṇī of the Noble Perfection of Wisdom in Eight Thousand Lines.
