Homage to the Omniscient One.

A certain god approached the Guide of the World and said:
"Blessed One, I ask you this: by what means may one be always blessed with well-being?"

Having heard these words, the Guide of the World, the Holy One, gave this teaching:
"O god, listen well to how well-being comes about everywhere.

Those whose faith in the Tathāgata is unshakable, firmly and well established,
those who are virtuous and keep to moral discipline,
for them well-being comes everywhere.

Those who always serve and honor well
their father and mother, their sons and daughters, and their servants,
for them well-being comes everywhere.

Those who, with firm faith, make offerings to the renowned arhats
and who act in accord with the god's instruction,
for them well-being comes everywhere.

Those who are free of wrongdoing in body, speech, and mind,
those who have thoroughly restrained all three,
for them well-being comes everywhere.

Those who, toward all beings, boundlessly and without disturbance,
cultivate a mind of loving-kindness,
for them well-being comes everywhere.

Those who, in order to abandon all suffering,
well cultivate the eightfold noble path,
which leads to happiness,
for them well-being comes everywhere.

Those who always see well the emptiness of all conditioned things,
and who have become established in this within the mind,
for them well-being comes everywhere.

Those who, having directly realized the stainless nature of the body, go about freely,
arhats who possess a liberated mind,
for them well-being comes everywhere.

Those who are free from attachment, aversion, and delusion,
and free of clinging,
in whom undisturbed wisdom has arisen,
for them well-being comes everywhere.

Those who, as the protector of the world with its gods..."
