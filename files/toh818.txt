Verses of Auspiciousness (Svastyayanagāthā)

This short work, known in Sanskrit as the Svastyayanagāthā and in Tibetan as the Bde legs kyi tshigs su bcad pa, is a brief verse composition of auspicious blessing, offering words of good fortune and truth-based benediction in the manner traditional to Buddhist verses of svasti (well-being). Catalogued as Toh 818 in the Kangyur, it belongs to the general class of auspicious verses (maṅgala-gāthā) recited to invoke blessings and protection, often appended or used alongside other canonical texts for ceremonial purposes. Though brief, it exemplifies the genre of dedicatory and benedictory verse found throughout the Kangyur, affirming truth as the basis for excellent outcomes and closing with an aspiration for supreme well-being. Its concluding lines mark it explicitly as a completed set of verses of good fortune, underscoring its function as a ritual coda rather than a doctrinal treatise.
