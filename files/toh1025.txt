The Dhāraṇī That Pacifies Anger

The Dhāraṇī That Pacifies Anger, known in Tibetan as Khro ba zhi bar byed pa'i gzungs, is a very brief ritual text centered on a single mantra invoking the Blessed One to subdue, stupefy, and calm all wrath. After an opening homage to the Three Jewels, it gives the mantra and a simple instruction: sitting facing east, one recites it, wipes one's face seven times with one's own hand, and then enters a gathering, whereupon those who are angry will become joyful. Its practical aim is to dissolve hostility and bring harmony to social encounters. The work is Toh 1025 in the Kangyur, where it belongs among the collection of dhāraṇī texts, the short incantations and protective formulas preserved in the Tibetan canon.

Homage to the Three Jewels!

oṃ bagabace jaṃbhani stoṃbhani mohani sarbaroṣapraśamani svāhā

If one sits facing east, recites this mantra, wipes one's face seven times with one's own hand, and then goes into an assembly, those who are angry will become joyful.
