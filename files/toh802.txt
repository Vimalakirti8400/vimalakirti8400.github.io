The Dhāraṇī for Curing Phlegm Disorders

This very brief text, whose Tibetan title can be rendered as the dhāraṇī for curing phlegm disorders and relieving the vomiting of blood and piercing pain they cause, is a short healing dhāraṇī, a type of protective incantation used for curing illness. Homage to the Three Jewels.

The mantra for curing the illness of consumption (kṣaya):

Namo hetu heke svāhā.

When illness has arisen in the body, take a cooling water, and having sprinkled it over the afflicted person, recite the mantra sixty-one times, and then sweep it over that person's body. In this way the illness will be gone.

Thus ends the dhāraṇī for curing the illness of consumption.
