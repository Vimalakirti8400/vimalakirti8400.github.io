Homage to the Three Jewels.

The mantra for eliminating the disease of consumption (kṣaya):

Namo hetu heke svāhā.

If a disease has arisen in the body, if one has been struck by the wasting illness, sprinkle water with a cooling fan, recite the mantra sixty-one times, and then fan it onto that person's body — the disease will be cured.

The dhāraṇī for eliminating the disease of consumption is complete.
