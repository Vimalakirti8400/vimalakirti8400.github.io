Homage to the Three Jewels. Homage to the noble Avalokiteśvara, the bodhisattva, the great being, the one of great compassion. The dhāraṇī is as follows:

Oṃ akaṭe vikaṭe nikaṭe kataṃkaṭe karoṭe karoṭa vīrye svāhā.

Without the heavy soil in front of the Blessed One falling to the ground, one arranges eight maṇḍalas separately, and recites the mantra up to thirteen times over each maṇḍala. Then, with the remaining mantra recitation, one anoints the sick person nearby, and all illnesses will be pacified.

This will be accomplished in seven days, or thirteen days, or twenty-one days — provided one has not committed any of the five acts of immediate retribution (ānantarya). If it has not been accomplished in that way, it means that one has committed one of the five acts of immediate retribution.

The Dhāraṇī of the Lion's Roar is complete.
