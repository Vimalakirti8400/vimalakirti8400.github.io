The Dhāraṇī of the Noble Perfection of Wisdom in Twenty-Five Thousand Lines (Āryapañcaviṃśatisāhasrikāprajñāpāramitādhāraṇī)

The Dhāraṇī of the Noble Perfection of Wisdom in Twenty-Five Thousand Lines, known in Tibetan as Phakpa Sherab kyi Parol tu Chinpa Tongtrak Nyishu Ngapé Zung and in Sanskrit, by reconstruction, as Ārya Pañcaviṃśatisāhasrikā Prajñāpāramitā Dhāraṇī, is a very brief text centered on a single incantation. It opens with homage to the Blessed Mother, the Perfection of Wisdom, and then presents a dhāraṇī that invokes wisdom as the radiance that dispels the darkness obscuring all phenomena and calls upon the goddess to grant accomplishment. A short closing statement explains that retaining this formula is equivalent to retaining the entire Perfection of Wisdom in Twenty-Five Thousand Lines, and that it purifies all obscurations created by karma. The work is Toh 933 in the Kangyur, where it belongs to the collection of dhāraṇī texts, the class of short scriptures devoted to sacred spells for recitation and protection.

tadyathā oṃ prajñā prajñe prajñā apabhāse prajñā valokati sarva dharma andhakāra vidhamani siddhe susiddhe siddhyantu māṃ bhagavati sarva jñāna sandhari bhagavati vacchela prasaradhehasta mama sukara siddhi siddhi buddha buddha kaṃpa kaṃpa pāla pāla dhara dhara vara vara garja garja āgaccha āgaccha bhagavati māvilaṃba svāhā

By retaining this, one comes to retain the Perfection of Wisdom in Twenty-Five Thousand Lines, and all obscurations of karma are purified.

This completes the Dhāraṇī of the Noble Perfection of Wisdom in Twenty-Five Thousand Lines.
