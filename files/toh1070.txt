Homage to All Buddhas and Bodhisattvas

This brief text, Toh 1070 in the Kangyur, is known only by its opening words, a Sanskrit invocation beginning "namaḥ sarva," meaning "homage to all," and no fuller English, Sanskrit, or Tibetan title can be determined with confidence. It is a short mantra of praise addressed to all the buddhas and bodhisattvas, saluting them as those whose higher knowledge has blossomed in every direction, and closing with the words "homage to you" and the ritual exclamation svāhā. The only instruction accompanying the mantra is that reciting it eight times constitutes the act of praise, so the whole text is a compact devotional formula rather than a discourse or narrative.
