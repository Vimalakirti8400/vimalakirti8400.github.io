The Noble Dhāraṇī Called Endowed with Wealth (Āryahiraṇyavatīnāmadhāraṇī)

The Noble Dhāraṇī Called Endowed with Wealth, known in Sanskrit as the Ārya Hiraṇyaparī Nāma Dhāraṇī, is a short scripture centered on a dhāraṇī, a sacred formula believed to hold and convey spiritual power when it is retained and recited. Its setting is Tuṣita heaven, where the Blessed One sits on a stone slab white as wool among many thousands of bodhisattvas, each of whom has been prophesied to perfect awakening and is only one birth away from it. Addressing the bodhisattva Supratiṣṭhita, the Buddha teaches that whoever holds this dhāraṇī will see, hear, and experience the tathāgatas of the past, present, and future, and will gain the spiritual attainments that follow from that encounter. The text is Toh 991 in the Kangyur, where it belongs among the dhāraṇī texts, the class of works devoted to such powerful formulas and the benefits of preserving and reciting them.

In the language of India: ārya hiraṇyaparī nāma dhāraṇī
In the language of Tibet: The Noble Dhāraṇī Called Endowed with Wealth.

Homage to all the buddhas and bodhisattvas.

Thus did I hear at one time. The Blessed One was dwelling in Tuṣita heaven, on a stone slab like white wool, together with many thousands of bodhisattvas. All of them had been prophesied to unsurpassed, perfect awakening, and every one of them was bound to just one more birth.

Then the Blessed One said to the bodhisattva, the great being Supratiṣṭhita: "Supratiṣṭhita, any bodhisattva great being who wishes to hold this Dhāraṇī Endowed with Wealth sees the tathāgatas of the past, the future and the present. He hears them. He experiences them. Whoever sees, hears and experiences the tathāgatas in this way attains the dhāraṇī.

"Why is that? Because, Supratiṣṭhita, the bodhisattva great being does not grasp at the thought, 'I have made merit.' He does not grasp at the thought, 'I have done what is not merit, what is virtuous, what is nonvirtuous.'

"Why is that? Because, Supratiṣṭhita, the bodhisattva great being does not conceive of holding the dhāraṇī. He does not discriminate it. The bodhisattva great being who has attained the dhāraṇī does not conceive of past, future and present phenomena as things or as characteristics. He does not conceive of them at all: not as conditions, not as without conditions; not as going, not as without going; not as passing at death, not as birth; not as empty, not as not empty; not as signs, not as signless; not as wishes, not as wishless; not as arising, not as ceasing.

"Supratiṣṭhita, the bodhisattva great being who practices within this limit is said to abide in wisdom. When he has come to abide in wisdom, that is called nirvāṇa. When he has passed into nirvāṇa, he takes up the Dhāraṇī Endowed with Wealth, comprehends it completely and holds it, and its meanings and syllables are never exhausted.

"He is also one whose moral discipline is free of conceptual examination: he possesses true moral discipline and pure moral discipline. He is one whose qualities are free of conceptual examination: he possesses true Dharma and pure Dharma. He does not depend on others, and he has entered into his own Dharma. He has done what is to be done above all for the Buddha. He has entered upon the proper rite.

"Supratiṣṭhita, in this way the bodhisattva great being is not attached. He does not cling. He does not let go. Supratiṣṭhita, when the bodhisattva great being is not attached, is not cut off and does not let go, then, through the family of the buddhas and through the bases of secret mantra that are the other names of the vidyā-mantras of the buddhas, the blessed ones of the past, the future and the present, he attains the Dhāraṇī Endowed with Wealth:

tadyathā kuṭi kuṭi kucala elā imirito kothāla ireri imiriṇi

"Supratiṣṭhita, these are the other names of the vidyā-mantras of the buddhas, the blessed ones of the past. They are the other names of the vidyā-mantras of the buddhas, the blessed ones of the future and the present.

"Supratiṣṭhita, this Dhāraṇī Endowed with Wealth is the mother of the buddhas, the blessed ones of the past, the future and the present. Why is that? Because it does not abide in the object of the past, and it does not abide in the object of the future or the present.

"Supratiṣṭhita, any bodhisattva great being who holds this Dhāraṇī Endowed with Wealth has made offerings and done service to countless buddhas, blessed ones. He has been proclaimed and made to understand by countless buddhas, blessed ones. He has been prophesied to unsurpassed, perfect awakening by countless buddhas, blessed ones.

"Supratiṣṭhita, the bodhisattva great being who holds this Dhāraṇī Endowed with Wealth should know it, thinking: 'I have pleased countless buddhas, blessed ones, that is, the wisdom of the buddhas. I have done them service and made them offerings. I have generated countless merit.'

"Whoever holds this Dhāraṇī Endowed with Wealth, with whatever intention, that very thing will come to him. For one who wants wisdom, wisdom will come; for one who wants dhāraṇī, dhāraṇī; for one who wants wealth, wealth; and for one who wants learning, learning will come.

"This is its rite. During the waxing fortnight of the moon, at sunrise, one should eat cooked śāli rice mixed with milk. One should constantly keep in mind these vidyā-mantras of the Buddha and the other names of the vidyā-mantras of the buddhas, the blessed ones.

"Moreover, through the power of these bases of secret mantra, whatever asuras, garuḍas, kiṃnaras, mahoragas, yakṣas, pretas, kumbhāṇḍas, piśācas, humans or nonhumans dwell in world systems as numerous as the sands of the river Ganges, they will be pleased with him. They will not create obstacles. They will always protect him. He will also be protected by the bodhisattva great beings Excellent Joy, Great Expanse, Vajrapāṇi, Saṃtuṣita, the One Who Dwells on the Solitary Mountain, and the One Who Dwells on the Snow Mountain. And those bodhisattvas as far as Akaniṣṭha, too, through the power of the Dhāraṇī Endowed with Wealth, will bless him in times of fear or in the wilderness. They will grant him fearlessness."

When the Blessed One, dwelling in Tuṣita heaven on the stone slab like white wool, had spoken thus, the bodhisattva great being Supratiṣṭhita and those many thousands of bodhisattvas rejoiced and praised what the Blessed One had said.

This completes the noble dhāraṇī called Endowed with Wealth.

It was translated and edited by the Indian preceptors Jinamitra and Dānaśīla and the chief editor and translator Bandé Yeshé Dé, then revised according to the new terminology and finalized.
