Homage to the King of Lunar Days, Constellations, and Moments

The Dhāraṇī for Accomplishing All Aims is a very short incantation text, known in Tibetan under a title that expresses the same idea of bringing every purpose to fulfillment. After paying homage to the Three Jewels, it honors a buddha called the King of Lunar Days, Constellations, and Moments, a name that evokes the astrological reckoning of auspicious times. It then gives a brief Sanskrit dhāraṇī invoking the constellations and lunar days, followed by a simple rite: anyone about to set out in any direction should recite the formula seven times before departing, and all their aims will be accomplished. The work is Toh 1029 in the Kangyur, where it belongs to the collection of dhāraṇī texts, the short protective and beneficial spells gathered within the tantra section of the canon.

Homage to the Three Jewels.
Homage to the Blessed One, the Tathāgata, the arhat, the perfectly and completely awakened buddha, the King of Lunar Days, Constellations, and Moments.

tadyathā nakṣatre sarva tithī mūrte naśatrani mesamitadhani bhavantu svāhā

Its rite is as follows. Whatever direction one wishes to go in, if one recites this seven times and then sets out, all one's aims will be accomplished.

This completes the Dhāraṇī for Accomplishing All Aims.
