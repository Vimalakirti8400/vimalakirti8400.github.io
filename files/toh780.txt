Harm from epidemic disease will not occur.

Oṃ namo mañjuśriye, namaḥ suśriye, nama uttamaśriye svāhā.

This completes the dhāraṇī for paying homage.

Homage to the Three Jewels.

Homage to the great yakṣa general, Wrathful Vajrapāṇi.

Iri miri, dhadaṃme, bahu phalaṃ bhavatu svāhā.

Having recited this a thousand times over a single scrap of cloth, if one then gives it to a person of noble family, or to a teacher, or to any other single person, within half a month one will obtain a hundred scraps of cloth. Likewise, whether it be wealth, food, or livestock of any kind, the same result will follow.

This completes the dhāraṇī for obtaining a hundred garments.
