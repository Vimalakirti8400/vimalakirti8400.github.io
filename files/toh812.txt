Just as has been taught, may I come to possess the power of accomplishment. Just as that Lord of Compassion's liberation through great compassion is spontaneously accomplished, may it become of one taste with the compassion of all buddhas. Just as the Lord of Compassion brings sentient beings to full maturity, purifies buddha-fields completely, and brings all buddha qualities to full perfection, so may I and all sentient beings likewise bring all sentient beings to full maturity, purify all buddha-fields completely, and bring all the qualities of buddha-fields to full perfection.

For as long as I wander in saṃsāra,
for as many bodies as I take birth in,
for that long may I make offerings to the Protector,
and may I gather accumulations through bodily engagement.

For as long as I wander in saṃsāra,
for as many words as I utter,
for that long may I recite the Protector's name,
and may I gather accumulations through verbal engagement.

For as long as I wander in saṃsāra,
for as many thoughts as arise in my mind,
for that long may I recollect the Protector,
and may I gather accumulations through mental engagement.

Thus, having made the Powerful Lord of Compassion the support
through body, speech, and mind,
may body, speech, and mind be free of faults,
and may they come to be endowed with excellent qualities.

Thus, having constantly engaged, through body, speech, and mind,
with the body, speech, and mind of the Powerful Lord of Compassion,
Avalokiteśvara, the Powerful Lord of Compassion,
the sole refuge and hope of the three realms of existence,
whose qualities are inconceivable —
may I too become accomplished just like him,
and may I bring the hopes of the three realms of existence to complete fulfillment.
