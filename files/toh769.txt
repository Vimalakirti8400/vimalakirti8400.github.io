Homage to the Omniscient One.

Thus did I hear. At one time, the Blessed One was dwelling in the Jewel City, in the residence of the bodhisattva Maṇibhadra, son of Maṇibhadra the great yakṣa chieftain.

Then the great yakṣa chieftain Maṇibhadra's son addressed the Blessed One with these words: "Blessed One, if any monk, or nun, or layman, or laywoman recollects my essence mantra three times by day and three times by night, Blessed One, I will always follow after that person. I will protect them from yakṣas at a distance of a hundred leagues. I will bring all their wishes to complete fulfillment. Apart from misdeeds arising from desire, I will bestow upon them food and drink, clothing and nourishment, wealth and grain, goods, and gold.
