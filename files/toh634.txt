Homage to the great bodhisattva Avalokiteśvara.

Namas sarva-buddha-bodhisattvānāṃ. Samanta-amogha. Apratihata. Śāsanānāṃ. Nama āryāvalokiteśvarāya. Mahābodhisattvāya. Vajrasattvāya. Vajravipulāya. Padmasaṃbhavāya. Padmayonye. Then all the Tathāgatas of the ten directions extended their right hands and anointed the crown of the great bodhisattva Avalokiteśvara's head, and declared: "O great heap of precious qualities, it is excellent, excellent that you have expounded this dhāraṇī mantra and thereby taught sentient beings the Dharma from which buddhahood arises. This alone, merely by being heard, purifies sentient beings most completely. Whoever listens to it, bears it in mind, reads it, and masters it fully will obtain all excellent qualities in their perfection and will attain the supreme state of purity."

I bow to you, supreme Vajrasattva (Skt. vajrasattva).
Bearing the immaculate lotus in the midst of your hands,

Then the Blessed One, the Self-Arisen, Dispeller of Darkness,
steady as the roll of a great drum, declared:
"This praise-song to the supreme great Vajra, the hymn
of the one hundred and eight names, is excellent and clears away all faults.

If there is anyone who always desires the highest good
and praises the lord Avalokiteśvara,
without long delay they will attain
the supreme great enjoyment of qualities and the Dharma.

Whatever person constantly praises him thus,
the noble lord Avalokiteśvara will grant them blessings.
Sins will no longer arise in them,
and whatever they desire they will easily obtain.
They will not be stained by the impurity of wrongdoing,
and they will go on, keeping their sight on the straight and good path.
The lotus-born, the sole refuge worthy of the three realms of existence —
they will likewise come to practice the supreme Dharma.

So it is that whoever seeks the accomplishment of these distinctive names,
whoever always desires happiness,
should ceaselessly praise Avalokiteśvara,
the lord of all beings, the master of every living creature,
the one arisen from the lotus."
