This is the rite for this dhāraṇī. By reciting it even once, all the karmic obscurations accumulated over a thousand eons will be exhausted. One will obtain the roots of virtue accumulated by a thousand buddhas. For a thousand eons one will turn one's back on saṃsāra. After passing from this life, one will obtain the kingdom of a universal monarch. At the time of death one will see a thousand buddhas. One will be born in pure buddha fields. If one recites it twenty-one times in each of the three periods of the day and the three periods of the night, one will see in dreams whatever one wishes. One will also obtain the golden-colored body of a tathāgata. And one will come to have unshakable faith in the domains of the buddhas and bodhisattvas. These and other limitless benefits will arise.

The dhāraṇī called The Thousandfold is complete.
