Homage to the Wrathful One, the Immovable Protector.

Now what is to be truly taught:

Beholding the three realms filled with aversion,
in order that all aversion be exhausted,
the Blessed One, whose very nature is compassion,
fashioned the Wrathful Immovable Protector.

The lord whose essential nature is pure,
with mantra, mudrā, and meditative absorption,
with pure dhyāna and enlightened activity —
the Blessed One, the Great Vajradhara,
in the blazing, tumultuous charnel ground,
holding fast, he bowed and earnestly entreated:

"O listen! True great wrathful chief!
For the sake of abiding in all-encompassing purity,
from the secret tantra of the Protector Immovable,
the meditative absorption of the great bodhisattva —
the wondrous liberation — please surely reveal it!"

Then the truly primordial one spoke:

"The hero who dwells in the charnel ground is excellent.
The six mudrās, certainly adorned with bone ornaments,
the one who is the master of the assembled retinue there,
abides in the four causes and the four immeasurables:
this is the conceptual thought of gnosis-thought.

By the unbroken supreme one,
upon the seat of wisdom and skillful means — sun and moon —
he abides in the essential nature of body, speech, and mind,
bearing the marks of the transformed five wisdoms.
Blessed with the beauty of the six mudrās,
he embraces the lady of the charnel ground.

Abiding in the aggregates and so forth as a sage,
lotus and vajra are bestowed with blessing.
Without defensive preparation,
free from signs, unconditioned,
beyond conceptuality, action-free —
the body in the manner of dark gathered poisons.
With vajra noose, threatening, he stands.
The left leg extended, eyes reddish-veined,
in the manner of wisdom — lotus and curved blade.
Embraced on the left, satisfying, ornament-free,
skillful means and wisdom, free from attachment,
the final blessing pressed down, he abides
in the center of the blazing maṇḍala.

Upon Hūṃ stands the cross-marked vajra.
From E, the Dharmadhātu vessel is accomplished — the female.
With weapons, spears, nets.
Resting upon the five deity mudrās of support.
Devotion is what is fully accomplished."

This concludes the first chapter: the chapter on the purity of devotion.

Now what is to be truly taught:

The secret of non-dual transformation:
the wheel of the Immovable, great gnosis.
At the throat, a garland — source of jewels.
Ear ornaments of form — Amitābha.
Bone ear ornaments — Amoghasiddhi.
Ashes the radiant — perfectly pure.

At the eyes, the Vajrā of Desire.
At the ears, she is called the Lady of Aversion.
The nose abandoned entirely the manner of envy.
At the tongue, the Vajrā of Jealousy.
At the body, Vajra Delusion.
At the mind, the Sovereign of Dharma.

The maṇḍala that is the very body, perfectly pure:
the channels are by nature the bindu-drops.
The eight pillars are the eight legs.
The five nectars and the five hues,
right, left, above, below — these are the gates.
Teeth, nails, body hair — tva ra ṇa.
The very hub at the center of the heart.
The queens of the five aggregates,
known as the goddesses of the elements and sense bases.
The four elements are the four gatekeepers.
A fathom in span, four-cornered, the seat —
this is the definitive teaching on the Immovable Protector.
