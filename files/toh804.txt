The Dhāraṇī for Offering the Impurities of the Body as a Gift

The dhāraṇī that cannot be overpowered by living creatures is complete.

Homage to the Three Jewels.

The dhāraṇī for offering the impurities of the body as a gift:

For saliva: oṃ āḥ śleṣmā hare bhyaḥ svāhā.

For spittle: oṃ āḥ khreda āhare bhyaḥ svāhā.

For nasal mucus: oṃ āḥ siṃghāna āhare bhyaḥ svāhā.

For bodily filth: oṃ āḥ mala āhare bhyaḥ svāhā.

For feces: oṃ āḥ viṭa āhare bhyaḥ svāhā.

For urine: oṃ āḥ mūtra āhare bhyaḥ svāhā.

For each of these one should say, "May those who consume these attain them," and then snap the fingers.

Whatever merit is accumulated by giving these in this way should be dedicated to awakening for the sake of all sentient beings.

These were brought forth from the sūtras and tantras by the guru of Vajrāsana, and translated by the translator Bari.
