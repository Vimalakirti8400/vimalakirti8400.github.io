The Noble Great Strength: A Sūtra of the Great Vehicle (Ārya-Mahābala-nāma-mahāyānasūtra)

Toh 757 in the Kangyur is a Mahāyāna sūtra known in English as "The Noble Great Strength," in Sanskrit as the Ārya-Mahābala-nāma-mahāyānasūtra, and in Tibetan as Phags pa stobs po che zhes bya ba theg pa chen po'i mdo. It belongs to the general class of sūtra literature and centers on the bodhisattva Great Strength, a powerful four-armed figure associated with Vajrapāṇi, presenting teachings delivered by the Buddha on Gṛdhrakūṭa mountain in the presence of this great being. The text combines narrative and ritual elements, including mantras, and sits within the broader Kangyur collection of translated scriptures regarded as buddhavacana, or the word of the Buddha.

The praise with mantra to Smoky Flame (Skt. Umertseg), King of Wrathful Ones, is complete.

In the language of India: Ārya-Mahābala-nāma-mahāyāna-sūtra. In the Tibetan language: The Noble Great Strength, a sūtra of the Great Vehicle.

Then, abiding in the manner of one resting upon a lotus, he gazed out over the maṇḍala of the fully assembled retinue, and entered into meditative equipoise in the absorption called "Bringing Joy" — he being the wrathful, disheveled one, adorned with a belt of snakes.

The moment he had entered that equipoise, he himself perceived that the disheveled wrathful one was making many hundreds of thousands of tens of millions of obstructing spirits tremble and be crushed. Those spirits, scorched, moaned and gave up their lives; thinking it to be the kalpa of conflagration, they wept in voices of desperation. They went before the bodhisattva Vajrapāṇi and cried out: "Blessed One, please fully protect us! Bodhisattva, please fully protect us! Blessed One, we are being crushed and intoxicated by the Blessed One's disheveled wrathful one, and in our wretchedness we no longer care for our very lives!"

Then the great bodhisattva Vajrapāṇi arose from that absorption and spoke to all the maṇḍalas of Māra: "How wondrous! This great wrathful one has tremendous miraculous power! He has tremendous might!"
