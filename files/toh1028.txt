The Dhāraṇī for Protecting Oneself

The Dhāraṇī for Protecting Oneself is a very brief protective incantation from the Buddhist tradition. After a homage to the Three Jewels, it gives a short mantra and a simple ritual instruction. The practitioner faces the sun, recites the mantra seven times over water, and then scatters the water in the ten directions to secure personal protection. The source lines do not clearly preserve its Sanskrit and Tibetan titles, but the text identifies itself by this name in its closing line. It is Toh 1028 in the Kangyur, where it belongs to the large class of dhāraṇī texts, short spells and formulas valued for their protective power.

Homage to the Three Jewels.

tadyathā oṃ ale kāli ale kāli mucci te svāhā

Facing the sun, recite this mantra seven times over water and scatter the water in the ten directions, and one's self will be protected.
