The Tantra Called the Great Web of Illusion of the Goddess (Devījālīmahāmāyātantra)

This text, known in Sanskrit as the Devī­jāla­mahāmāyā­tantra and in Tibetan as Lha mo sgyu 'phrul dra ba chen mo'i rgyud, bears the English title The Tantra of the Goddess Great Magical Net. It belongs to the class of tantric scriptures centered on a great goddess, presenting esoteric teachings, ritual instructions, and visionary discourse associated with the Blessed One's great secret. Catalogued as Toh 836, it is preserved in the Kangyur among the collection of tantras, reflecting the tradition's transmission of goddess-centered tantric practice and doctrine.
