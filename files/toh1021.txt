The Dhāraṇī for Clearing Away the Illness of Indigestion

The Dhāraṇī for Clearing Away the Illness of Indigestion is a very brief healing incantation, and its Sanskrit and Tibetan titles are not given in the material available here. After paying homage to the Three Jewels and to the Tathāgata called King of the Radiance of Fire, it presents a short mantra and a simple accompanying rite: one recites the mantra over one's hand and then rubs the belly, which is said to clear away indigestion. It is a practical example of the protective and curative formulas that circulated widely in Buddhist practice, joining devotion to a buddha with a concrete remedy for an everyday ailment. The text is Toh 1021 in the Kangyur, where it belongs among the collected dhāraṇīs, the class of short spells and incantations preserved in the canon.

Homage to the Three Jewels.
Homage to the Blessed One, the Tathāgata, the arhat, the perfectly complete Buddha, King of the Radiance of Fire.

tadyathā hure hude huṭagahane svāhā

The rite: after reciting this over one's hand, if one rubs the belly with it, the illness of indigestion will be cleared away.

The Dhāraṇī for Clearing Away the Illness of Indigestion is complete.

This concludes the translation. The following are translator's notes.

The buddha's name, rendered here as "King of the Radiance of Fire," is a translation of the Tibetan. "Bsreg gsol," literally "eater of burnt offerings," is a standard epithet of fire (Skt. hutabhuj or hutāśana). I have not identified a Sanskrit form of the full name with confidence.

The written "ltо mnyes nam zhu ba'i" has been read as "ltо mnyes na, ma zhu ba'i," that is, "if one rubs the belly, the illness of indigestion." This fits the dhāraṇī's title, which names the illness as "ma zhu ba."
