In the Indian language: Āryavighnavināyakāradhāraṇī. In the Tibetan language: The Noble Dhāraṇī That Clears Away Obstacles.

Thus did I hear. At one time the Blessed One was dwelling in Śrāvastī, in the Jeta Grove, the garden of Anāthapiṇḍada. At that time the Blessed One addressed the venerable Ānanda:

"Ānanda, whoever holds the essence of the Lord of the Assembly — that person will accomplish all rites and all intentions. All secret mantras will be accomplished.

"Thus it is: namostu te, kaṭa kaṭa, maṭa maṭa, dara dara, ḍaha ḍaha, gṛhṇa, bhañja bhañja, namostu te, ruru cacaye svāhā, abhūtrā, vinaduḥkhan mantseddha para samagacchati, hahaya, mahāhastikṣatanana, pragobaye mo, kuru kuru, curu curu, muru muru, namo namaḥ svāhā.

"Rising at dawn, recite this mantra seven times. Take a vessel of water and scatter it in the directions above and below, and all obstacles will be bound. One who wishes to accomplish secret mantras should tie twenty-one knots in a white thread and bind it to the arm — the knowledge mantra will be accomplished and all obstacles will be bound. In whatever place the Lord of the Assembly appears, if this mantra is read even once, the obstacles will not cause harm."

When the Blessed One had spoken these words, the entire assembly was filled with joy, and the world together with its gods, humans, asuras, and gandharvas rejoiced and praised what the Blessed One had taught.

The Noble Dhāraṇī That Clears Away Obstacles is complete.

Furthermore, if one is enveloped in the smoke produced by burning yellow bdellium resin and the thick refuse of wheat, mixed with the stabilizing substance that repels all thieves, then even if one enters the presence of a person stricken with a contagious illness, one will not be infected. If it touches a wound, it quickly heals it. For harmful spirits causing harm in the home it is greatly praised.
