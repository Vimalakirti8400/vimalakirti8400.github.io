The Blessed One, the Buddha, was dwelling in the Veṇuvana grove in the city of Rājagṛha. At that time lightning appeared, shooting stars fell, and meteoric iron descended from the sky. The king Rūpahṛdaya, together with his retinue, summoned his brahmin astrologers and said to them: "What is the cause, and what are the conditions, for all this agitation in the sky?" The brahmins replied: "Omens such as these arise from the scriptural science called the Twelve Eyes. We are too ashamed to expound it."

Then King Rūpahṛdaya went to where the Blessed One was, paid homage at his feet, and spoke these words: "Blessed One, out of compassion for sentient beings, please teach the scriptural science of omens of the Twelve Eyes." The Blessed One declared: "One who has gone forth into renunciation is not of the sort to teach such worldly scriptural sciences as these, which belong to non-Buddhists."
