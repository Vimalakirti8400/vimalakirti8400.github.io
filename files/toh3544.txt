A Concise Sādhana of the Yellow Prajñāpāramitā (Pītavarṇasaṃkṣiptaprajñāpāramitāsādhana)

In the language of India: pītabarṇasaṃkṣiptaprajñāpāramitāsādhanaṃ. In the language of Tibet: A Concise Sādhana of the Yellow Prajñāpāramitā.

Homage to the Blessed Lady Prajñāpāramitā.

Following the rite described earlier, after meditating on emptiness, visualize a yellow syllable dhīḥ resting on a red lotus and a moon. It transforms completely, and from it arises Prajñāpāramitā, yellow in color. She has a crown of matted locks in which Akṣobhya is seated. She makes the mudrā of teaching the Dharma, and at her left side she holds a blue utpala lotus bearing a book. She wears an upper garment of white cloth, and she is beautiful, adorned with every kind of garment and ornament.

Having visualized her in this way, one should recite the mantra oṃ dhīḥ śuta smṛti bijāya svāhā.

The Concise Sādhana of the Yellow Prajñāpāramitā is complete.
