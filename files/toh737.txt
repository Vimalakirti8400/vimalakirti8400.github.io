Homage to the Three Jewels.

Homage to the great bodhisattva (bodhisattva), the great being endowed with great compassion, who cherishes all sentient beings with loving tenderness — the noble Avalokiteśvara, Lord of Compassion.

This dhāraṇī is to be recited in order to protect life and guard the breath: it subdues all spirits, averts all contagious disease, stops all untimely death, utterly destroys all harmful influences of planets and stars and all spirit-afflictions, vanquishes all the abodes of māra, repels all enemies one by one.

Thus: Avalokite, Avalokite, Sambhave, Dhara dhara dhara, Bara bara bara, Kāle kāle kāle, Mahākāla, Avalokita, Prasādhane, Dhame dhame dhame, Dhāmāne, Samāhite —

I pray: protect me, protect me. I pray: be my protection from all terror and fear. I pray: give me complete refuge. I pray: hold me completely. I pray: care for me completely. I pray: bring about peace and wellbeing. I pray: put aside punishment. I pray: put aside weapons. I pray: cut off the afflictions. I pray: bind the ground.

Kuru kuru paṃ. Mokṣi. Bidhi bidhi bidhi. Mārgasaṃprasvate. Amṛtate javati —

I pray: protect me always from all harm. Protect me.

Mulu mulu mulu, Khukhu me, Ghori ghori, Bale bale, Tara tara, Muya muya, Muñcani, Khakhare, Dhu hūṃ hūṃ ni, Dhu hūṃ ni —

I pray: protect me from all strife, fighting, dispute, and conflict. Protect me.

Jayaṃ kuru kuru, Phuru phuru svāhā, Amale svāhā, Amṛte svāhā.

This concludes the dhāraṇī called the Noble Powerful Averter.
