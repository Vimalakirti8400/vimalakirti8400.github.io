The Noble Dhāraṇī of the Perfection of Wisdom in One Hundred Thousand Lines (Āryaśatasāhasrikāprajñāpāramitādhāraṇī)

The Noble Dhāraṇī of the Perfection of Wisdom in One Hundred Thousand Lines, whose Tibetan title begins Pakpa Sherab kyi Parol tu Chinpa, is a very short dhāraṇī text connected with the vast Perfection of Wisdom sūtra in one hundred thousand lines. After paying homage to the Blessed Mother, the Perfection of Wisdom, it presents a single Sanskrit incantation invoking the qualities of the Dharma, wisdom, learning, memory, and intelligence. It then briefly states its benefits: whoever holds this dhāraṇī will come to hold the great sūtra itself, constant recitation will make the mind keen and retentive, and all karmic obscurations will be purified. The work is Toh 932 in the Kangyur, where it belongs to the class of dhāraṇī texts, the short spells and formulas gathered in the canon's collection of dhāraṇīs.

The Noble Dhāraṇī of the Perfection of Wisdom in One Hundred Thousand Lines

Homage to the Blessed Mother, the Perfection of Wisdom!

tadyathā oṃ muni dharme saṃgraha dharme anugraha dharme vimuktidharme sāra anugraha dharme vaiśravaṇa parivartana dharme sarva kārya paripramaṇa dharme samantānu parivartana dharme svāhā oṃ prajñe śruti smṛti mati vijaye svāhā dhīḥ dhāraṇī svāhā oṃ prajñāpāramitā bala svāhā

By holding this, one will come to hold the Perfection of Wisdom in One Hundred Thousand Lines. If one recites it constantly, one's mind will become keen and retentive. All obscurations of karma will be purified.

This completes the Dhāraṇī of the Perfection of Wisdom in One Hundred Thousand Lines.
