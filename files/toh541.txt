Homage to all buddhas and bodhisattvas. Homage to the Tathāgata, Source of Qualities. Homage to the Tathāgata, Fragrant with All-Pervading Incense. Homage to the Tathāgata, Endowed with Skill Unvanquished by Others. Homage to the Tathāgata, Dispeller of All Obscurations. Homage to the Tathāgata, Supreme Moon. The mantra is as follows: dhare dhare, dhāre dhāraṇī, rana-koṭe abhayā-kalpe, kalpe nigaṇi, ananta-kalpe, amṛta-kalpe, hutāśare, ananta-mukhe, ananta-cakṣu-samudgate, oṃ bha svāhā.

Noble son, these dhāraṇī mantras were spoken, blessed, and rejoiced in by buddhas, Blessed Ones, as numerous as the grains of sand in the Ganges River, for the sake of sentient beings who would go to the lower realms.

Noble son, whatever bodhisattva holds this dhāraṇī shall utterly transcend the eight fears in their boundless measure: the boundless fear of the hells, the boundless fear of birth in the animal realm, the boundless fear of the realm of Yama, lord of death, the boundless fear of hungry ghosts, the boundless fear of treacherous paths, the boundless fear of birth, the boundless fear of illness, and the boundless fear of death. The buddhas and Blessed Ones of the ten directions will turn their minds toward that person. At the time of death one's mind will remain undistracted, and one will be born in the presence of the buddhas. One will obtain a body of inexhaustible nature.
