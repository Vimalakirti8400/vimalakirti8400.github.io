The Dhāraṇī of Amitābha

Homage to the Blessed One, the Tathāgata Amitābha, the arhat, the perfectly and completely awakened buddha.

Tadyathā: amite, amitodbhave, amitasaṃbhave, amitavikrāntagāmini, gaganakīrtikare, sarvakleśakṣayaṃkari svāhā.

These are the benefits of this dhāraṇī. It should be understood that even karmic obscurations accumulated over a hundred thousand eons are exhausted when it is recited three times. If it is recited twenty-one times, the negativity of the four root downfalls is completely purified, and one also comes to have planted roots of virtue under a thousand buddhas.

The Recollection of Amitābha is complete.
