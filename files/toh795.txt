The Dhāraṇī Called Pacifying Poison

Homage to Vasudhārā, goddess of ambrosial medicine.

Tadyathā: oṃ suru suru ka varavarake viri hiri śuddhe viśuddhe svāhā.

By the truth of the Buddha, the Blessed One, and by his splendor, the poison of nāgas, the poison of snakes, compounded poison, poison of infusion, root poison, and the poison of the fangs of predatory beasts are defeated. One should proclaim: "The poison is certainly defeated."

This completes the Dhāraṇī for Pacifying All Poisons.
