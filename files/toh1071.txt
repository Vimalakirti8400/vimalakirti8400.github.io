Blessing the Offerings with the Mantra Oṃ Sarva Tathāgata Pūjā Megha

This brief text, which may be called in English the Blessing of Offerings, consists of a single mantra and a one-line instruction. Its Tibetan wording ends with the phrase mchod pa byin gyis brlab bo, meaning "one blesses the offerings," and no separate Sanskrit title is evident. The mantra opens with oṃ sarva tathāgata, invoking all the tathāgatas, and seems to evoke clouds of offerings spreading out in great abundance before closing with hūṃ. The practitioner recites it to consecrate the offerings presented in ritual worship, so that they become a fitting gift to the buddhas. The text is Toh 1071 in the Kangyur, where it belongs among the short dhāraṇī and mantra texts gathered in the collection's tantra section.
