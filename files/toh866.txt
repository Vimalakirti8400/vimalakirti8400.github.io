Homage to the Three Jewels. Homage to the Tathāgata, the arhat, the perfectly complete Buddha Padmanetra (Lotus Eye). Homage to the bodhisattva mahāsattva Sarvanīvaraṇaviṣkambhin.

Tadyathā: oṃ tara tara, tiri tiri, turu turu, kala kala, kili kili, kulu kulu, bhara bhara, bhiri bhiri, bhuru bhuru, bhaya bhaya, kuru kuru, sarva karma āvaraṇa jani meśa prabhaya svāhā. Jvalani vidhani vai svāhā. Kuru pañcali svāhā. Sāgara saṃvire svāhā. Sarva tathāgata adhiṣṭhite svāhā.

This is the rite for this dhāraṇī. If one rises in the morning and recites it one hundred and eight times, then once twenty-one days have passed, all obscurations of karma will be exhausted. The five eyes will also become completely pure. Eye disease will not arise for that person. Disease will not arise in the body. Faults of mind will not arise. The five eyes will also become completely pure. Likewise, the five purities of mind will also be attained. In every lifetime one will encounter the appearance of buddhas. Until one dwells at the seat of awakening, one will never be parted from seeing the buddhas, hearing the Dharma, honoring and serving the Saṅgha, and bringing all sentient beings to full maturity. The body will become beautiful. The mind will become clear and serene.

Here ends the noble dhāraṇī called Padmanetra.
