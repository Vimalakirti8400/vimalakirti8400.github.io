Homage to the Blessed One, the Tathāgata, the arhat, the perfectly complete buddha, the King of Light who takes away the burning.

The buddha's name is rendered according to the Tibetan, bsreg gsol 'od kyi rgyal po. This probably corresponds to a Sanskrit name along the lines of "King of Light of the Burning Offering" (such as a fire-offering or smoke-offering buddha), but the Tibetan is compressed. A reading such as "King of Light invoked in the burning-offering" is also possible; I have chosen the sense of removing or consuming the burning, but this is a consequential choice. The dhāraṇī is left in transliteration as it stands, and since the passage ends mid-formula after "huṭa gatani," it is evidently an excerpt of a longer mantra.
