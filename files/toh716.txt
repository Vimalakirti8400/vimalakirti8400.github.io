Here ends the dhāraṇī for memorizing. Homage to the Three Jewels. Homage to the great bodhisattva, the bodhisattva of great compassion, the noble Avalokiteśvara, Lord of What Is Seen.

Tadyathā: Oṃ munikante muniprabhasvare svāhā.

Having formed a maṇḍala before the Blessed One, the noble Avalokiteśvara, one should take a thumb-sized portion of clean butter, recite this mantra one hundred and eight times, and then eat it. On the very first day, one will memorize as much as if one had read one thousand verses. On the day that three days have elapsed, one will memorize three thousand verses. On the day that seven days have elapsed, one will retain whatever one has heard. Here ends the dhāraṇī for memorizing three thousand verses.
