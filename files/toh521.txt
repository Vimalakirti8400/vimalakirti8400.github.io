The Heart of Noble Dependent Origination (Ārya Pratītyasamutpāda Hṛdaya Nāma)

The Heart of Noble Dependent Origination, known in Sanskrit as the Ārya Pratītyasamutpāda Hṛdaya and in Tibetan as Phags pa rten cing 'brel par 'byung ba'i snying po, is a brief Mahāyāna sūtra centered on the doctrine of dependent origination, one of the Buddha's most fundamental teachings. The text presents the celebrated verse — "Those phenomena that arise from causes, their causes, and the cessation of those causes: thus has the Tathāgata declared" — which encapsulates the entire teaching of conditioned arising and is traditionally considered a potent distillation of the Dharma. Alongside the verse in both translation and original Sanskrit, the text attributes great purificatory and protective power to its recitation, positioning it as a kind of dhāraṇī-like heart essence. It is Toh 521 in the Kangyur, classified among the Mahāyāna sūtras.
