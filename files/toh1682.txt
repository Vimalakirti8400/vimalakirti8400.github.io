The Rite of Recollecting Glorious Vajrasattva (Śrīvajrasattvānusmṛtināmavidhi)

The Rite of Recollecting Glorious Vajrasattva, known in Sanskrit as Śrī Vajrasattva Anusmṛti Nāma Vidhi and in Tibetan as Pal Dorjé Sempa Jesu Drenpé Choga, is a short tantric ritual manual devoted to the meditative recollection and worship of Vajrasattva, the embodiment of the purity of all the buddhas. Written largely in verse, it guides the practitioner through visualizing the maṇḍala as a magical display of nondual wisdom, generating the deity with his consort, retinue goddesses and door guardians from seed syllables, and then making offerings, praises, mantra recitations and hand gestures that unite skillful means and wisdom, before closing with a torma offering and dedication. It is Toh 1682 in the Tengyur, where it belongs to the collection of tantra commentaries and practice texts, and its colophon attributes it to a great Indian master.

In the language of India: śrī vajrasatva anusmṛtināma vidhi. In the language of Tibet: The Rite of Recollecting Glorious Vajrasattva.
