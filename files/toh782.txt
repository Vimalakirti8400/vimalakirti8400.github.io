Homage to the Tathāgata, Arhat, Perfectly Completed Buddha who thoroughly clears away all bad rebirths (durgati).

Tadyathā: oṃ śodhani viśodhane mama sarva-pāpaṃ viśodhane śuddhe viśuddhe sarva-karma-āvaraṇa-viśuddhe svāhā.
