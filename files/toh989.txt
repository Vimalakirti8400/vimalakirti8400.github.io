The Dhāraṇī of the Noble Goddess Cundā (Āryacundadevīnāmadhāraṇī)

The Dhāraṇī of the Noble Goddess Cundā, known in Sanskrit as the Āryacundādevīnāmadhāraṇī and in Tibetan as 'Phags pa lha mo skul byed ma zhes bya ba'i gzungs, is a short dhāraṇī text devoted to the goddess Cundā. After paying homage to the Three Jewels and to the Buddha Vairocana, it praises Cundā as a powerful and wrathful protector, praised by all the tathāgatas, who overcomes adversaries and holds a sword, an axe, a hook, and a noose. It centers on her mantra, which is recited to invoke her power and to bring about auspiciousness and protection for the practitioner. The text is Toh 989 in the Kangyur, where it belongs to the class of dhāraṇī texts, the collection of spells and incantations used for protection and blessing.

In the language of Tibet: The Dhāraṇī of the Noble Goddess Cundā.

Homage to the Three Jewels. Homage to the Blessed One, the Tathāgata, the arhat, the perfectly and completely awakened Buddha Vairocana.

Blessed Lady, goddess Cundā, endowed with immeasurable might and power, accomplished in miraculous power and in overcoming adversaries, praised and extolled by all the tathāgatas, homage to you! Make me wholly auspicious in every way!

namaḥ sbtānī saṃmyaksaṃbuddha keṭīnāṃ tadyathā oṃ cale cule cunde svāhā

You of great diligence, unobstructed, the tamer; you of great power, great in overcoming adversaries; you who hold in your hands a sword, an axe, a hook and a noose; great wrathful lady, sovereign lady of the great wrathful ones; you of fierce form; you of infinite faces; you who possess a thousand arms; unvanquished one; you whom others cannot overcome; unfailing one; you who are hard to tame; thousand-eyed one; you who are blessed by all the tathāgatas; you to whom all the gods bow and present offerings; you with vajra claws; you who draw forth with vajra upon vajra; you with vajra weapons; you with a vajra body; you with a vajra fist; you whose gaze is of wide-open vajra eyes; you who, though not fierce, display yourself transformed into a fierce form; you whose body is adorned with ornaments of vajra and beryl:

oṃ bhagavati cunde dhraṃ dhruṃ krūṃ krūṃ mrūṃ mrūṃ srūṃ srūṃ rūṃ rūṃ āveśaya gṛhṇa gṛhṇa gṛhṇāpaya gṛhṇābaya hara hara bhañja bhañja sara sara mara mara māraya māraya paca paca daha daha gṛhṇa gṛhṇa

Hostile grahas; fevers of one day, of two days, of three days, of four days and of seven days; constant fever and momentary fever; bhūtas, vetālas, pretas, piśācas, yakṣas, rākṣasas and kumbhāṇḍa grahas; those born from the womb and those born from karma; these that move about and these that stay; whatever hostile ones harm me, all those malevolent ones:

saṃdhaya saṃdhaya mardaya mardaya śoṣaya śoṣaya tāpaya tāpaya udsādhaya utsādhaya hana hana vajreṇa sara sara baṇḍena māraya māraya khakgen hūṃ hūṃ hūṃ cūṃ cūṃ cūṃ drūṃ drūṃ drūṃ oṃ cale cule cunde sarva artha sādhaya svāhā

By the power and blessing of the Blessed Lady, the noble goddess Cundā, may I be well and free from all harm! svāhā

The Dhāraṇī of the Noble Goddess Cundā is complete.
