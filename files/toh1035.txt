The Dhāraṇī for Release from Bondage

The Dhāraṇī for Release from Bondage is a very brief incantation text that opens with homage to the noble Avalokiteśvara, the bodhisattva of compassion, and then presents a single short mantra. The mantra invokes Tārā, the female saviour closely associated with Avalokiteśvara, and calls upon her as the one who frees from bonds. The text then promises that reciting this mantra of the noble one brings release from bondage. A Sanskrit title along the lines of Bandhamocanī Dhāraṇī can reasonably be inferred from the mantra itself, though the source lines given here do not state the Sanskrit or Tibetan titles explicitly. The work is Toh 1035 in the Kangyur, where it belongs among the dhāraṇī texts, the class of short scriptures that preserve protective and liberating formulas for recitation.

Homage to the noble Avalokiteśvara.

tāre tāre tārāya bhagabate bandhamocani svāhā

If one recites this mantra of the noble one, it brings release from bondage.

The Dhāraṇī for Release from Bondage is complete.
