Dhāraṇī of the Vimalā Uṣṇīṣa (Vimalā Uṣṇīṣa Dhāraṇī)

The Dhāraṇī of the Vimalā Uṣṇīṣa, known in Sanskrit as the Vimalā Uṣṇīṣa Dhāraṇī and in Tibetan as Bimalā Uṣṇīṣa'i gzungs, is a dhāraṇī text belonging to the broader category of incantation literature focused on the ritual construction and consecration of stūpas. It presents sacred mantras and accompanying ritual procedures for the planting of stūpas, explaining how even a single stūpa, when properly consecrated through the recitation of these dhāraṇīs over materials such as clay, can accrue merit equivalent to the construction of tens of millions of stūpas. The text belongs to the tradition of uṣṇīṣa dhāraṇīs, a family of texts concerned with the purifying and spiritually potent qualities associated with the crown protrusion of a buddha, and it promises that practitioners who engage correctly with its rites will ultimately attain full awakening. The mantra for planting from one stūpa up to ten million stūpas, and the motivation throughout, are all the same. For planting one stūpa so that it counts as ten million, the mantra and the ritual procedure are as follows: one must recite this mantra twenty-one times over all the materials, including clay and so forth.

Nama trai ya dhvikānān. Jinānān. Bandhe ratnaṭayan śubhana. Akṣobhya amitābhañca bande. Ahaṃ alokitaṃ. Namastry dhānugatānān. Sarva tathāgatānān. Sukṣme sukṣme. Nāga de. Śānte. Dānte. Śīva nirākule. Yaśo yaśo vati. Teje tejo vati. Sarva buddha adhiṣṭhite svāhā.

For the mantra for inserting the seal or the mantra:

Oṃ dharmadhātu garbhe svāhā.

For the mantra for inserting the inked impression into the mold-vessel:

Oṃ vajra dhātu garbhe svāhā.

For the mantra recited while striking with the mallet and pressing:

For the mantra for placing the wheel and the finial crown:

Oṃ bhramatanye svāhā.
