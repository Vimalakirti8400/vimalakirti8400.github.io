Homage to the Three Jewels. Homage to the Buddha, who teaches without obstruction from every direction. Homage to the noble bodhisattva, the great being endowed with great compassion, Avalokiteśvara, Lord of Vision.

The mantra is as follows: tadyathā — ka ba tsa karṇa la la te svāhā.

If one recites this over water seven times and washes one's face with it, all people will come to rejoice in one's presence.

The ritual for this is as follows: one places water before the presence of the noble Avalokiteśvara, recites the mantra seven times, and drinks the water. In the morning one eats food mixed with milk. If one does this in the same manner for seven days continuously, one will come to master a thousand ślokas in a single day.
