The Ritual of the Single-Syllable Mantra of Noble Mañjuśrī.

The mantra is as follows: Tadyathā — Oṃ Kṣmīṃ.

This single-syllable secret mantra is supremely secret. It accomplishes all aims. It performs all actions. It is the heart-essence of all the Tathāgatas. It completely fulfils all hopes.

If one recites the mantra over a tooth-cleaning twig and holds it in the mouth, toothache will be relieved.

If one recites the mantra seven times over rock salt applied to a diseased eye and fills the eye with it, the eye disease will be cleared.

If a fragment of a weapon remains lodged in a person's body, one should recite the mantra seven times, or one hundred and eight times, over aged clarified butter, and then either pour it in or rub it onto the wound; the fragment of the weapon will come out.

For indigestion, bloating and pressure in the abdomen, and diarrhea caused by fever, one should recite the mantra seven times over ordinary salt, or rock salt, or any other kind of salt, and eat it; one will be freed from that illness and recover on that very same day.

For headache, one should recite the mantra seven times over a crow's feather and brush it over the affected area; that illness will be healed.
