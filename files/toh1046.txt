Homage to the Three Jewels.
Homage to the Buddha, the teacher who is wholly unobstructed.
Homage to the bodhisattva, the great being endowed with great compassion, the noble Avalokiteśvara.

tadyathā kavaca karṇa lalate svāhā

If one recites this seven times over water and washes one's face with it, all people will be pleased with one.

As for its rite: place water before the noble Avalokiteśvara, recite the dhāraṇī over it seven times, and drink it. In the morning, eat food mixed with milk. If one does this for seven days, then even in a single day one will master a thousand ślokas of text.

Here ends the dhāraṇī for mastering a thousand ślokas.
