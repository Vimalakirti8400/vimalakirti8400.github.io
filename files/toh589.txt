Homage to the noble Laṅkāvatāra Sūtra. Mahāmati, these mantra syllables are to be declared. Thus:

Oṃ padme padme devai, hine hine hine, tsulu tsulu tsulu, hule hule hule, yule yule yule, pale pale pale, muṃ ccheda na de, bhinde bhañjva, mande pramade, dinakare svāhā.

Mahāmati, whatever son of noble lineage or daughter of noble lineage holds, retains, recites, or perfectly masters these mantra syllables — no opening or occasion will be found against them by any god or goddess, nāga or nāginī, yakṣa or yakṣiṇī, asura or asurī, garuḍa or garuḍinī, kinnara or kinnarī, great serpent or great serpent-female, gandharva or gandharvinī, spirit or spirit-female, kumbhāṇḍa or kumbhāṇḍinī, flesh-eater or flesh-eater-female, oppressor or oppressor-female, one who causes forgetfulness or female who causes forgetfulness, rākṣasa or rākṣasī, piśāca or piśācī, life-force thief, male or female, one who causes bodily decay, male or female — none among all these non-humans and the like whatsoever.

Whoever recites these mantra syllables will be counted as having recited the entirety of all the Laṅkāvatāra sūtras. These mantra syllables were proclaimed by the Blessed One for the purpose of repelling rākṣasas and the like.

The dhāraṇī mantra that constitutes the recitation of the entirety of all the noble Laṅkāvatāra sūtras is here complete.
