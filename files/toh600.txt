Homage to the Blessed One, the Blazing Crown Protrusion.

Namas samanta-buddhānāṃ. Apratihata-śāsanānāṃ. Oṃ kha kha khāhi khāhi. Hūṃ hūṃ. Jvala jvala. Prajvala prajvala. Tiṣṭha tiṣṭha. Phaṭ phaṭ. Sarva-dur-nimitte. Duḥsvapna śāntaṃ kuru svāhā.

This mantra of a thousand buddhas
is renowned as the Blazing Crown Protrusion.
The actions — those that number in the thousands,
eighty and five times ninety —
the various and manifold kinds of actions,
and all of auspiciousness, glory, and renown, it accomplishes.
