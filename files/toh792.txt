Homage to the Three Jewels.

Oṃ bhagavati śruti smṛti bala bala parama siddhe svāhā.

As for the ritual: for seven days, each morning, before the noble Avalokiteśvara, one should cast the mantra seven times over a handful of water and then drink it. Even after a single day, one will be able to memorize three hundred ślokas' worth of letters. If this is done for a month, one's voice will become as melodious as that of a kinnara (a mythical being renowned for its sweet voice). One should have no doubt about this.

The Dhāraṇī for a Melodious Voice is complete.
