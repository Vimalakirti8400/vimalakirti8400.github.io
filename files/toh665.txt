The Noble Heart of the Lord of Hosts (Ārya-Gaṇapati-hṛdaya)

The Noble Heart of the Lord of Hosts, known in Sanskrit as the Ārya-Gaṇapati-hṛdaya and in Tibetan as Pakpa Tsokyi Dakpö Nyingpo, is a short dhāraṇī text centered on the figure of Gaṇapati, the Lord of Hosts, and the efficacy of holding his heart-mantra for the accomplishment of all aims and the activation of secret mantra attainments. Cast in sūtra form, it opens with the Buddha teaching on Vulture Peak Mountain at Rājagṛha before delivering the mantra directly to Ānanda, promising that all endeavors will be fulfilled through its recitation. It is Toh 665 in the Kangyur, belonging to the dhāraṇī class of canonical texts.

Homage to all buddhas and bodhisattvas.

Thus did I hear. At one time, the Blessed One was dwelling on Vulture Peak Mountain at Rājagṛha, together with a great Saṅgha of twelve hundred and fifty monks, and with a great multitude of bodhisattvas (bodhisattvas).

"Ānanda, whatever son of good family or daughter of good family, monk or nun, layman or laywoman recites this Heart of the Lord of Hosts, the Sovereign of Hosts, and accomplishes the secret mantra — whether going to another region to make offerings to the Three Jewels, or entering the retinue of a royal court — let them offer flowers and incense to the blessed buddhas and recite this Heart of the Lord of Hosts. All affairs shall be accomplished; entertain no doubt about this.

Even in the midst of strife, dispute, conflict, and all manner of civil disturbance, if one but calls this to mind, all of it shall be entirely pacified. If each day, upon rising at dawn, one recites it aloud, one will retain what one hears. No yakṣa, no demon, no ḍākinī shall be able to rob one of one's vitality, and all such beings shall be driven far away."

When the Blessed One had thus declared this, the entire retinue that was present, together with the gods, humans, asuras, and gandharvas of the world, were filled with joy, and offered their praise for what the Blessed One had taught.

Here ends the Noble Heart of the Lord of Hosts.
