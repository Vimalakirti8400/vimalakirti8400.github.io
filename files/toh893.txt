Then Mañjuśrī, who had become a youth, rose once more from his seat. He draped his upper robe over one shoulder, placed his right knee on the ground, and bowed with his palms joined toward where the Blessed One was. He bowed his head at the Blessed One's feet and said to the Blessed One: "Blessed One, I shall make a spell (vidyā) manifest. When this supreme spell is spoken, it will be a wonder."

That whole assembly gave the bodhisattva, the great being Mañjuśrī, their "Excellent!", saying: "Mañjuśrī, excellent, excellent! Mañjuśrī, speak the spell!"

"By the word of the Blessed One I shall speak it."

The bodhisattva, the great being Mañjuśrī, paid homage to all phenomena, the essence of all the buddhas, made offerings, and spoke the spell:

Whoever recites it three times at midnight, and also recites it seven times during the day, will obtain great knowledge. Such a person will retain three hundred ślokas of human composition.

Its rite is this: one makes a maṇḍala with sandalwood and also makes offerings with lamps of clarified butter. If one then recites it eight thousand times, it will be accomplished. Even if it is not accomplished, one will retain a hundred ślokas each day.

"Blessed One, if this which I have taught does not succeed, then, just like one who has committed the five acts of immediate retribution, I too shall have committed the five acts of immediate retribution."
