The Noble Great King of Tantras Victorious in the Equality of Non-Duality (Ārya-dvaya-samatā-vijaya-kṣubhikalpa-mahārāja)

Toh 452 in the Kangyur is a tantra known in Sanskrit as the Ārya-dvaya-samatā-vijaya-kṣubhikalpa-mahārāja and in Tibetan as Phakpa Nyisu Mepa Nyamnyi Nampar Gyalwa Zheybyai Tokpai Gyalpo Chenpo, rendered into English as "The Noble One Victorious in the Equality of Non-Duality: A Great King of Tantras." It belongs to the tantric section of the Kangyur and is presented as a teaching delivered by the Buddha while residing in the Heaven of Mastery over Others' Emanations, surrounded by an vast assembly of bodhisattvas who had each attained the stage of Vajrasattva. The text concerns the profound theme of non-duality and equality, framed within a Vajrayāna context and introduced with homage to the bodhisattva Vajrapāṇi, who also figures among the principal figures in the surrounding assembly alongside Samantabhadra, Avalokiteśvara, and other great beings.

In the language of India: Ārya-dvaya-samatā-vijaya-kṣubhikalpa-mahārāja. In the language of Tibet: The Great King of Tantras (Skt. kalpa-mahārāja) called "The Noble One Victorious in the Equality of Non-Duality."

Homage to the glorious bodhisattva Vajrapāṇi.

Thus did I hear. At one time the Blessed One was dwelling in the divine palace of the Heaven of Mastery over Others' Emanations, together with an assembly of bodhisattvas, great beings, equal in number to the finest dust particles of ninety-nine Mount Merus. Every one of those bodhisattvas had attained the stage of Vajrasattva and had each been empowered as a regent of the vajra. Among them were the following: the bodhisattva, great being Vajrapāṇi; the bodhisattva, great being Samantabhadra; the bodhisattva, great being Avalokiteśvara, the Powerful; the bodhisattva, great being Ākāśagarbha; the bodhisattva, great being Vajramuṣṭi; the bodhisattva, great being who, the very instant the awakening mind of equality arose, turned the wheel of Dharma; the bodhisattva, great being Gaganagañja; the bodhisattva, great being who utterly conquers all the powers of Māra; and together with these, a further assembly of bodhisattvas, great beings, equal in number to the finest dust particles of ninety-nine Mount Merus.

At that time, through the power and blessing of the Tathāgata, that divine palace of the king of gods became adorned in magnificent splendor: with multistoried pavilions of precious jewels, with great crowning jewels of precious gems, with silken banners shimmering in every brilliant hue, with pennants, parasols, victory banners, canopies, garlands of ornaments of every precious substance, lattices and half-lattices, peacock-feather fans, bunches of flowers, bells, and silk streamers stirred by the wind — and further such adorning arrangements, so that it was beautiful on every side, like a full and perfect lunar disk.

The Teacher, the Blessed One, possessing supreme and distinguished forms of the pledged wisdom consecrated by the vajra blessing of all the Tathāgatas, having received empowerment as king of Dharma in the three realms by means of the jeweled crown of all the Tathāgatas, perfectly consummate as the great lord of great yoga endowed with the omniscient wisdom of all the Tathāgatas, having fully understood the mudrā-Dharma of the equality of all phenomena — the seal of all the Tathāgatas, performing the various wondrous activities of all the Tathāgatas, the great Vairocana, whose very nature is great compassion, ever abiding in the three times in the vajra equality of the body, speech, and mind of all the Tathāgatas, fulfilling without exception the hopes of the entire realm of sentient beings — he took upon himself what all the Tathāgatas had praised and extolled, and what all the Tathāgatas had beseeched him to do, namely: to turn the wheel of Dharma.

Furthermore, gathered from the world-systems in all ten directions, there were Tathāgatas as numerous as the grains of sand in the river Ganges — like Jambudvīpa filled to the brim with sesame seeds. From each and every one of those Tathāgatas there shone forth immeasurable and uncountable Tathāgata pure land appearances.

In all those buddha fields as well, this very gateway of Dharma — supremely secret, transcending duality, equal in all things, victorious — was taught.

Then at that time, Vajrapāṇi, called the Great Vajra-Holder, who dwells perfectly within the mind of all the Tathāgatas, who brings to full accomplishment the conduct of all the Tathāgatas, who upholds the enlightened body-activity of all the Tathāgatas, and who brings to complete fulfillment the innermost intention of all the Tathāgatas — he, abiding in the yoga that holds fast to and preserves the Dharma of his own family, caused his own body to become invisible in the midst of that great assembly.

Then all the Tathāgatas of the ten directions, seeing that the bodhisattva Vajrapāṇi had become invisible within the assembly, proclaimed with one voice this solemn declaration:

"How wonderful! O Great Vajra-Holder,
You who are the agent of us all —
Now that you have vanished from the midst of this assembly,
O protector, where have you gone?"

Having said this, they all fell silent, abiding in the state of blessing of the supremely indestructible precious vajra, speaking not a word.

Then Vajrapāṇi, the Great Vajra-Holder, arising from the five abodes of all the Tathāgatas, rose up in the body of the Blessed One, the Great Vairocana. He consecrated with blessing the vast solar maṇḍala — the appearance of the minds of all the Tathāgatas manifesting as a great solar disc — extending it to the measure of a thousand leagues, and came to abide at its center.

Then all the Blessed Tathāgatas again assembled and made supplication to the lord of all the Tathāgatas:

"Because you are the vajra-Dharma, excellent are you!
Because you are the vajra-eye, excellent are you!
Because you are the vajra-wisdom, excellent are you!
Because you are the wisdom of the buddhas, excellent are you!"

And so it continues in the same manner.

Then all the Blessed Tathāgatas prostrated to the Blessed One, the Great Vajra-Holder, and addressed him thus:

"You who act for the benefit of all the buddhas —
We, the protectors of sentient beings, make this request:
O lord of all, please teach the way of the Great Vehicle,
the supreme path of the highest vehicle."

Then the Blessed Vajrapāṇi spoke to all the Tathāgatas:

"O Blessed Tathāgatas, do not say such things. All the Tathāgatas have already proclaimed, in definitive terms, the way of the supreme suchness of the highest joy."

"But why, O Vajra-Holder, do you proclaim it? Let all the Blessed Tathāgatas consider."

At that very moment, the great bodhisattvas in the vast assembly — Maitreya and the rest — upon hearing this great expression of the supreme meaning of the highest Great Vehicle, fell into a swoon in a thousand different ways. They made supplication to all the Tathāgatas:

"Through your blessing of pacification,
you who bear the form of the buddhas —
O lord, you who are the vajra, suchness itself —
let your blessing pervade all things equally."

Then the Blessed Vajrapāṇi, casting his gaze, bestowed the exclamation of "Excellent!" upon all the Tathāgatas, filled them with the radiance of the great wheel of all the Tathāgatas, and entered into meditative absorption called "Perfectly Rousing the Minds of All the Tathāgatas." The very moment he entered that absorption, four great solar maṇḍalas fully arose.

In the eastern direction there fully and perfectly arose a great solar maṇḍala named "Holder of Inexhaustible Form." In the southern direction there fully and perfectly arose a great solar maṇḍala named "Vajra of the Precious Wish-Fulfilling Jewel." In the western direction there fully and perfectly arose a great solar maṇḍala named "Vajra of Deathlessness." In the northern direction there fully and perfectly arose a great solar maṇḍala named "Vajra of the Pledge of Meaningful Purpose."

Then the Blessed Vajrapāṇi taught the great king of cognitions called "Victorious in Non-Duality, the Equality of All the Tathāgatas and All Bodhisattvas." And likewise, all the Tathāgatas called "Earnestly Engaged in Vajra Conduct" appeared in the ten directions from Vajrapāṇi himself in just the same way — together with their miraculous displays of supremely excellent qualities and the maṇḍala of their retinue endowed with those same qualities — and took their places in the ten directions.

In this way, all the world-systems of what is called the great trichiliocosm became enveloped in the light-rays of the sky's luminosity. The light of all the solar maṇḍalas combined made a radiance that could not be endured even for a single moment of companionship, and yet in that very manner the light of all the Tathāgatas became permeated and suffused with the light of Vajrasattva and pervaded everything everywhere.

Then the Blessed Tathāgatas worshipped Vajrapāṇi completely with clouds of offerings for bodhisattvas and for all the Tathāgatas, aroused him as Samantabhadra, and he abided in a mode of complete silence.

Then Samantabhadra, the great Vajradhara, placing this in his own heart among all the bodhisattvas, entered into equanimity in his own meditative absorption — the cause that, together with the great secret, clarifies the secret of all the Tathāgatas, which is the arousing of this gnosis-lineage — and taught the meditative absorption of the gnosis-lineage:

A square with four sides and four corners,
adorned with four jewels and perfectly complete,
spread throughout with milk and yogurt —
thus properly undertake the meditation.

In its center, a great lotus,
with radiance equal to crystal and moonlight,
endowed with a perfectly luminous maṇḍala —
meditate continuously upon this maṇḍala.

Within those blazing, gathered forms,
visualize the form of the buddha perfectly.
Entirely and perfectly rounded,
contemplate it as twenty cubits across.

One who knows the ritual should, in the center of that,
meditate upon one's own body as virtuous.
Properly undertake the meditation
having abandoned conceptuality of self and what belongs to self.

Through the ritual of the gnosis-lineage,
hold the secret mantra in mind:

Oṃ sarva-tathāgata-jñāna-kula-svabhāvātmako 'ham.

By gathering the equal mudrā through this,
let all misdeeds be utterly abandoned.

First visualize ten thousand light-rays,
and through that purify all defilements.
Through the ritual of the gnosis-lineage,
meditate perfectly upon the king of knowledge.

Fine, blazing white light —
arrange that everywhere.
Make everything into suchness,
and thus meditate.

This was spoken by the Blessed Tathāgatas as the vajra empowerment.

Having made one's own body supremely stable,
through the rituals of the mode of light,
by gathering the form of the buddha together,
the awakening mind of vajra is indicated and aroused.

If one undertakes this properly, knowing the ritual,
the siddhi of that will not be difficult to find.
Having made one's own form perfectly unmoving,
perform the rituals of the vajra awakening mind.
As long as the sky is contemplated,
perform these with joy, knowing the ritual.

With the form of the buddha and one's own form alike,
having made them, through the characteristics of total union —

At the time of the yoga of meditative concentration,
one should fully enter into that which is to be perfected.
One's own form and the form of the buddha —
hold them in mind just as they abide.
Whatever is fitting to apply,
meditate on it as Vajrasattva (the vajra bodhisattva).
The lord of all Tathāgatas,
the Blessed One, declared this:
"The yogin who meditates on their own body
as the blazing garland of shimmering light —
through the very nature of vajra,
meditate on that as vajra-inseparable.
Having fully emitted the five lights,
perfectly cultivate the awakening mind.
At the heart center,
make offerings to all buddhas."
The lord of all Tathāgatas,
the Blessed One, declared this:
"Contemplate the five maṇḍalas.
Having filled them with the gathered forms of the buddhas,
and having cultivated the mantras,
make them like reflected images of the buddha forms,
and meditate on the very nature of one who meditates.
Contemplate one's own form there as well.
Through the ritual of arousing conceptual thought,
at the mind center also cultivate the awakening mind —
from that, yoga arises in the one who meditates.
Adorned with Vajrasattva,
through knowing the ritual, draw it forth.
And there also, in Vajrasattva,
perfectly visualize the form of the buddha.
Through knowing the ritual, perfectly visualize
the form of the buddha in the subtle syllables.
And in each and every syllable,
perfectly visualize the form of the buddha.
Together with those forms and the two syllables,
equally contemplate one's own form in union.
In the manner of the solar maṇḍala,
the wise one should contemplate their own form.
For as long as the sky has expanse,
let it be spread to pervade this field.
In the manner of the solar maṇḍala,
perfectly cultivate the vajra yoga.
In the applications:
perfectly visualize the form of the buddha.
In the manner of the solar maṇḍala,
let it radiate into this world-realm.
For as long as the sky has expanse,
let it be spread to pervade this field.
In the manner of the lunar maṇḍala,
from the cultivation of the solar maṇḍala,
through the manner of the solar maṇḍala,
perfectly visualize the form of the buddha.
Having perfectly visualized the form of the buddha,
contemplate the lunar maṇḍala.
In the manner of the lunar maṇḍala,
perfectly visualize the form of the buddha."

O King of Dharma,
through the rituals of the lotus yoga,
examine all meditations.
From the complete holding of meditation and the like,
there is no worship to be done other than that.
Having perfectly visualized the form of the buddha
with a mind made utterly stable and firm,
perfectly generate the awakening mind
and continuously meditate on one's own nature.
This is the peaceful mode of vajra.
Light equal to the four sharp ones —
having perfectly visualized Vajrasattva,
through knowing the ritual, fill it completely.

Thus spoke the Blessed One Samantabhadra.

Then Samantabhadra, the great Vajradhara, was drawn forth from the mind of all the bodhisattvas. Then those great bodhisattvas, having filled the entire expanse of space, perfectly made offerings to all the bodhisattvas with clouds of offering, and remained continuously present in a state of prostration before the Blessed One, the great Vajradhara.

Then all the Tathāgatas gathered together once more and perfectly made offerings to the great bodhisattva Samantabhadra with great clouds of offerings to all the Tathāgatas, and prostrated. Fully entering into the vajra of awakening mind, they completely filled everything with the light rays of all the Tathāgatas, and those rays of light perfectly stirred the true source of all the Tathāgatas.

Then Samantabhadra, the great Vajradhara, having firmly established all the Tathāgatas, consecrated his own form as the form of Vairocana, which is the cause that expands this family of wisdom. With the light rays of the appearance of his own form he drew forth the chapter on the meditative absorption of the family of wisdom, the essential samaya of all the Tathāgatas.

Accomplishing all of that very suchness,
through the defining marks of unified concentration,
one who knows the rite should undertake it with full resolve.
This is the pure way of suchness.

Contemplate the supreme solar maṇḍala
as measuring one thousand cubits.
In the center of the lunar maṇḍala,
meditate upon the syllable VAṂ.

Perfectly round on all sides,
conceive it as roughly thumb-high.
One who knows the rite, regarding that which blazes,
should meditate upon it in its various and diverse colors.

From that, true blazing arises.
Then visualize the form of a buddha.
In all directions, the syllable OṂ
should be conceived in various and diverse colors.

By meditating in turn on Vairocana and the rest,
one who knows the rite should bring them into being.
In the center of the solar maṇḍala,
meditate upon the lunar maṇḍala.

Contemplating oneself in that,
from the rite that generates joy,
at the center of the heart, with fine needle-point,
on the stainless lunar maṇḍala,
with forms of the fine syllables of mantra,
contemplate each one in the mind.

Perfectly unite the radiance of OṂ
with the blazing garland of equal light.
Through the defining marks of the form of a buddha and so forth,
one who knows the rite should cause them to issue forth.

In all the forms of buddhas,
meditating on the lunar maṇḍala —
from the rite for generating buddhas —
one should apply it to one's own form in that way.

From the rite performed at the vajra-heart,
five suns are to be arrayed.
The mantrin should meditate on the lunar maṇḍala
visualized upon the forehead.

Meditate on the blazing radiance of equal light
as the form of a buddha.
Passing progressively beyond the world-realms,
one should then draw them back into it again.

By what exceeds the measure of a cubit,
meditating on the lunar maṇḍala,
Vajrasattva is perfectly generated.
Meditate on it as one's own very form.

Thus spoke the Blessed One Samantabhadra.
This is the meditative absorption called Victorious in the Family of Wisdom.

In the center of the solar maṇḍala,
meditate with virtue on one's own form.
To the side, place Vairocana,
and meditate upon Vajrasattva.

Thus spoke the Blessed One Vajrasattva.

One who knows the rite should contemplate
sixteen forms in four colors.
At the heart center and throughout all of them,
meditate upon those deities.

Thus spoke the Blessed One Vajradhara.

To conquer obstructing forces in one's own form,
meditate upon it steadfastly in the mind.
Having emitted the syllable HŪṂ with the out-breath,
direct the light rays forth fully.

Thus spoke the Blessed One, all the Tathāgatas.

Light equal to one's own form and so forth
is instantly transformed into the deity.
Through the measure of the syllables,
meditate upon the form of VAṂ.

The mantrin at the center, by means of the syllable itself,
having applied it, should undertake all activities.
The mantrin should recollect from the forehead
down as far as the navel.

Here ends the first chapter, the chapter on arrangement, from the Great Tantra of the Non-Dual Equality, the Victorious One.

Meditate upon the great lotus
to the measure of a chariot-wheel.
In the center of the solar maṇḍala,
meditate upon the stalk of that lotus.

Generate perfectly the karma-vajra,
and meditate upon it as one's own form.
Through deep meditation on the karma-vajra,
one becomes equal to Vajrasattva.

Thus spoke the Blessed One Vajrasattva.

Having made use of milk and yogurt,
meditate on the lotus at the center.
Making one's own form like a buddha,
the skilled one should meditate on one's own form.

Fill the element of space entirely,
and then apply that very suchness once again.
Having blended milk and yogurt together,
the light of variegated colors equally arrayed,
in the manner of a lunar maṇḍala,
meditate upon the solar maṇḍala.
One whose own form equals a buddha —
let the skilled practitioner in ritual procedure draw it forth.

Meditate on one's own form everywhere,
visualizing gods and the like throughout.
When one has gone beyond the measure of a cubit,
meditate upon the solar maṇḍala.

Having acted as one who vanquishes all obstructors,
let this be spread throughout the world-realm.
Contemplating one's own mudrā at the heart,
at the center of the solar maṇḍala —
knowing the ritual procedure in the manner of a buddha,
meditate upon a maṇḍala of light.
Bestowing the syllable at its center,
let this be spread throughout the world-realm here.
Having made it possess the capacity to tame beings and so forth,
apply it once again to that very suchness.

When one has gone beyond the measure of a cubit,
meditate upon the lunar maṇḍala.
Perfectly generate the vajra-sword,
and traverse the world-realm in its entirety.
The radiance of the white lotus blazes forth,
adorned entirely with bodhicitta.
At the center, place the form of one's own deity;
at the sides, place the consort.
Thus grasp it through knowing the ritual procedure,
and introduce one's own vajra into it.
Throughout all world-realms,
worship with that very buddha-suchness.

Thus spoke the Blessed One Samantabhadra.

For as long as genuine attainment through joining arises,
the mantra practitioner should again gather it in.
When one has gone beyond the measure of a full span,
meditate upon the solar maṇḍala.
Having made one's own form extremely stable,
visualize gods and the like.
Bestowing the mantra syllable at the heart,
worship all buddhas.

When one has gone beyond seven spans in measure,
meditate upon the four maṇḍalas.
Abiding at the center of the deity's form,
meditate upon that at the heart.

Thus spoke the Blessed One Vajrasamaya.

Making one's own form into what is called the eye,
meditate upon buddhas in full.
At the very heart and throughout everything,
let the skilled one meditate on one's own form.
There, in the manner of an essence,
meditate fully upon the bodhisattva's yoga.

When one has gone beyond the measure of an arrow-shot,
meditate fully upon the form of a buddha.
Fill the sky with that form,
and from the essences generate perfectly.
When one has gone beyond the measure of an arrow-shot,
meditate upon the maṇḍala of clarity.
Having firmly established one's own yoga,
meditate upon the perfect yoga of the buddha.

When one has gone beyond the measure of an arrow-shot,
meditate fully upon bodhicitta.
Having perfectly generated the deity,
meditate fully upon the mantra syllables.

When one has gone beyond the measure of a full span,
meditate in the manner of a lotus.
Having made one's own body immaculate,
meditate upon the maṇḍala of clarity.

Having firmly established the center of that,
meditate upon one's own form from the very beginning.
Visualize fully the five lotuses
along with their garlands of blazing white light.
At the center of that, place one's own form;
at the sides, place the consort.
One's own seminal essence and the rest arise,
and let the skilled practitioner in ritual procedure spread it forth.

Meditating upon the element of space
filled entirely with the forms of buddhas,
having made one's own form like a sword,
place it at the hearts of all.
Apply one's own form in perfect union with a buddha,
proceeding in sequence through the defining marks and so forth.
As a bodhisattva, meditate fully
upon the element of space filled perfectly.

Apply one's own form with the perfect noose,
and place the form of the all-pervading buddha.
For as long as it remains within the maṇḍala of space,
draw it in through knowing the ritual procedure.
Apply one's own form in perfect union with a buddha,
and meditate fully upon bodhicitta.
Apply mantra syllables and so forth,
and swiftly meditate upon that manifestation.

Generate bodhicitta perfectly,
adorned with blazing rays of light.
One's own form equal to a buddha —

Draw it in through knowing the ritual procedure.
Upon that very form of oneself, let form come to rest,
with radiance equal to a blazing garland of light —
this is called the Eye, the one that delights the mind.
Draw it in through knowing the ritual procedure.
Dwelling in the center of the lunar maṇḍala,
place the Eye perfectly at the heart.
Through knowing the ritual procedure, with rays of light,
introduce Vajrasattva fully therein.

Thus spoke the Blessed One Vajrasattva of all the Tathāgatas.

Within the swift maṇḍala of one's own form,
generate Vajrasattva perfectly.
Unite fully in the divine form.
Diligent meditation is not other than this.

In the center of the solar maṇḍala,
generate Vajrasattva perfectly.
With the form of the iron hook, the noose, and so forth,
having done so, draw the disciple fully in.

Generate Vajrasattva perfectly.
Place one's own form perfectly at the heart.
Having placed it so, with the hands and the rest,
then commence the count precisely.
Recitation and so forth, or what is to be consumed —
let the intelligent one do accordingly.
Practitioners shall perform continuous recitation.
Otherwise, accomplishment will simply not occur.

By the pledge of all the Blessed Tathāgatas, thus was it spoken.

Generate one's own form perfectly.
In the center, meditate upon one's own family.
And finally, for the destruction of enemies and obstructors,
meditate upon the essence there.
Through the ritual procedure of these,
the sequential order of the ritual — meditation and so forth —
in the manner of the solar maṇḍala,
meditate fully upon the king of awareness.

Meditate in the form of a wrathful one,
blazing with a garland of flames, a churning conflagration.
Recite that very color as it truly is.
Recite that very suchness as the ultimate.
Contemplate that very suchness with that very suchness.
Otherwise, accomplishment will not occur.

By all the Blessed Tathāgatas, thus did Samantabhadra speak.

Having made a maṇḍala of one's own form,
in the center, with radiance equal to blazing fire,
throughout all world-realms everywhere,
knowing that very suchness, place it at the heart.
Upon that, generate oneself perfectly.
Commence upon its very intrinsic nature.

With one's own form dwelling in the center of a lotus,
with radiance equal to Vairocana and the rest,
having formed a syllable at the heart,
meditate fully upon Vajrasattva.

In the assembly of Vajrasattva,
meditate fully in the form of a buddha.
Generate perfectly in the manner of awareness,
and all accomplishments shall be obtained.

Therefore, for the mantra practitioner, the supreme
shall be the excellent mode of awareness.

By all the Blessed Tathāgatas, thus did Vajrasattva speak.

The practice of making everything suchness:
in the center of the solar maṇḍala,
contemplate the form as Vairocana,
and meditate fully upon the mantra at the heart.
Recollect that very suchness in the mantra.
From the ritual of the mode of awareness,
with the mind of the master filled with delight,
draw it in through knowing the ritual procedure.

At the hearts and throughout all things,
meditate upon the lunar maṇḍala.
Generate one's own form perfectly,
and upon it meditate upon the sword and so forth.
Generate perfectly through knowing the ritual procedure.
Meditate fully upon the five blazing forms.
Recognize form and color completely,
and send these forth throughout world-realms.

By all the Blessed Tathāgatas, thus did the Eye speak.

This is the second chapter, the chapter on the meditative absorption of the wisdom family, from the Great King of Tantras, the Great Royal Tantra of Thought Victorious in Non-Dual Equality.

Then the Blessed One Vajrapāṇi, dwelling in the lunar maṇḍala of all the Tathāgatas, blessed it with the form of Vairocana, and they too were established in their own forms within those great assembled maṇḍalas.

Through the meditative absorption of the condensed family,
the Blessed One bestowed the chapters of instruction.
Meditating upon milk and curds,
along with the four noble pledges,
in the center, the mantra practitioner meditates upon the utpala lotus —
let one not contemplate otherwise.
Perfectly unite the wisdom maṇḍala.

Above that, one meditates upon the various forms.
Perfectly generate the wrathful form.
Meditate upon the perfect union of rays of light.
There, in the gatherings of rays of light,
meditate upon Vajrasattva.
One who knows the ritual places one's own form
perfectly in the center and meditates upon it.

Thus spoke the Blessed One Samayavajra.

Oṃ sarva-tathāgata-jñāna-svabhāvātmako 'haṃ.

By means of this king of secret mantras,
one should completely abandon one's own conceptual thought.
Having declared "my own form is the syllable Hūṃ,"
one then meditates upon it in every respect.
One who knows the ritual should, in those as well,
contemplate the syllable Ha as the ultimate end.
Light equal in nature to one's own form and so forth —
the deity transforms in that instant.
Perfectly unite the luminous maṇḍala.
Meditate upon it at the heart center.
One who knows the ritual, seeing the rite,
should meditate upon the syllable of the secret mantra.

Thus spoke the Blessed One Samayavajra.

What is to be perfectly applied at the five places —
meditate upon it at the five points.
At every syllable of the mantra,
meditate upon Vajrasattva.
Having meditated upon the forms of the buddhas,
one who knows the ritual should emanate them.

Then the Blessed One Vajrapāṇi, uniting his own vajra with the bhaga of all the queens in the manner of perfect union, thereby blessed and consecrated to the full measure of his own form. At that very moment, all the Tathāgatas likewise, to that exact same measure, with the wisdom of the skilled masters of former times, declared the great wisdom vow called "Vajrapāṇi of All Families Blessing the Suchness of the Wisdom of the Vow."

Through the mode of the four maṇḍalas,
one should meditate upon the mantra in the center.
Having made a maṇḍala in the heart,
one should meditate upon Vajrasattva.

The lord of all Tathāgatas, the Blessed One, declared this.

Through the mode of the four maṇḍalas,
one meditates upon Vairocana and the rest.
Having generated all through suchness itself,
one then makes offering to that very suchness.

Through the mode of the four maṇḍalas,
one should contemplate Vajrasattva.
One perfectly generates the form of a buddha
and is said to make offering to it.

Through the mode of the four maṇḍalas,
one meditates upon one's own family and the rest.
Having stabilized the form in the center,
one is said to make offering to it.

Having perfectly generated the eye
adorned with a variety of ornaments,
through the mode of the luminous maṇḍala,
one should meditate upon the white common-folk family.

Bearing the characteristics of total union and the rest,
perfectly adorned with manifold ornaments,
resembling a blue lotus —
one should always meditate upon Māmakī.

Like a garland of flowers and such in their proper meaning,
adorned appropriately as is fitting —
she is renowned as the great Vidyā, the white one;
one should contemplate her as resembling blood.

Excellently adorned with the splendid total union of mind,
gathered and ornate —
the auspicious one who is like a lotus:
the mantra practitioner should always contemplate her.

The Blessed One Samantabhadra declared this.

Through the total union of butter and yellow mustard and the like,
one should meditate upon that which is pleasing.
That which performs the union through the mind using alcohol —
while meditating upon that very thing,
in the center of the lunar maṇḍala,
one perfectly generates through one's own mantra.
By means of the one who performs the expulsion, all the rites —
this is to be generated and meditated upon.

With the hand, one grasps the throat and other such places;
the feet are to be joined there to it.
With the defining characteristics of two flowers and so forth —

One should meditate upon inserting one's own vajra.
One perfectly performs the union in the lunar maṇḍala.
The seminal fluid is to be caused to fall there.
Thus did the Blessed One, Vajradhara, declare.

Having filled the entire expanse of space,
one should then meditate upon suchness itself.
With the fourfold flower and so forth, according to the proper method —
it is intended that one performs worship therein.

Through the offering of the five enjoyments,
the master of worship should perform worship through the union of all.
Fragrance, form, taste, and touch —
all of these are bestowed through suchness itself.

The vajra-minds, by the learned one,
together with the generation performed by all the buddhas,
are to be excellently proclaimed for the sake of pleasing and worshipping.
And they are spoken of as being supremely joyful.

By this, the accomplishment of secret mantra —
the practitioners shall be victorious.
For the sake of effecting the illuminating and expanding accomplishment,
buddhahood itself and so forth are perfectly attained.

If the nature of reality is unchanging,
this is the nature of reality of the buddhas.
The full grasping of buddhahood itself and so forth —
otherwise it cannot be attained.

Non-virtue of mind is not to be performed;
conduct that is to be condemned is not to be performed.
This is gathered from all the unchanging nature of reality of all the buddhas.

Whoever the other queens may be,
they are to enjoy desirable pleasures.

The union with the solar maṇḍala
is to be worshipped as befits the occasion.
Generosity, moral discipline, and likewise patience —
are also said to be taught as diligence.

This is to be dwelt in with superior ceremony
by the learned mantra practitioner.
One's own mantra is to be perfectly recited
and the union is to be performed as though like the moon.
There, having gathered it into the vajra,
again the mantra practitioner should cause it to be inserted.
At the side, one should meditate upon the jewel
and perform that very act at the seed syllable.

Through the collected colors of those,
one should come to understand the distinctions of the family.
And common to all three families,
the qualities of the Vajrasattva arise.
One should perfectly generate the syllables thereof,
and by diligently taking up meditative concentration upon the insertion,
through the distinctions of activities and so forth,
the defining characteristics of the rites are well renowned.

For the wisdom family, the blue lotus —
it is also renowned to those who know its condensed meaning.
For the family of desirable worship, the lotus —
and it is likewise intended for the Dharma family.

By one who knows the rite, a queen of ten years
is understood for the wisdom family.
She who is to be examined under the term condensed
must be of twelve years, endowed with qualities and radiance.

The mantra practitioner should always, with knowledge of Dharma,
earnestly take up one who is sixteen years of age.
For all the families of desirable worship,
one who is twenty years old is to be relied upon.

Thus did the Blessed One, the maṇḍala of the pledges of all the Tathāgatas, declare.

As long as the seminal fluid is perfectly gathered,
one who is twelve years of age is to be employed.
In brief, whatever is desired
must be fully endowed with various ornaments,
adorned with garlands of flowers and the like —
this is to be performed as is fitting.

Having performed Vairocana and so forth,
one then properly begins the worship.
Through the distinctions of the families, the rites —
sandal and saffron are to be excellently proclaimed.
One who abides at ease and has been made beautiful —
that very maṇḍala should perform that action.
The full act of gathering is to be performed.
If one does not know the distinctions according to their division,
then even buddhahood will not be accomplished.

Thus did the mind of the Blessed One, of all the Tathāgatas, declare.

Seven girls adorned with all marks,
made to be adorned with ornaments,
of the appearance of sixteen years —
as long as they are taken up to eighteen years of age.
Not excessively tall nor short,
not slight, nor born of inferior birth —
an incomparable young woman is to be accomplished.
Otherwise accomplishment will not come.

One who knows the rite should take hold of her
and accomplish it through that very means of accomplishment.
In Jambudvīpa, adorned with all the defining characteristics
of melodious voice and so forth —
if one accomplishes a sixteen-year-old who knows the rite,
accomplishment will come; there is no doubt here.

A maṇḍala measuring four cubits is to be drawn
and accomplishment is to be practised upon it continually.

By means of the ritual of vidyā,
the expert should recite five hundred thousand times.
By the measure of lamps and so forth —
otherwise accomplishment will not come.

Thus spoke the Blessed One, the buddha Samantaprabha.

In a maṇḍala of one cubit's measure,
perfectly generate Vajrasattva.
The great wrathful one called Amṛta —
bestow him and then begin the actual activities.

One who knows the rite should arrange
the signs there and accomplish it.
Making offerings of incense, flowers, and the like,
one should accomplish it — not otherwise.

By lakes, on pleasant riverbanks,
and in solitary mountain retreats as well,
one who knows the rite, gathering these,
should draw the maṇḍala oneself.

Worship with all precious things
is the worship of Vajrasattva;
that is described as the worship
of the protector Avalokiteśvara.

Passing beyond the first watch,
abiding fully in the second,
the yogin should strive in accomplishment —
and that very suchness will be accomplished.

Arising rightly in the half-watch,
and throughout all the intervals up to five —
blessed by Vajrapāṇi,
the practitioner will always succeed.

Having become like a sovereign of the family,
he moves through all world-realms
with innumerable consorts, by the volume of their conduct,
so as to realise accomplishment.

By his activity, buddhahood itself —
Vajrapāṇi, the source of all things,
as numerous as the dust-motes of Mount Meru —
is what acts for the sake of sentient beings.

This completes the eighth chapter, the chapter on the secret worship, the great sādhana of all samayas, in the great King of Tantras, the Great Royal Tantra of Non-Dual Equality, the Victorious Thought.

Then all the Tathāgatas gathered once again and addressed the bodhisattva Vajrapāṇi with these words: "Blessed One, please teach how the practitioners of the great secret mantra samaya called Penetrating the Wisdom of All the Tathāgatas will, through the wisdom of the samaya called the Playful Lion, swiftly awaken to the unsurpassed, perfect, and complete awakening."

Then Vajrapāṇi, lord of all the Tathāgatas, having heard the words of those Tathāgatas, placed his vajra upon his own heart and addressed those Tathāgatas with these words: "Blessed Tathāgatas, why do you say this? Blessed One, there is none who can explain all that the Tathāgatas have definitively set forth. Moreover, Blessed One, for this one the rays of wisdom would shatter into a thousand pieces. Otherwise it would turn dark."

Having done just that, the Blessed One Vajrapāṇi thereupon caused all those Tathāgatas to abide in their own forms upon the lunar disc at the heart and upon the crown of the head of all the Tathāgatas.

Then the Blessed One Vajrapāṇi declared the samaya of the secret mantra called Penetrating the Wisdom Samaya:

All the countless beings —
slay them by applying suchness.
In this very life buddhahood
will be attained; of this there is no doubt.

Full assemblies of ten million buddhas —
slay them by applying wisdom.
In this very life it will be accomplished;
Vajrasattva will be attained.

By the measure of the great lotus,
crush the heaps of sentient beings.
Dwelling in meditative absorption on the suchness of phenomena,
through destruction accomplishment will be obtained.

One who knows the rite should slay the guide
who destroys by the measure of the lotus.
By meditating on suchness as the desired offering —

— by that, great accomplishment is proclaimed.
The Blessed One, through the purity of essential nature, spoke thus.

Fill the expanse of space to the full;
by that characteristic mark, slay.
That very suchness is buddhahood here —
in this very life alone,
all the varied aspects are obtained.

The mind of sentient beings through ritual
is to be trained again and again.
The identity of slaying and so forth arising —
otherwise, attainment will not occur.

The mind of all sentient beings,
luminous as the fourth clarity —
unstained by the various things of substance —
meditate on that suchness spoken of as mind.

One who knows the rite should fully enjoy
the suchness that is the Mother of buddhas.
All virtue arising from secret mantra —
otherwise, accomplishment will not occur.

Let the entire company of mantra-practitioners
extract the supreme gathering completely.
The arising of virtue in Vajrasattva —
otherwise, it will not become a source of joy.

Enjoy fully, always; undertake all enjoyment.
If otherwise, by one's own bewilderment
one will be tormented as if in Avīci.

One should eat everything oneself;
the pure liquor, the meat — drink and eat them oneself.
This becomes the gift of all the buddhas;
swift accomplishment will occur.

Always eat the great flesh;
sesame oil too may be drunk on its own.
One who knows the rite — whether urine or excrement —
let the one of eating-nature eat it.
Even that which has issued from the wound's opening
should be offered to the buddhas and the rest.

Having made a great cloud of flowers and sweet fragrance,
and all things according to their purpose,
through the mantra-practitioner's constant enjoyment by way of tasting,
let the drinking proceed.

This is the samaya spoken of by the wisdom-mind of all buddhas.
Since the Blessed One, the Tathāgatas of all kinds, sanction this, they spoke thus.

Let the mantra-practitioner always use the maṇḍala —
be it with urine and excrement, each separately,
with lying down and so forth —
and resolve the intention very firmly.

Having generated the four-cornered form filled with urine and excrement,
fill the center of that form of one's own body
from the tip of the nose inward.

The arising of the Blessed One, the buddhas,
by that means — the master of passion — is without difficulty.
Whatever other three have been gathered —
let them not remain here in separation.

One who knows the rite should afterward
do this here and combine it with others.
Urged on by the wheel of wisdom,
one proceeds to that vast accomplishment.

Whether oneself or another —
suchness is not to be conjoined.
As long as space gives rise to all things,
buddhahood itself is not obtained.

I am Avīci, I am fearsome;
I am suffering, I am bliss.
Aversion is in me, and likewise delusion;
I am attachment, dwelling each in its place.

Thus it is said.

This concludes the ninth chapter, the great supreme secret, from the Great Royal Tantra of Thought called "Triumph of Non-Dual Equality: Making the Wisdom of All Tathāgatas Enter Within."

Then once more the Blessed One Vajrapāṇi spoke the great secret samaya of the essence of all bodhisattvas — Vajrasattva in its entirety — through the causes and fruit of obtaining the wisdom of all Tathāgatas:

One should not bow to an arhat.
Nor to a monk, though one may bow.
The buddhahood of buddhas —
attaining that is the bow; what is bowed to is not.

Contemplating one's own form by one's own form,
through that very suchness in the rite —
having placed the vajra and the rest at the heart —
combine it correctly with the object of offering.

Even if Vajrasattva,
or some among them, should later transgress this —
one who has spoken of one's own awakening mind
will have one's head split into seven.

Having placed the liṅga (Skt. liṅga) firmly in the bhaga (Skt. bhaga),
recite the secret mantra again and again.
Generate Vajrasattva completely
and meditate upon one's own form.

From the distinction of always protecting
that very buddha, that very guru —

Otherwise, the masters will not attain buddhahood itself. Having fully meditated on one's own form, from that very suchness one generates the subtle form — the awakening mind — completely. One should meditate upon the master as a buddha. This gnosis of the practitioners arises precisely from suchness as samaya (Skt. samaya). Through this engagement with samaya and the rest, even buddhahood will not be accomplished. Through the supreme yoga of the mudrā-seal (Skt. mudrā), one should meditate on a form at one's own heart. There, generate the vajra completely and stabilize it firmly for as long as one desires. Possessed of regard for the proper time and so forth, this is the supreme samaya. For as long as one is to accomplish the siddhis, one should always guard it by that means. The Blessed One spoke thus through the awakening mind.

Generate one's own form completely. By the defining mark of one's own form and so forth, what is declared as the nectar (Skt. amṛta) at the heart — from that, set forth the mantra correctly. This too is the samaya-gnosis, the purification of all defilements. The siddhi of desire, once stabilized, should be guarded with the effort of conduct and discipline. Having fully meditated upon all the buddhas, one should contemplate the form of what is to be accomplished. Generate Vajrasattva completely and meditate upon its essential nature. One who knows the rite should always meditate on this in the three times as well. If one were to recite or accomplish it otherwise, that would not be the genuine thing. Generate the precious bodhisattva completely — the consort (Skt. vidyā) who possesses form and all its attributes, adorned with a blazing garland — and meditate fully upon her in his hand. While meditating in the condensed form, one will come to clearly perceive the siddhi. If, in contrast, one is deluded, that generosity of his will not be accomplished there. Having placed the utterly brilliant, utterly stainless, wisdom-jewel crown upon the head, one who is supreme among practitioners moves about together on the earth. "I, Vajrasattva, the siddhi, am blessed by Vajrasattva. I shall always lead the way to the stage of vajra-wisdom-Dharma." "I, the siddhi, am blessed by vajra-wisdom as amṛta and so forth. I, the excellent one, exceedingly subtle, supreme as space, engage in discernment." The Blessed One, the great Vajradhara, spoke thus.

Then the Blessed One Vajrapāṇi once again proclaimed the samaya of all Tathāgatas — the chapter called "Blessing the Suchness of the Perfectly Pure Secret Mantra of the Body, Speech, Mind, and Gnosis of All Tathāgatas."

Generate Vajrasattva;
declare it as "the vajra of mind."
Having meditated upon that in the mind,
fully introduce the vajra-gnosis.

The meaning of the pure nature of the vajra —
fully examine it as the vajra of speech.
Having fully meditated on the pure gnosis,
meditate fully on purity of body and speech.

The blessing of the vajra is supremely clear;
it is the indivisible vajra-body itself.
The very nature of the buddha's body —
bless it, knowing the rite.

Blessed by this vajra,
one thereby attains awakening.
Knowing the rite, in six months
attain buddhahood right here.

From all those vajras unstained
by the phenomena of passion arising —
having thus performed the suchness-rite,
all siddhis are gathered.

Then Vajrapāṇi proclaimed the samaya of all-pervading conduct of the gnosis of all Tathāgatas.

Through the mode of the unfailing,
from the arising of the three vajra-gnoses,
by fully enjoying all of these,
one becomes the conduct of all buddhas.

If the buddhas, who fill the expanse of space,
are overcome —
buddhahood in this very birth,
condensed, is the vajra-master.

In the center of the solar maṇḍala,
the vajra form is perfectly generated.
Filling the expanse of space
with the form of the buddhas, one fully cultivates.

At the heart, the mantra is perfectly generated;
by meditative equipoise one sets it in motion.
The samaya of universal delight —
this the vajra-master ever cultivates.

Abiding in the center of the solar maṇḍala,
one's own mantra is perfectly generated;
filling the expanse of space with form,
one cultivates everything at the heart.

Through practicing that very thing itself,
one abides in the rank of the buddhas' own proclamation.

Abiding in the center of the lunar maṇḍala,
one's own mantra is perfectly generated;
knowing the rite, one cultivates the form
in the five abodes of aspect.

The awakening mind is perfectly generated;
all of these are set in motion by one who holds the vow.

In the center of the Dharma maṇḍala,
one's own mantra is perfectly generated;
having thus cultivated it as a diadem,
one makes offering and cultivates.

The buddha-master who possesses bliss —
that itself is the bliss of the yogin.
Having abandoned all terror entirely,
the mantra-bearer moves like a lion.

Buddhahood in this very birth —
in that instant one is definitively joined.

The Blessed One, the Tathāgata, proclaimed this with the samaya of all Tathāgatas. This is the tenth chapter, the chapter on samaya, in the Great King of Tantras called Victory over All in the Non-Dual Equality.

Then all the Tathāgatas arrived through their gathering, and with an array of offering clouds of the body, speech, mind, and gnosis of all Tathāgatas, they made perfect offering to the Blessed One Vajrapāṇi and spoke thus:

The meditative absorption that accomplishes all siddhis,
the meditative absorption that has become the supreme maṇḍala,
the great maṇḍala that has become supreme —
that itself: may the Blessed One explain it.

Then the Blessed One Vajrapāṇi, having heard these words of supplication from all the Tathāgatas, entered into meditative absorption called "The Vajra Blessing of the Samaya of All Tathāgatas," and in order to spread universally the gnosis of the siddhi of all samayas, taught this great maṇḍala.

Then, that which is rightly to be explained —
the supreme great maṇḍala:
it is like vajra-gnosis,
making the awakening of buddhas fully luminous.

It is to be contemplated as milk and yogurt;
it is adorned nearby with fine lotuses;
filled with clusters of flowers of every variety —
with utmost effort one should cultivate it.

Let the wise examine it thoroughly at the four sides and four corners.
It has arisen perfectly from Jāmbūnada gold;
in that, at the center, one cultivates.

It is to be made sixteen cubits across;
the buddha-master abides in the rite.
Knowing the rite, one cultivates in the corners
the five-pointed summit.

In the five locations as is fitting,
examine the maṇḍala of the moon.
Those who cultivate maṇḍalas
generate it perfectly in a single hand.

As is fitting, in the east
one fully examines Akṣobhya.
Knowing the rite, in the south
one now sets out Ratnasambhava.
The west is called Amitābha;
the north is Amoghasiddhi.

Knowing the rite, by the golden hue,
one cultivates by meditating on that very suchness.
At the center, the all-pervading lord Vairocana
is perfectly generated and cultivated.

In all the aspects of the disciple,
knowing the rite, one fully cultivates.
The great awareness goddesses — Locanā and the others —
one fully cultivates in the corners themselves.

Knowing the rite, what is called "nectar"
one cultivates at all the gates.
Through the condensed colors of all of these,
one understands them each individually.

In all their forms —

one fully cultivates as Vajrasattva.
The five rays of light are perfectly generated.
Through the condensation of the five sense pleasures,
one makes offering to the all-pervading lord.

According to the rite of the buddhācarya,
one fully cultivates, meditating that the space-realm
is completely filled by all buddhas.
This is renowned as cultivating worship
through all the seminal essences.

Thus is complete the meditative maṇḍala called "The Accomplishment of All Pledges."

Then the Blessed One Vajrapāṇi again taught the meditative maṇḍala called "The Inviolable Pledge," as follows:

The intelligent buddhācarya
does all things just as they are in suchness.
By way of the subtle maṇḍala method,
one fully cultivates everything therein.

The supreme of all phenomena in the east —
Vajrasattva — is to be placed.
The mantra-practitioner examines the south:
Vajrasattva is to be placed there.
In the west, perfect awakening as primordial wisdom —
the buddhācarya is to be placed.
In the north, he who terrifies all —
Vajrasattva is to be placed.
At the center, the equalizer of the three realms —
one fully cultivates in the form of vajra.

Having cultivated in the white color,
one then meditates on the black at the center.
The queens, having opened,
one inserts the vajra into that very suchness.
The sovereign lady of Vajrasattva —
through effort, in the four abodes.

According to what is fitting, in all their colors,
one fully cultivates in the form of buddhas.
Knowing the rite, in all directions,
one meditates on the supreme wrathful ones.
The mantra-practitioner, through the five sense pleasures,
properly undertakes mantra-worship.

One fully cultivates, meditating that the space-realm
is completely filled by Vajrasattva.
With the seminal essence of all vajras and so forth,
one meditates on it as worship.

Thus is complete the meditative maṇḍala called "The Inviolable Pledge."

Then Vajrapāṇi once more entered into the meditative absorption (samādhi) called "The Vajra Blessing, the Arising of the Pledges of All Tathāgatas," and taught this tathāgata maṇḍala, the great secret of the great meditative absorption called "The Phenomenon of Great Desire":

one cultivates everything therein.
Knowing the rite, in the east,
one places the blazing dharma-holder.
In the south, wisdom-phenomena now —
one cultivates through the nature of the meditator.
In the west, the lotus of enjoyment.
In the north, born from the dharmadhātu.

Having cultivated fittingly,
one places the lord of phenomena at the center.
Having cultivated in white,
one meditates on the red at the center.
Through the rite, the queen of the lord of phenomena

The great wrathful one called Horse —

In all the yoga of phenomena,
one makes offering through the five sense pleasures.
Vajrasattva is perfectly generated
and fully cultivated at the sides.
With seminal essence itself, the space-realm —

As befits, through union with the dharmakāya,

Thus is complete the great secret of the great meditative absorption called "The Phenomenon of Desire."

Then Vajrapāṇi once more entered into the meditative absorption called "The Vajra, Source of All Pledges," and taught this great meditative maṇḍala called "The Mode of All Desire":

By way of the body-maṇḍala method,
one cultivates everything therein.
In the east, the definite destroyer of enemies —
one meditates on him in variegated colors.
In the south, perfect union with flowers —
one cultivates through essential nature.
In the west, perfectly pure primordial wisdom —
one meditates in a peaceful form.
In the north, what is called the All-Victorious —

one meditates with diligent intention.
In the center, perfect union with the offering of desire —
from the meditation on one's own form,
through the rite one contemplates
the Lord of Desire as having the essential nature of mind.
One who knows the rite should distinctly contemplate
the lotus-hand in the corners themselves.
Having made offering with all the five sense pleasures,
one offers and continues to offer.
Vajrasattva is drawn forth
and contemplated to the side.
Through the method of the offering of desire using the vital essence,
one generates it and makes offering to the All-Pervading Lord.
The master of desire should examine;
the offerings and so forth are to be comprehended in full.

This concludes the great meditative maṇḍala called "The Pledge-Samādhi of All Desires."

In this very maṇḍala,
one always performs the role of the buddha-master.
Throughout everything and in the maṇḍala,
the vajra-master meditates,
and in accordance with what the master of desire has taught,
one acts accordingly — not in any other maṇḍala.

Then Vajrapāṇi once more entered into the meditative absorption called "The Vajra that Blesses the Suchness that Rouses the Essence of the Uṣṇīṣa of All Tathāgatas," and taught this great meditative maṇḍala called "Rousing the Essence of All Uṣṇīṣas":

Imagining it fashioned of curds,
adorned with beautiful lotuses and so forth —
in the center of that crystal
one contemplates everything completely.
On four sides with four corners,
one carefully examines the four gates.
The maṇḍala of Dharma distinctly —
that one is to be meditated upon in the center.
The mantra-holder who knows the rite places
a wheel-turning sovereign in the center.
One who knows the rite meditates distinctly on Vairocana
precisely in the east.
One who knows the rite, in the north,
meditates distinctly on the one called Unnāmita, the Upward-Soaring.
In the south, the one called Amitābha —
one meditates with steadfast intention.
As is fitting in the west,
one meditates distinctly on the Heap of Splendor.
The Dharma-form equal to the form of a noble queen
is perfectly generated.
The symbolic marks — vajra and so forth as defining marks —
through the rite one bestows these upon them.
The great vidyā-holders — Locanā and the rest —
are to be contemplated in all the corners.
The great wrathful ones whom none can withstand
are to be meditated upon distinctly at each gate and in each gateway.
Vajrasattva is perfectly drawn forth;
one applies him to that suchness.
Through the union of wisdom, one makes offering
to all with the five sense pleasures.
The vital essence is perfectly generated therein —
what generates the offering is said to be generated.
All the uṣṇīṣas of the space-realm

Then the Blessed One taught this rite of pleasing the great Vairocana, Vajrasattva.

The buddha, the master with beautiful wings,
residing in the vajra cross-legged posture,
enjoying through the proximity of Garuḍa —

that which sustains and maintains life — the remainder of what is to be spoken is taught. It does not become idle chatter about wandering beings and dancing and the like. A student who dwells in what is not elevated — if during a Dharma session he commits sins and the like, that fool will be tormented in the Incessant Hell (Avīci). If one merely with the mantra were to attack Brahmā with both hands, for as long as the sky truly arises, one will be tormented in the Incessant Hell. The secret of women is not to be spoken. If someone undertakes effort for the sake of the master, whether experiencing happiness or bodily and mental suffering, for as long as one lives, if the commands of the masters are carried out with a joyful mind, then buddhahood and the rest will be accomplished for oneself — of this, here, there is no doubt. With regard to that, for as long as one lives, one should dwell everywhere through signs. Resting on one's hips with the hands and so on — as long as one lives, this should not be done. One who seeks the fruit of accomplishment (siddhi) should not name the master by name. For as long as the sky truly arises, one will be tormented in the hell of Incessant suffering. One should act toward the Tathāgata just as one does in like manner. Regarding food as well, one's own student should know: one should meditate clearly upon the buddha's form, and the letters of his name one should not recite for as long as one lives. If one were to slay buddhas beyond counting, this sin would be supremely surpassing. When someone is praised with these qualities, true merit arises. At that time, when the master is free of concepts, if elsewhere I am a deluded fool, then at that time one will be tormented with weeping and wailing. When one serves with devotion — listening and the like — that disciple who requests mantras, just as some have seen masters directly as bliss, and some masters who are themselves scorching and burning with the black mark — those will be tormented with weeping and wailing. Whoever seeks a master, one should offer that to him. Otherwise, without accomplishment, an uninspected disciple should accomplish what serves the purpose of the master's wife, or else by giving a sister accomplishment will arise — of this, here, there is no doubt. Whatever master, for the sake of a disciple, undertakes prostration should accomplish it. For sixty eons that one will be tormented experiencing the suffering of hell. Thus these surpassing qualities should be clarified over six months. If elsewhere I am deluded, one will be tormented as if in the Incessant Hell. Whatever one seeks in a master, one should accomplish individually. Through the vigorous rousing of vajra-wisdom, one will proceed there toward broad accomplishment. Whoever possesses these qualities should know that merit arises. This master's tantra and the like — such things and the like should not be revealed. Thus, having oneself attained purity and abiding through the rite of the nature of reality (dharmatā), one should clearly reveal the master. Accomplishment will come swiftly — not otherwise. Abiding in the abode of the buddha, that supreme Vajrasattva (Vajrasattva), upon being pleased, swiftly accomplishes — not otherwise. One who is a buddha and has attained buddhahood itself — from pleasing that one the supreme arises. By that, the three worlds are accomplished, and the buddha-wisdom itself as well.

The eighteenth chapter, the chapter on pleasing the secret Vajrasattva of the Tathāgata, is complete.

Having done what is called devadatta, whoever makes this radiant — if even a buddha were there, that one would be reduced to ashes. The skilled practitioner should perform the fire offering at midnight and again at noon. For the span of one eon, one should bestow it — otherwise never at any time.

If even a buddha or anyone else should pass away with a deluded mind, the head splits into seven pieces. The awakening mind itself he proclaimed. What is called the first is known as the Raising and the Felling; the second is called by that name; the third is the supreme among gods; and the fourth is known as Vajrapāṇi even in the noble realm. Through the conjunction of all essences, actions are accomplished swiftly.

At places abounding in various touches and the like, in pleasure-groves and at great festivals, the activity of that maṇḍala should be begun by offering incense. Whether it be Śakra (Indra) or Brahmā, a buddha or a great sage — one who knows the ritual will subdue them in seven days; of this here there is no doubt.

One who knows the ritual, having fully entered with a wrathful mind into a dampened garment, and having pressed the linga with the foot, will certainly subdue even a buddha. Having placed Viṣṇu, the asuras, and the other gods underfoot, one should begin. One who knows the ritual will subdue all three realms in seven days.

Thus spoke the Blessed One, great Vairocana, in teaching all the mantras. The nineteenth chapter, on the great secret, is complete.

Then again the bodhisattva known as "Vajra of the Pledge of the Wish-Fulfilling Jewel" addressed the bodhisattva Vajrapāṇi with these words: Blessed One, what are the various and particular qualities of the Dharma that are to be individually desired upon seeing that bodhisattva?

Then Vajrapāṇi, the lord of all the Tathāgatas, declared to that bodhisattva:

Know without doubt and in equanimity —
regard the buddhas as Dharma.
Perceiving without doubt —
regard all phenomena as desired.
Perceiving without doubt —
regard all conditioned things as desired.

It is as follows. What is called a son of the family is one who is perfectly established on the bodhisattva ground of the cloud of Dharma. To that one, there is no conceptuality and no doubt with regard to the dharmakāya of all the Tathāgatas, the saṃbhogakāya of all the Tathāgatas, and the nirmāṇakāya of all the Tathāgatas — thinking "this is mine" — and all phenomena have become seen as open as the sky. In brief, son of the family, such a bodhisattva is to be called "Vajra Gnosis."

Then all the Blessed Tathāgatas were filled with wonder, and bowing in homage to the supreme being of all the Blessed Tathāgatas, they spoke these words: Blessed One, how will this bodhisattva who practices the conduct of mantra come to have faith in the ultimate secret?

And further: how will it be that all those called Blessed Tathāgatas, throughout the world-realm of the great trichiliocosm, perfectly attain complete and manifest awakening as buddhas, the Blessed Ones? All the Tathāgatas declared: A single Blessed Tathāgata will attain perfect and complete awakening.

The vajra-bearer asked: How will perfect and complete awakening be attained? It is like this — moreover, the sentient beings who form the ground, who have become as particles of dust of the world-realm of the great trichiliocosm, will all attain perfect and complete awakening. In just the same way, Blessed Tathāgatas all, this manner of Vajrapāṇi is to be understood.

Then those bodhisattvas, having obtained wonder, joy, and supreme delight, uttered this song in that very tone:

Emaho! Gnosis, great gnosis —
the great vajra is supremely beheld!
By the wondrous world,
the eye of gnosis has been fully purified!

Then the Blessed One Vajrapāṇi, lord of all Tathāgatas, entered into the meditative absorption called the Vajra of Non-Duality Equipoise with All Tathāgatas. The very moment he entered that absorption, the Blessed One sent forth hundreds of thousands of millions of light rays as numerous as the grains of sand in the river Ganges, filling the east, south, and all the remaining directions — directions beyond telling, and beyond telling again — filling as many buddha-fields as there are fine particles of dust in that many Ganges-river sands, with the gnosis of the three bodies.

The bodhisattva bestows the essence.
Dwelling in the expanse of space,

In phenomena devoid of defining marks,
conceptual elaborations simply do not exist.
By the gnosis of the vajra's intrinsic nature,

Then the Blessed One Vajrapāṇi arose from meditative absorption and called out to Vajradharma: "Great being, do you see that which is the sacred commitment?" "Blessed One, I see it."
