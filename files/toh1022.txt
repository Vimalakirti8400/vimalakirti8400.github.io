The Dhāraṇī Completely Pacifying All Hostility

The Noble Dhāraṇī Called "Completely Pacifying All Hostility" is a very brief incantation text, known in Tibetan as a gzungs, or dhāraṇī. It opens with homage to the Three Jewels and to the Buddha Samantadarśin, the "All-Seeing," and then gives a short mantra. Its instruction is that anyone who recites the mantra seven times while supplicating the bodhisattva Mañjuśrī will find that all people become loving toward them, so that enmity and hostility are calmed. The text is Toh 1022 in the Kangyur, where it belongs to the collection of dhāraṇīs, short protective and beneficial formulas meant for recitation.

Homage to the Three Jewels.
Homage to the Blessed One, the Tathāgata, the arhat, the perfectly complete Buddha Samantadarśin.

tadyathā dīri tiṣṭha taskara baddho si macala macala svāhā

If one recites this seven times and makes supplication to noble Mañjuśrī, all people will become loving.

The noble dhāraṇī called "Completely Pacifying All Hostility" is complete.
