The Noble Dhāraṇī of Yaśovatī (Āryayaśovatīdhāraṇī)

The Noble Dhāraṇī of Yaśovatī, known in Sanskrit as the Āryayaśovatīdhāraṇī and in Tibetan as Phags pa grags ldan ma'i gzungs, is a short ritual text devoted to protective and healing incantations. After paying homage to the Three Jewels and to the noble bodhisattva Avalokiteśvara, it presents a series of mantras, each paired with simple instructions for its use. These include reciting a mantra three times over a thread or over water to free someone from contagious fever, and tying knots in a five-colored or red thread to ward off harmful spirits known as grahas and pacify obstacles. It is Toh 992 in the Kangyur, where it belongs to the class of dhāraṇī texts, the collections of incantations valued for their power to protect, heal, and remove hindrances.

In the language of India: ārya yaśovatī dhāraṇī
In the language of Tibet: The Noble Dhāraṇī of Yaśovatī.

Homage to all the buddhas and bodhisattvas!

namo ratnatrayāya nama āryāvalokiteśvarāya bodhisatvāya mahāsatvāya tadyathā jvare mahājvare dukhe mukta susaṃpanne mahāsaṃpanne saṃpatti mahāsaṃpatti kase mahākase kaccha jvara mukte si svāhā

If one recites this mantra three times over a thread or over water, one will be freed from contagious fever.

namo ratnatrayāya nama āryāvalokiteśvarāya bodhisatvāya mahāsatvāya tadyathā cili cili mili mili rakṣa rakṣa māṃ āryāvalokiteśvarā svāhā

For all grahas, recite this mantra three times over a thread of five colors or over a red thread, tie three knots in it, and bind it on. Obstacles will then be pacified.

namo ratnatrayāya nama āryāvalokiteśvarāya bodhisatvāya mahāsatvaya tadyathā kili kili cili cili nigile svāhā

If one recites this mantra three times over sesame oil and anoints with it, tumors are cured.

namo ratnatrayāya nama āryāvalokiteśvarāya bodhisatvāya mahāsatvāya tadyathā sare sare visare visare saranāśani svāhā

If one recites this three times into the ear of one who has died, that person will be revived.

namo ratnatrayāya nama āryāvalokiteśvārāya bodhisatvāya mahāsatvāya tadyathā nisire nisire kalanimire kalaricirī pacile svāhā

For goiter, for pain in the throat, for pain of the tongue, and for all diseases caused by grahas, recite this mantra three times over clay and smear it on. It will help.

namo ratnatrayāya nama āryāvalokiteśvarāya vajrapāṇidhyama oṃ oṃ krārprabhave bhagavati buddhe vrati buddhe śuddhe śāntikiri hana hana mama pāpamaṃ daha daha mama pāpadaṃ paca paca mama pāpakaṃ oṃ oḍe grasa grasa riteḍe riteḍe śīntaṃ karīya svāhā śubha karīya svāhā kṣema karīya svāhā

If one performs the ritual brushing on someone stricken by an epidemic disease, that person will recover from the epidemic. If one empowers medicine with the mantra and gives it to drink, the sick person will recover. If one empowers food with the mantra and eats it, poison will not harm one. For those seized by bhūta grahas, recite the mantra twenty-one times over a thread spun by a young girl and tie it on them. They will be freed.

The Noble Dhāraṇī of Yaśovatī is complete.

This concludes the translation. The following are translator's notes.

"Goiter" reads the written wa ba (normally "fox") as lba ba, goiter, which fits this list of throat ailments. The throat and tongue ailments (lkog ma'i lha gor and lce kyi ba) use obscure anatomical terms, so they are rendered generally. "Ritual brushing" renders byab pa, the rite of sweeping or wiping the patient, here presumably while reciting the mantra.
