The Noble Dhāraṇī That Pacifies Anger (Ārya Krodhapraśamanī Dhāraṇī)

The Noble Dhāraṇī That Pacifies Anger, known in Tibetan as Pakpa Tröpa Shiwar Jepé Zung, is a very short incantation text whose Sanskrit title is not securely attested but would correspond to a noble dhāraṇī for the pacification of anger. After a brief homage to Mahāśrī, the Great Glorious One, it gives a single mantra and explains its use: when recited in the presence of someone who is angry, that person's anger will be calmed, and he will be drawn favorably toward the one reciting it. It is Toh 1026 in the Kangyur, where it belongs to the collection of dhāraṇī texts, the brief spells and protective formulas valued for their practical, everyday benefits.

The Noble Dhāraṇī That Pacifies Anger

Homage to Mahāśrī!

kaṭe bikaṭe roṣagraśamani svāhā

If one recites this in front of someone who is angry, that person's anger will be pacified, and he will certainly come to one.

The Noble Dhāraṇī That Pacifies Anger is complete.

This concludes the translation. The following are translator's notes.

The final clause, literally "will certainly come," most likely means that the angry person will come back to the reciter in a reconciled frame of mind.
