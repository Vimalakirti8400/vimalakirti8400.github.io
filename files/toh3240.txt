Translated from the Indian language, the title is: Vasu-dhāraṇī Upadeśa. In Tibetan: The Practical Instructions on the Dhāraṇī of Vasudhārā.

Before a thangka of Vasudhārā or an image of her, one makes a square sandalwood maṇḍala. Into that maṇḍala one invites the Blessed Lady to be present in mind, and makes offerings to her. Then, with hands anointed with sandalwood, one takes up the book of the Vasudhārā dhāraṇī and places before it a water vessel into which has been placed a garland of flowers well-strung together with thread. With one's mind, one meditates correctly with great loving-kindness upon all sentient beings, and, with the aim of accomplishing one's desired purpose held clearly in one's heart, one recites the Vasudhārā dhāraṇī.

After reciting, each mantra is to be concluded by adding the syllable svāhā at its close. Then, together with white flowers including roots of dūrvā grass and unbroken grains, these are placed into the water vessel, and this continues for the duration of up to six months. After the recitation is complete, that water is to be poured out onto a clean patch of ground.

Here ends the Practical Instructions on the Dhāraṇī of Vasudhārā.
