This is a teaching spoken by the buddhas, or equally found in a treatise: if one hears it, one will be able to memorize one thousand verses in a single day. Thus ends the dhāraṇī for memorizing one thousand verses.

Homage to the Three Jewels. Homage to the bodhisattva, the great being, the noble Avalokiteśvara, Lord Who Sees.

Tadyathā: tri śātri śula śākhi āryāvalokiteśvarāya ājñāpayati varenabuhala mṛddhātu svāhā.

The ritual procedure is as follows. One draws a maṇḍala before the feet of the noble Avalokiteśvara, Lord Who Sees, and makes offerings with whatever is available. One then recites the mantra over water and sets it aside. In the morning, one recites the mantra seven times over the water, and at dawn takes food mixed with milk. If one does this for seven days, one will be able to memorize one thousand verses in a single day. As a sign of confidence...
