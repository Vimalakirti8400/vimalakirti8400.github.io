The Noble Dhāraṇī of Amoghapāśa Called "Fulfilling the Six Perfections" (Āryāmoghapāśapāramitāṣaṭparipūrakā nāma dhāraṇī)

The Noble Dhāraṇī of Amoghapāśa Called "Fulfilling the Six Perfections," known in Sanskrit as Ārya Amoghapāśa Pāramitā Ṣaṭparipūraka Nāma Dhāraṇī and in Tibetan as Phagpa Dönyö Zhakpé Pharol Tu Chinpa Drug Yongsu Dzokpar Jepa Zhejawé Zung, is a short dhāraṇī text centered on Amoghapāśa, the form of the bodhisattva Avalokiteśvara whose name means "Unfailing Noose." After paying homage to all the buddhas and bodhisattvas, to the tathāgatas of the three times, and to the greatly compassionate Avalokiteśvara, the text presents mantras described as the heart-essence of Amoghapāśa's perfections, beginning with the perfection of generosity. It promises that a person who gives even a gift the size of a mustard seed while reciting the mantra seven times, or even merely recollecting it, will bring that perfection to fulfillment, and its title indicates that it goes on to address each of the six perfections in a similar way. The work is Toh 903 in the Kangyur, where it belongs to the class of dhāraṇī texts, the incantations and ritual formulas preserved in the Tibetan canon.

In Sanskrit: ārya amoghapāśa pāramitā ṣanparipūrakā nāma dhāraṇī
In Tibetan: The Noble Dhāraṇī of Amoghapāśa Called "Fulfilling the Six Perfections."

Homage to all the buddhas and bodhisattvas!

Homage to all the Tathāgatas who have gone and who abide throughout the three times!

Homage to the bodhisattva, the great being endowed with great compassion, the noble Avalokiteśvara!

oṃ amogha pāśa mahā dāna pāramitā pāri pūraya hūṃ dara dara vibadhi vicitraiḥ sarva satvā upabhoga sarva tathāgata mahā dāna pūja megha prabartaya tara tara tāraya mahā padma maṇi hūṃ phaṭ svāhā

This is the great heart-essence of Amoghapāśa's perfection of generosity. If someone gives a gift even the size of a mustard seed and recites this seven times, merely by recollecting it, then the perfection of generosity will be fully completed: a great offering, a great magical display, a great and most vast giving.

Homage to all the Tathāgatas of the three times!

Homage to the bodhisattva, the great being endowed with great compassion, the noble Avalokiteśvara!

oṃ amogha śīla saṃbhara saṃbhara bhara bhara mahā śuddha satva padma vibhāṣita bhuja dhara dhara samanta avalokite hūṃ phaṭ svāhā

Having taken up the eight branches of the restoration ceremony and keeping the fast, one should recite this dhāraṇī from memory twenty-one times, without speaking. Then one should recite the heart mantra of Amoghapāśa. As soon as one has recited it, one will possess great purity of moral discipline. The perfection of moral discipline will be fully completed. The many sweet fragrances of moral discipline will arise from one's body, and one will become renowned throughout the worlds of the ten directions. Whoever recollects this dhāraṇī every single day will always be completely pure in moral discipline. If any monk whose moral discipline has been ruined and has lapsed keeps the fast, then as soon as he has recited this dhāraṇī, his moral discipline will become completely pure, and he will be endowed with abundant moral discipline.

Homage to all the Tathāgatas of the three times!

Homage to the bodhisattva, the great being endowed with great compassion, the noble Avalokiteśvara!

oṃ amogha kṣānti sarva bodhi satva kṣamiṇi kṣaṇa kṣaṇa mahā maitri kāruṇa sarva satva batsala mahā kāruṇika sarva satva kṣamiṇi hūṃ phaṭ svāhā

By recollecting this dhāraṇī, one abides in equipoise with a mind endowed with patience, great patience, love, and great compassion toward all who have become sentient beings. And if anyone, out of love for the wild animals, birds, crawling creatures, and others belonging to the animal state of birth, whether they have passed away, are living now, or will be born, cultivates supreme patience by recollecting this dhāraṇī, that person will be completely liberated from all the hells, the realm of hungry ghosts, and the animal realm, and will be released from among the many multitudes of beings who dwell in suffering. Until one comes to dwell in a completely pure buddha field, the perfection of patience will be fully completed.

Homage to all the Tathāgatas of the three times!

Homage to the bodhisattva, the great being endowed with great compassion, the noble Avalokiteśvara!

oṃ mahā vīrya amogha vilokite vara vara dṛdha vīrya mahā bala bala bala mahā bodhyaṃga bala bodhani hūṃ phaṭ svāhā

As soon as one recollects this dhāraṇī at all times, or recollects it with a pure and joyful mind, the perfection of diligence will become completely pure. Whoever, endowed with this attention, performs even a few kinds of virtue will have done what is immeasurable; it will become powerful, and it will come swiftly. Until one sits at the seat of awakening, one's diligence will not decline. One will never be separated from the perfection of diligence, and one will become greatly diligent.

Homage to all the Tathāgatas of the three times!

Homage to the bodhisattva, the great being endowed with great compassion, the noble Avalokiteśvara!

oṃ sarva tathāgata mahā amogha kāruṇa dhyāna samādhi sarva vimokṣa aprakambyaka curu curu hūṃ phaṭ svāhā

If, in the presence of the noble Avalokiteśvara, one recollects this dhāraṇī single-pointedly with attention, then as soon as one recollects it, the perfection of meditative concentration will be fully completed. Moreover, one will individually attain a hundred thousand meditative absorptions. One will enter all buddha fields. As soon as one has recited it, one will gain mastery over all meditative absorptions.

Homage to all the Tathāgatas of the three times!

Homage to the bodhisattva, the great being endowed with great compassion, the noble Avalokiteśvara!

oṃ amogha mahā prajñā pāramitā avabhāsa svabhāva samaṃta sāraṇa buddhi prasara prasara samanta buddhi avalokite bhagabāna prajñā avalokite cakṣuṣaṃ mahā prajñā barada maṇi mahā prajñā padma dhāriṇi bhuje hūṃ phaṭ svāhā

As soon as one recollects this dhāraṇī, the perfection of wisdom will be fully completed. Great virtuous understanding, through various kinds of skillful means and wisdom, will greatly increase. One will always and without interruption possess the understanding of the ten perfections. Bearing in mind all the Tathāgatas of the ten directions, and all the Tathāgatas of the past and those who will appear in the future, one will be fully endowed with the wise understanding that recollects the Buddha. Never at any time will one be separated from the six perfections.

One will never fall away from the perfections of generosity, moral discipline, patience, diligence, meditative concentration, and wisdom. Until one is fully and perfectly awakened to enlightenment, one will never be confused. One will be fully endowed with the reality called "perfectly following the suchness of all phenomena." One will be richly endowed with immeasurable intelligence. One will be held in mind by all the Tathāgatas of the ten directions. All the bodhisattvas will think of one with care.

This completes the great dhāraṇī of noble Amoghapāśa that fully completes the six perfections.

This concludes the translation. The following are translator's notes.

In the passage on moral discipline, the text reads "mdo" (sūtra), which makes no sense there; it is evidently a slip for "mod," as in the parallel phrase "as soon as one has recited," and has been translated that way. In the passage on generosity, "byas ni" has been read as "byas na," "if one recites."
