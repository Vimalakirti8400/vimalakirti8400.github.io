In the Tibetan language:

The noble dhāraṇī called "The Indestructible."

Thus did I hear. At one time the Blessed One was dwelling in Vaiśālī, in the pavilion beside the Monkey Pond. At that time Śakra (Indra), lord of the gods, addressed the Blessed One with these words: "Blessed One, the asuras are attacking and harming me, and it has come to pass that the asuras and I have long been at enmity, without friendship. Therefore I beseech the Blessed One to teach me the Dharma that will bring me lasting benefit and lasting happiness." When he had spoken thus, the Blessed One declared to Śakra, lord of the gods:

"Therefore, O Kauśika, receive this knowledge-mantra of mine called 'The Indestructible.' It is as follows: tadyathā — urbapa, nirbisirbī, urpurpure, hūle hūle, dekhume, pratyaṃgama āgaccha mahā māyāri nāma — protect me from all fears, all harm, and all their retinues. Protect! Protect! sarva kāya vāka manaś carite svāhā."

The Blessed One, pleased, having declared these words, Śakra, lord of the gods, was filled with joy.
