The Twenty-Five Gateways of the Perfection of Wisdom (Ārya-pañcaviṃśatika-prajñāpāramitā-mukha-nāma-mahāyānasūtra)

Toh 20 in the Kangyur is a Mahāyāna sūtra known in Sanskrit as the Ārya-pañcaviṃśatika-prajñāpāramitā-mukha, in Tibetan as the Shes rab kyi pha rol tu phyin pa'i sgo nyi shu rtsa lnga pa, and in English as The Twenty-Five Gateways of the Perfection of Wisdom. It belongs to the Perfection of Wisdom genre, a major class of Mahāyāna scripture concerned with the nature of prajñāpāramitā, or transcendent wisdom, and the path of the bodhisattva. The text opens with the Buddha dwelling on the summit of Mount Meru, surrounded by a vast assembly of bodhisattvas, gods, and other beings within a great maṇḍala, and proceeds to expound the perfection of wisdom through twenty-five thematic gateways or modes of entry. As its catalog number indicates, it is Toh 20 in the Kangyur, placed among the Perfection of Wisdom sūtras that form one of the most extensive and central sections of that collection.

Homage to all buddhas and bodhisattvas.

Thus did I hear. At one time, the Blessed One was dwelling on the summit of Mount Meru, in the abode of the all-night festival of all the gods, together with many hundreds of thousands of tens of millions of bodhisattvas. Then the Blessed One, within a maṇḍala extending five leagues in every direction, was surrounded entirely by gods, sons of gods, asuras, wind gods, garuḍas, nāgas, yakṣas, kinnaras, rākṣasas, all vidyādhara holders of mantra, elemental spirits, malevolent guides, flesh-eaters, and the hosts of visible and invisible māmo spirits — a great and vast multitude. Encircled by twelve hundred and fifty monks arrayed before him, he taught the Dharma.

Then the great bodhisattva Vajrapāṇi, Lord of Secrets, surrounded by all the vajra families, by all those hosts of vidyā and mudrā, and by many tens of millions of vidyādhara mantra-holders, went to where the Blessed One was. Having descended from the sky, he bowed his head at the feet of the Blessed One, circumambulated him three times, and sat down to one side.

Then the Blessed One declared to the great Lord of Secrets, Vajrapāṇi: "O Lord of Secrets, these twenty-five are the gateways of my Perfection of Wisdom."
