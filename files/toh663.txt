Then the Blessed One shall explain the ritual procedures of Dhāraṇī of the Stream of Wealth. First of all, on the first day of the bright fortnight of the last month of spring, one should go to a river that flows into the ocean, and from that point until evening one should sit submerged in the water, gazing directly toward the sun, and recite for as long as one remains there. Having abandoned all food, having sapped and silenced the afflictions, one should recite for seven nights. Then a painted image of the Blessed Lady — her body anointed with saffron and sandalwood except for her hair — should be made: rising early in the evening, it shall be an image seven cubits in height and three cubits in breadth, ten cubits altogether, fashioned with all manner of colors as a divine figure with the ritual observance of restoration, the Blessed Lady with a complexion of variegated yellow-green, adorned with all manner of ornaments; in her right hand she holds a great fruit; in her left hand she holds a precious basket set with pearls, a vajra, and vaiḍūrya fruits, to be given to practitioners. Above, divine sons are shown raining down a shower of jewels. To her right the Great Glorious One bearing a lotus stands in an attitude of care and attention toward the practitioner. To her left there is a hand filled with a cupped handful of precious lotus splendor. Below, the practitioner holds hands formed in the lotus gesture. Then the painted image, together with a reliquary stūpa containing relics, is spread out facing westward; and thereupon, apart from the torma for daily use, one presents the treasury offerings as well and performs a great offering with fragrant flowers, and burns sandalwood and aloeswood incense. One should offer a hundred lamps of clarified butter. Then, remaining on a single seat, fixing the mind on the face of the Blessed Lady, and reciting throughout the night until dawn — at the time when dawn breaks, a stream of all precious wealth sufficient to fill the dwelling of a great man will flow and the house will become full. Alternatively, at midnight one should recite eight hundred clear recitations within the water. By performing this practice continually for one month, a stream of wealth will flow, and for some it will increase beyond knowing. Alternatively, if one desires the royal treasury, one should recite within the water by day, and at midnight perform eight hundred fire offerings with sesame seeds, and one will obtain great gain from the royal lineage. If one performs fire offerings with white mustard mixed with butter, one brings the king under one's power. If one performs fire offerings with kuṇḍuru incense, one brings the king's ministers under one's power. With whatever practice some perform, if at night one recollects the Blessed Lady enthroned upon a lotus, one will become a great patron. Furthermore, the great sādhana shall now be explained: beginning from the first day of the bright fortnight of the month of miracles, having cut off all food as completely as possible, one spreads and hangs a painted image facing westward before a stūpa containing relics, and by day one reads the Perfection of Wisdom, and at night, remaining on a single seat, one recites silently in a whisper — one will find a great heap of wealth, and even if it is scattered and damaged it will not be exhausted. One should build a great temple. If this ritual procedure is performed with recitation during the bright fortnight of the month of miracles, one will attain sovereignty as a wheel-turning king, one will actually behold the Blessed Lady Dhāraṇī of the Stream of Wealth in person, and one will become a non-returning bodhisattva.
