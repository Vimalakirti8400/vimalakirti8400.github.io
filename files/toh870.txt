Recollection of the Name of the Buddha Ratnaśikhin (Ratnaśikhinnāmānusmṛti)

The Recollection of the Name of the Buddha Ratnaśikhin is a very short dhāraṇī text in the Kangyur, devoted to the buddha Ratnaśikhin, whose name means "Jewel Crest" or "Jewel Topknot." The Tibetan title line begins with a homage to this buddha and gives the spoken formula in transliterated Sanskrit, and the Tibetan title appears to concern recollection at the time of death, though the line is cut off and its full wording cannot be confirmed here. The text consists of a brief homage, a short mantra-like invocation addressed to the tathāgata, arhat, and perfectly awakened one, and a promise of benefit: whoever recalls it at the moment of death will be freed from all the lower realms and will be reborn as a god in the Heaven of the Thirty-Three. It belongs to the genre of dhāraṇī and name-recollection texts, which offer concise protective formulas and the invocation of a buddha's name as a practice for securing a favorable rebirth. It is Toh 870 in the Kangyur, found among the tantra-section texts, where many such short dhāraṇīs are gathered.

Homage to the Buddha Ratnaśikhin.

Namo ratnaśikhine tathāgatāya arhate samyaksaṃbuddhāya. Tadyathā: ratne ratne ratnaśikhine svāhā.

If one recollects this at the time of death, one will be completely freed from all the lower realms. And after dying and passing on, one will be born as a god in the Heaven of the Thirty-Three.

This completes the Recollection of the Name of the Buddha Ratnaśikhin.
