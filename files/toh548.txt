Homage to the noble Mañjuśrī.

Oṃ vākye te jāya. Oṃ vākye ṣa ṣe sva. Oṃ vākye khaṃ ja ye. Oṃ vākye niṣṭha a. Oṃ vākye ya namaḥ. Oṃ vākye daṃ namaḥ.

By reciting this very name of the noble Mañjuśrī twenty-one times, all karmic obscurations of the five actions of immediate retribution will be purified.

The names of the noble Mañjuśrī are complete.
