Homage to the Three Jewels.

In order to thoroughly pacify the suffering
of the boundless realm of sentient beings,
to deliver them from the lower realms,
and to liberate them from the afflictions,
whatever manifold sufferings there are
when existence is fearful and uneasy,
in order to protect them completely from all this,
I generate the mind of awakening.

Of all destitute sentient beings
may I myself always be the refuge.
May I be a protector for those without protector,
a support for those without support,
may I become a refuge for those without refuge,
and turn suffering into happiness.
May I be the one who pacifies
the afflictions of all sentient beings.

Whatever virtuous actions,
gathered in this life and in others,
may they become the two accumulations
called merit and wisdom.

Through the gateway of the six perfections,
whatever diligence I undertake,
may that too benefit
all sentient beings without exception.

Whether it be making the span of life limitless,
or whatever small thing I may do,
in short, for the sake of pacifying
all the afflictions of all sentient beings,
and in order to liberate them, I strive,
and joyfully exert myself toward awakening.

Then I generate throughout
the mind of perfect awakening, according to the rite.

Toward all sentient beings
I shall make compassion and loving-kindness ever present.

In order to properly train the mind,
through various methods, the six recollections,
with an inwardly pacified nature,
I shall meditate with a single-pointed intention.

Just as the sons of the Victors of the past
made their aspirational prayers,
I too, with a virtuous mind,
likewise offer aspirational prayers.

Having settled in equipoise
upon the manifold enjoyments of the Dharma, I rejoice.
May all sentient beings have happiness,
be at peace, and be free from illness.

May I be capable of every activity
and endowed also with good qualities—
possessed of wealth, able to give, endowed with wisdom,
possessed of patience, foremost in virtue—
and in all the lifetimes of sentient beings
may I remember my births and be filled with tender affection.
