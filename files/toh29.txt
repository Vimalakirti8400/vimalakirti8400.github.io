In the language of India: Ārya-prajñāpāramitā-vajrapāṇi-mahāyāna-sūtra. In the Tibetan language: The Noble Perfection of Wisdom, Vajrapāṇi, Great Vehicle Sūtra.

Homage to all buddhas and bodhisattvas.

Thus did I hear. At one time the Blessed One was dwelling at Rājagṛha. At that time, the bodhisattva Vajrapāṇi was present in that assembly. He rose from his seat and addressed the Blessed One with these words: "Blessed One, how should a bodhisattva train in the Perfection of Wisdom?"

The Blessed One declared: "Vajrapāṇi, a bodhisattva should train in the Perfection of Wisdom in this way: by understanding that all phenomena are without arising, one should train in the Perfection of Wisdom. Furthermore, because all phenomena — form and the rest — are without intrinsic nature, without distinguishing marks, devoid of things, and unapprehendable, one should train in the Perfection of Wisdom. Because of inner emptiness and the emptiness of non-existence of things, one should train in the Perfection of Wisdom.

"Vajrapāṇi, this Perfection of Wisdom is the mother of all buddhas of the three times. The words of the mantra of great knowledge-awareness are these: tadyathā — oṃ mune mune mahāmunaye svāhā.

"Whoever holds to this Perfection of Wisdom will come to grasp the Dharma of all the buddhas of the three times. They will come to remember their births. They will abandon all states of lack of leisure. They will attain the fruit of merit beyond measure. They will always dwell in the company of the blessed buddhas. They will come to be endowed with the awakening mind."

When the Blessed One had declared this, the bodhisattva Vajrapāṇi, along with the gods, humans, asuras, and gandharvas — the whole world — rejoiced and praised what the Blessed One had declared.
