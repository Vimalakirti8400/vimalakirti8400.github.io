The Noble Dhāraṇī Called the Purification of All Obstacles (Ārya Sarva Antarāyika Viśodhanī Nāma Dhāraṇī)

Toh 608 in the Kangyur is a dhāraṇī text known in Sanskrit as the Ārya Sarva Antarāyika Viśodhanī Nāma Dhāraṇī and in Tibetan as the Phags pa bar du gcod pa thams cad rnam par sbyong ba zhes bya ba'i gzungs, meaning in English the Noble Dhāraṇī Called the Purification of All Obstacles. It belongs to the dhāraṇī section of the Kangyur and presents a sacred incantation concerned with the removal of all obstacles, including those arising from the approach of death and the exhaustion of one's karmic formations. The text is framed as a discourse delivered by the Buddha at Rājagṛha, prompted by the god Śakra, lord of the devas, who in fear of his impending death and transmigration seeks protection and guidance from the Blessed One. As a noble dhāraṇī sūtra, it combines the narrative conventions of canonical scripture with the conferral of a powerful protective formula intended to purify hindrances for those who receive and uphold it.

In the language of India: Ārya Sarva Antarāyika Viśodhanī Nāma Dhāraṇī. In the language of Tibet: The Noble Dhāraṇī Called the Purification of All Obstacles.

Homage to the Buddha. Homage to the Dharma. Homage to the Saṅgha.

Thus did I hear. At one time the Blessed One was dwelling at Rājagṛha, in the Indra's Cave on the Vulture Peak mountain, to the north of a place called the Mango Grove, the brahmin's village to the east of Rājagṛha.

Then Śakra, lord of the gods — known as the Hundred-Powered One — came to be subject to the law of dying and transmigration. Having received within himself the portents foretelling that the remainder of the formations of his life was nearly spent, fear arose in him. Together with his retinue of queens and attendants, he went before the Blessed One, bowed his head to the feet of the Blessed One, circumambulated him three times, and sat down to one side. He then besought the Blessed One earnestly on behalf of this matter.

The Blessed One then declared to him: "Kauśika, hear now these dhāraṇī bases of vidyā mantra by which all māras, all those who would oppose, and all hostile adversaries are utterly vanquished — these dhāraṇī bases of vidyā mantra called the Purification of All Obstacles.

Tadyathā: abhaye bhaya-vigate bhaya-pariśodhane abhaye abhayaṃ-dade nirupakliṣṭi anākule akula-pariśodhane agavate avikalpe avikalpane acyute śubhe śubhe tejaste joba ti śame upaśame mahopaśame maitri maitryuddhite toṣe toṣayaṇe satye satya-darśi aviruddhe sahite sahitatve arthi artha-siddhi amṛte amṛtavati abhīte vajra-satye akṣaye akṣaya-prabhā dhantra-bare śuddhe śuddhe prabhā avabhāse avabhāsavati upayoge anuttare samācitta buddha-adhiṣṭhāne śīle viśodhane asaṃhārye su-nigṛhīta-mara-balānāṃ vara-jayo-dṛṣṭāgatrāṇāṃ jvalanaṃ dharmo-lkāye udghaṭānāṃ dharma-nidhanasya saṃgrahāṇāṃ dharma-koṣasya.

Kauśika, these are the dhāraṇī bases of your vidyā mantra — those dhāraṇī bases of your vidyā mantra by which all māras, all those who would oppose, and all hostile adversaries are utterly vanquished. If this is recited, all obstacles will be fully purified — excepting the karmic maturation of past karma — and all aims will be accomplished."

When the Blessed One had spoken these words, Śakra, lord of the gods, rejoiced and at that moment made this solemn utterance:

Though I have not yet passed beyond the gods,
while remaining here in this very place,
I have already obtained long life —
let all companions know it to be so.

By the power of all of this, may all the obstacles, obstructers, and misleaders that stand against me be utterly eliminated. May all excellence and good fortune be fully accomplished.

Namo aketulenage caṇḍanaye svāhā. Sarva-artha-sādhanaye svāhā. Dharmārtha-sādhanaye svāhā. Sarva-antarāyika-viśodhanaye svāhā.

Having thus spoken, the Blessed One — the Śakra, lord of the gods, together with his retinue of queens and attendants, and the entire assembled retinue — rejoiced and praised what the Blessed One had proclaimed.

The Noble Dhāraṇī Called the Purification of All Obstacles is complete.
