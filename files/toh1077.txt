The Essence of the Meteor of Wisdom

The Essence of the Meteor of Wisdom, whose Tibetan title would be rendered along the lines of Yeshé Karda Nyingpo and whose Sanskrit name may be reconstructed as something like Jñānolkāhṛdaya, is a brief ritual text centered on a single mantra. After paying homage to the Three Jewels and giving the mantra, it instructs the practitioner to sit beside a flowing stream or a lake, recite the mantra seven times over water held in the cupped right palm, and then scatter seven palmfuls into the water. The text promises that this simple offering will satisfy countless hungry ghosts with divine food and drink, freeing them from that state of suffering and leading them to rebirth in the higher realms, which makes it an example of the compassionate practices of giving to beings of the hungry ghost realm. It is Toh 1077 in the Kangyur, where it belongs among the dhāraṇī texts, the collection of short incantations and their associated ritual instructions.

Homage to the Three Jewels.

oṃ jñāñāvalokite samantaspharaṇarasmibhava samaya mahāmaṇi duru duru hṛdayajalani hūṃ

Sit on the bank of a flowing stream or a lake and pour water into the cupped palm of your right hand. Recite this Essence of the Meteor of Wisdom seven times, and then scatter seven palmfuls of water into the stream or the water.

When this is done, all the hungry ghosts that the eye of the Buddha beholds, however many there may be, will obtain the taste of the food of the gods and the supreme taste of drink. Each and every hungry ghost will be satisfied with seven measures of food and seven of drink, measured by the standard of the land of Magadha. Merely by drinking that water, all of them will be freed from birth as hungry ghosts and will be born in the higher realms of the fortunate destinies.

Whoever drinks that water, whether a man or a woman, a boy or a girl, or any other creature born in the animal realm, such as a wild animal, a bird, or a creature that lives in the water, all of them will be purified of every misdeed and every obscuration and will go on the path of the fortunate destinies.

The Essence of the Meteor of Wisdom is complete.

This concludes the translation. The following are translator's notes.

The title "Meteor of Wisdom" renders the Tibetan for a shooting star or meteor (Sanskrit ulkā, which can also mean a blazing torch). It is a text title, so it is translated rather than given in Sanskrit. The "measure" is the Tibetan bre, a standard unit of volume, here reckoned by the measure used in Magadha.
