Homage to the Three Jewels. Homage to the noble Mañjuśrī.

Namo ratnatrayāya. Namo bhagavate dharmadhātunigarhita-rājāya tathāgatāya arhate samyaksaṃbuddhāya. Namo Mañjuśrīye kumārabhūtāya. Tadyathā: Oṃ jñānāloke triratnavāṃśasaṃdhāraṇi bhagavati abhayaṃdādi abhayaṃdatte me bodhicitta vajra paripālaya samanta ava bhāsena hana hana sarvasattvasaṃtāna patitaṃ kleśānucchede davala kṣaṇa alaṃkṛta śarīre bodhisatvaśiśūpati pālaya buddhānadeśāya deva nāga yakṣa gandharva asura garuḍa kinnara mahoraga vaśeṣṭhāpāya apayaṃ dadati svāhā.

The benefits of this dhāraṇī are as follows. Even reciting it just once purifies the karmic obscurations accumulated over a thousand eons. At the moment of death one will behold the noble Mañjuśrī. One will also behold the form of noble Avalokiteśvara adorned upon his crown with a topknot. In short, the benefits of this dhāraṇī are boundless.

The Dhāraṇī Called "Spoken from the Mouth of Noble Mañjuśrī" is complete.
