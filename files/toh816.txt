Homage to the Three Jewels.

He in whom the miserly are bound to clinging
Has been utterly conquered, made free of stain,
His mind at peace, with no capacity to harm:
By him may well-being be wrought for you.

The Guide who leads beings
Into the path of liberation,
And who reveals all phenomena:
By him may well-being be wrought for you.

The Teacher, refuge of those who run in fear,
For the sake of all sentient beings
Possessing happiness himself:
By him may well-being be wrought for you.

The Protector, who with a mind of loving-kindness
Always guards all these beings
As a mother her only child:
By him may well-being be wrought for you.

He who has become the support of all beings
Wandering in saṃsāra,
Who has become their island and their strong kinsman:
By him may well-being be wrought for you.

He for whom all phenomena are directly manifest,
Pure, with no capacity to deceive,
Whose speech is made perfectly pure:
By him may well-being be wrought for you.

The great hero, by whose birth
Every perfection and every aim was fulfilled,
And all accomplishments were brought to completion:
By him may well-being be wrought for you.

He at whose birth the earth,
With its forests, trembled greatly,
And all beings were filled with joy:
By him may well-being be wrought for you.

He at the heart of awakening, by whom
The earth was shaken in six ways
And the mind of Māra was made uneasy:
By him may well-being be wrought for you.

When he turned the wheel of Dharma
And declared the noble truths,
The renown of the Sage that arose:
By it may well-being be wrought for you.

He by whom the non-Buddhists, who captivate the mind,
Were all defeated through the Dharma,
And whose every assembly was brought under his sway:
By him may well-being be wrought for you.

Buddha, may well-being be wrought for you.
May the well-being of the gods, with Indra (Śakra),
And the well-being of all spirits and beings
Be bestowed upon you always.

By the power of the buddhas' merit
And by the thoughts of the gods,
Whatever aim you desire,
May that aim be accomplished today.

Two-legged ones, may you be well.
Four-legged ones, may you be well.
May you be well as you travel the road,
And may your returning also be happy.
May the day be well, and the night be well,
And may the midday also be well.
May you all be well always,
And may no harm befall you.

Surrounded by thousands of buddhas and gods,
He has come, going forth with them.
May those who harbor hatred be driven away,
And may those whose nature is compassion remain.

By the truthful words of the buddhas,
Solitary buddhas, arhats, and trainees,
May those who bring harm to the world
Be sent away from this city.

May all sentient beings and all living creatures,
All spirits, and all of you,
Be endowed with happiness alone.
May all be free of illness.
May all see what is good,
And may none fall into evil.

All spirits who have come here,
Who dwell on the earth and in the sky,
May you always have loving-kindness for living beings,
And practice the Dharma by day and by night.

The noble one of Vaiśālī...
