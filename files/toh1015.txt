The Noble Dhāraṇī That Thoroughly Pacifies Illness

The Noble Dhāraṇī That Thoroughly Pacifies Illness, known in Tibetan as Phakpa Né Rabtu Zhiwar Jepé Zung and probably corresponding to a Sanskrit title along the lines of Ārya Roga Praśamanī Dhāraṇī, is a very brief incantation text devoted to healing. After paying homage to the Three Jewels and to the Buddha under the name Vajra Guru King, it gives a short mantra that invokes the vajra and calls for the destruction of all diseases. Its purpose is to be recited to bring relief from illness. It is Toh 1015 in the Kangyur, where it belongs among the dhāraṇī texts, the collection of short protective and beneficial spells.

Homage to the Three Jewels.

Homage to the Blessed One, the Tathāgata, the arhat, the perfectly and completely awakened buddha, the Vajra Guru King.

tadyathā vajra vajra mahāvajra sarba bhyadhi hana hana vajrena svāhā

The noble dhāraṇī that thoroughly pacifies illness is complete.

This concludes the translation. The following are translator's notes.

The buddha's name, rendered here as "Vajra Guru King," is a word-for-word rendering. The Tibetan word for "guru" can also stand for Sanskrit uttara, "supreme," so the original Sanskrit form cannot be confirmed.
