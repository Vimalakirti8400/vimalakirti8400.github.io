The Dhāraṇī Thoroughly Pacifying All Misdeeds (Sarvapāpapraśamanadhāraṇī)

Thoroughly Pacifying All Misdeeds is a very brief dhāraṇī text whose Sanskrit and Tibetan titles are not given in the opening lines. After homage to the Three Jewels and to the Buddha as Tathāgata, arhat, and perfectly complete Buddha, it consists almost entirely of a single incantation. The incantation calls upon Mañjuśrī in his youthful form, urging him to come quickly, to free the reciter from all suffering, and to destroy their negative deeds. As its title suggests, the text is meant to purify and pacify harmful actions through recitation. It is Toh 1023 in the Kangyur, where it belongs among the collected dhāraṇīs, the short ritual formulas preserved in the tantric portion of the canon.
